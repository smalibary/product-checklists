SECRETS CHECKLIST: 10 items
Keeping passwords, keys and tokens out of code and out of the wrong hands.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STORAGE
 1. Secrets in a secret store or environment settings, never in code or git   (Standard: owasp-secrets, twelve-factor)
 2. Secret scanning on every push; leaks revoked at once   (Standard: github-tokens, owasp-secrets)
 3. Separate secrets for dev, test and production   (Standard: owasp-secrets)

ACCESS
 4. Each service gets only the secrets it needs   (Standard: owasp-secrets)
 5. Access to secrets logged   (Standard: owasp-secrets)

LIFE
 6. Secrets rotated on a schedule and after anyone leaves   (Standard: owasp-secrets)
 7. Rotation without downtime (two valid during the switch)   (Standard: owasp-secrets)
 8. Short-lived credentials where the platform offers them   (Standard: owasp-secrets)

HANDLING
 9. Secrets never printed in logs, errors or build output   (Standard: owasp-secrets, owasp-logging)
10. Shared with people only through the secret store, never by chat or email   (Standard: owasp-secrets)

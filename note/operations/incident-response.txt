INCIDENT RESPONSE CHECKLIST: 12 items
What happens when the service breaks: who leads, how people are told, and how it gets learned from.
Backing: 2 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

READY
 1. Who is on call is written, with a backup   (Standard: sre-incident)
 2. Runbooks for the common failures   (Standard: sre-incident)
 3. Severity levels defined with examples   (Standard: atlassian-incident)

DURING
 4. One incident lead; roles for talking, fixing and writing down   (Standard: sre-incident)
 5. Stop the harm first, find the cause later   (Standard: sre-incident)
 6. A running timeline of what was done   (Standard: sre-incident)
 7. Customers told on the status page early and updated regularly   (Standard: atlassian-incident)  -> see website/status

DATA BREACHES
 8. Personal data breaches reported to the authority within 72 hours where required   (Rule: gdpr-33, pdpl)  -> see backend/data-privacy
 9. Affected people told when the risk is high   (Rule: gdpr-33)

AFTER
10. A blameless postmortem with causes and actions   (Standard: sre-postmortem)
11. Actions have owners and dates, and are followed up   (Standard: sre-postmortem)
12. Lessons shared with the whole team   (Standard: sre-postmortem)

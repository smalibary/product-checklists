BACKUPS CHECKLIST: 11 items
Copies of data you can actually restore when something goes wrong.
Backing: 2 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLAN
 1. How much data you can lose (RPO) and how long you can be down (RTO) agreed   (Standard: nist-contingency)
 2. Point-in-time recovery for the main database   (Standard: pg-backup)
 3. 3 copies, on 2 kinds of storage, 1 in another place   (Practice)

SAFETY
 4. Backups encrypted   (Rule: gdpr-32)
 5. At least one copy cannot be changed or deleted by the app's accounts   (Standard: nist-contingency)
 6. Access to backups limited and logged   (Standard: owasp-logging)

TESTING
 7. Restores tested on a schedule, timed, and written up   (Standard: nist-contingency)
 8. A full disaster-recovery practice at least yearly   (Standard: nist-contingency)

SCOPE
 9. Files, settings and secrets covered, not only the database   (Standard: nist-contingency)

PRIVACY
10. Deleted personal data removed from backups within a stated time   (Rule: gdpr-17)

WATCHING
11. An alert when a backup fails or is late   (Standard: sre-alerting)

MONITORING AND ALERTS CHECKLIST: 11 items
Knowing the service is healthy, and waking someone only when people are affected.
Backing: 0 Rule, 11 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

MEASURE
 1. The four golden signals: traffic, errors, time taken and how full the system is   (Standard: sre-monitoring)
 2. Service level objectives (for example 99.9% of requests succeed) agreed and written   (Standard: sre-slo)
 3. Checks from outside that act like a user (synthetic checks)   (Standard: sre-monitoring)

ALERTS
 4. Alert on user pain (SLO burn rate), not on every high number   (Standard: sre-alerting)
 5. Every alert can be acted on and links to a runbook   (Standard: sre-alerting, sre-incident)
 6. Urgent alerts page someone; the rest go to a ticket   (Standard: sre-monitoring)
 7. Noisy alerts fixed or removed   (Standard: sre-alerting)

DASHBOARDS
 8. One overview page per service   (Standard: sre-monitoring)
 9. Traces across services   (Standard: otel)

USERS
10. A public status page during problems   (Standard: atlassian-incident)

COSTS
11. Usage of paid services watched against limits, with alerts at 50% and 80%   (Standard: aws-cost)

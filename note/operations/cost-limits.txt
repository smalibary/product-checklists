COST AND USAGE LIMITS CHECKLIST: 10 items
Making every paid service last its whole billing period and never surprise you with a bill.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

KNOWING
 1. Each paid service listed with its plan, limits, reset date and which copies share them   (Standard: aws-cost)
 2. A daily budget per service (limit divided by days)   (Standard: aws-cost)

CAPPING
 3. Code that spends per call (AI, SMS, email, maps) counts use and stops at a daily cap   (Standard: owasp-llm)
 4. Retries give up after a few tries   (Standard: aws-backoff)
 5. Spending alerts and hard caps set in each provider's console   (Standard: aws-cost)

CHEAP BY DESIGN
 6. Jobs start when there is work, not on fast timers   (Practice)
 7. Test and dev copies use cheaper or separate accounts   (Standard: aws-cost)
 8. Repeated lookups and AI answers cached   (Standard: aws-cost)
 9. Unused servers, files and copies removed   (Standard: aws-cost)

WATCHING
10. Usage compared with the pace line each week; alerts at 50% and 80% when ahead of pace   (Standard: aws-cost)

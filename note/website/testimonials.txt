TESTIMONIALS PAGE CHECKLIST: 19 items
Real words from real customers that help a visitor believe the product works for people like them.
Backing: 7 Rule, 4 Standard, 6 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

HONESTY (THE LAW)
 1. Every quote is from a real customer and says what they really think   (Rule: ftc-endorsements, ftc-reviews)
 2. No fake, bought or AI-written reviews presented as real   (Rule: ftc-reviews)
 3. Paid, gifted or employee testimonials say so clearly beside the quote   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
 4. Results quoted are typical, or the typical result is shown beside them   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
 5. Bad reviews are not hidden from a page that claims to show all reviews   (Rule: ftc-reviews)
 6. Written permission kept for each name, photo and logo used   (Rule: gdpr-7)

WHAT MAKES A QUOTE BELIEVABLE
 7. Full name, role, company and a real photo   (Research: nng-reviews, nng-trust)
 8. A specific result (time saved, money earned) rather than general praise   (Research: nng-reviews)
 9. Quotes grouped by customer type or use case so visitors find people like them   (Research: nng-reviews)
10. A date on each quote, and old ones removed   (Practice)
11. A link to the full case study or the original review where it lives (G2, App Store)   (Research: nng-reviews)
12. Video testimonials with captions and a transcript   (Standard: wcag-captions)

LAYOUT
13. Short quotes in a grid or list; never an auto-moving carousel   (Research: nng-carousel)
14. Star ratings show the number of reviews behind them   (Research: nng-reviews)
15. Logos of customers used with permission   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
16. Text over photos keeps 4.5 to 1 contrast   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
17. Photos have alt text with the person's name   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
18. A call to action after the quotes (start, book a demo)   (Practice)

SEARCH
19. Review structured data only for real reviews of the item on the page   (Standard: google-review)

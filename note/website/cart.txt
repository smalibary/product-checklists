CART PAGE CHECKLIST: 23 items
Shows what the shopper is about to buy and the full cost, and moves them to checkout.
Backing: 3 Rule, 4 Standard, 10 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ITEMS
 1. Each item: photo, name, chosen options (size, colour), unit price and quantity   (Research: baymard-guest)
 2. Change quantity with a field plus minus and plus buttons   (Research: baymard-guest)
 3. Remove an item, with Undo instead of a confirmation box   (Research: nng-control)
 4. A link back to each product   (Practice)
 5. Stock warnings that are true ("Only 2 left" only if real)   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
 6. Save for later or move to wishlist   (Practice)

COST
 7. Subtotal, shipping, tax and discounts each on their own line   (Research: baymard-guest)
 8. Shipping cost or an estimate shown before checkout; hidden costs are the top reason people leave   (Research: baymard-abandon)
 9. No surprise fees added later; all mandatory fees in the first price shown   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees, eu-consumer-rights)
10. Total in the shopper's currency, stating whether tax is included   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
11. Delivery date estimate, not only a speed   (Research: baymard-guest)
12. Promo code field present but low-key (a link that opens it)   (Research: baymard-coupon)

MOVING ON
13. One clear Checkout button, at the top and bottom on long carts   (Practice)
14. Guest checkout offered; forcing an account makes people leave   (Research: baymard-guest, baymard-abandon)
15. Express pay buttons (Apple Pay, Google Pay) for speed   (Practice)
16. Continue shopping link   (Practice)
17. Trust signals near the button: returns policy, secure payment   (Research: baymard-trust)

STATES
18. An empty cart that suggests where to go next   (Research: nng-empty)
19. Cart kept across visits and devices for signed-in shoppers   (Practice)
20. Changes to totals announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)

ACCESS
21. Quantity buttons have names ("Increase quantity of Blue mug")   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
22. Touch targets at least 24 pixels, 44 recommended   (Standard: wcag-target)
23. Works at phone width with no sideways scroll   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

AFFILIATE PAGE CHECKLIST: 17 items
Invites people to promote the product for a commission and explains how the programme works.
Backing: 4 Rule, 1 Standard, 3 Research, 9 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE OFFER
 1. Commission stated plainly: percent or amount, one-off or recurring, for how long   (Practice)
 2. Cookie window (how long after a click a sale still counts)   (Practice)
 3. When and how payouts happen, the minimum payout and the currencies   (Practice)
 4. A worked example: "Refer 10 customers on the Pro plan, earn X a month"   (Practice)

JOINING
 5. Who can join, and who cannot (for example, no paid search on the brand name)   (Practice)
 6. A short application form; tell people how long approval takes   (Research: baymard-fields)
 7. A sign-in link for existing affiliates   (Practice)
 8. Terms of the programme linked before applying   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)

RULES AFFILIATES MUST FOLLOW
 9. Affiliates must say clearly that they earn a commission   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
10. No false claims, fake reviews or spam to promote the product   (Rule: ftc-reviews, can-spam)
11. Brand guidelines and approved logos to download   (Practice)

TOOLS
12. Links, banners and text ready to copy   (Practice)
13. A dashboard showing clicks, sign-ups, sales and money owed   (Practice)
14. A contact for affiliate questions   (Research: nng-contact)

TRUST
15. Quotes or numbers from current affiliates (real, with permission)   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
16. FAQ on tax forms, payment methods and returns that cancel commission   (Research: nng-faq)

ACCESSIBILITY
17. Form fields with visible labels and clear errors   (Standard: wcag-labels, wcag-error)

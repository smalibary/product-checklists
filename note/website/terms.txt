TERMS OF SERVICE CHECKLIST: 11 items
The legal agreement people accept to use the product.
Backing: 6 Rule, 1 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Plain-language summary at the top   (Research: nng-scanning)
 2. Headings and a table of contents   (Standard: wcag-headings)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 3. Who you are, how to contact you, and which law applies   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 4. Payment, renewal and cancellation terms spelled out   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)  -> see flows/canceling-subscription
 5. How accounts can be closed and data deleted   (Rule: gdpr-17)

ACCEPTANCE
 6. Shown before accepting, with a link at sign-up   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)  -> see website/sign-up
 7. Acceptance recorded with the version and time   (Practice)

CHANGES
 8. A date and version on the page   (Practice)
 9. People told before important changes take effect   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
10. Old versions kept and reachable   (Practice)

FAIRNESS
11. No hidden or surprising terms   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

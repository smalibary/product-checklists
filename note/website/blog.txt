BLOG (LIST PAGE) CHECKLIST: 17 items
The blog's front page that lists articles and helps readers find one to read.
Backing: 1 Rule, 9 Standard, 5 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

LIST
 1. Newest posts first, with a featured post at the top if useful   (Research: nng-blog)
 2. Each card: title, short summary, date, author, reading time and image   (Research: nng-blog)
 3. The whole card opens the post, with one link inside (not many nested links)   (Standard: wcag-link)
 4. Categories or topics to filter   (Research: nng-blog)  -> see flows/filtering-items
 5. Search over posts   (Research: nng-blog)
 6. Pagination with page numbers, or Load more that keeps position on Back   (Research: nng-infinite)  -> see design-system/pagination

SUBSCRIBE
 7. Email sign-up with what and how often   (Rule: gdpr-13, can-spam)  -> see website/newsletter-signup
 8. RSS feed linked   (Practice)  -> see web-app/feed

LAYOUT
 9. Cards in a grid that becomes one column on phones   (Standard: wcag-reflow)  -> see design-system/accessibility
10. Images have space reserved so the page does not jump   (Standard: webdev-cls)
11. Images lazy-loaded below the fold   (Standard: webdev-images)
12. Dates in a clear format with the year   (Practice)

ACCESS AND SEARCH
13. One main heading; card titles as headings   (Standard: wcag-headings)  -> see design-system/accessibility
14. Card images decorative (empty alt) when the title already says it   (Standard: wai-images)
15. Contrast meets WCAG, including over images   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
16. A unique title and description for the list and each category   (Standard: google-title)
17. Breadcrumbs on category pages   (Standard: google-breadcrumb)  -> see design-system/breadcrumb

PRIVACY PAGE CHECKLIST: 23 items
The privacy policy: what personal data is collected, why, who sees it, and what rights people have.
Backing: 17 Rule, 2 Standard, 0 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

REQUIRED CONTENT
 1. Who is responsible for the data: company name, address and contact   (Rule: gdpr-13, pdpl)
 2. Contact for the data protection officer, if one is required   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 3. What data is collected, grouped by type (account, payment, usage, device)   (Rule: gdpr-13, ccpa)
 4. Why each type is collected and the legal basis for it   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 5. Who data is shared with, by category, and whether it is sold   (Rule: gdpr-13, ccpa)
 6. Transfers to other countries and how they are protected   (Rule: gdpr-13, pdpl)
 7. How long each type is kept   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 8. People's rights: see, correct, delete, take away, object, withdraw consent   (Rule: gdpr-15, gdpr-17, gdpr-20, gdpr-21)
 9. How to use those rights, with a direct link or form   (Rule: gdpr-12, ccpa)
10. The right to complain to the data authority   (Rule: gdpr-13)
11. Whether any decisions are made automatically about people   (Rule: gdpr-13)
12. Cookies and trackers explained, with a link to change choices   (Rule: eprivacy)
13. Children: minimum age and how children's data is handled   (Rule: coppa)
14. A "Do not sell or share my personal information" link where California law applies   (Rule: ccpa)

CLARITY
15. A short summary at the top in plain words, the full text below   (Rule: gdpr-12)  -> see design-system/content-voice
16. Written in clear, plain language; no hidden meaning   (Rule: gdpr-12)  -> see design-system/content-voice
17. Headings and a contents list to jump to a part   (Standard: wcag-headings)  -> see design-system/accessibility
18. Available in the languages the service is offered in   (Rule: pdpl)
19. Effective date and a list of changes   (Practice)
20. People told by email when the policy changes in an important way   (Practice)

ACCESS
21. Linked from the footer of every page and from sign-up   (Practice)  -> see website/sign-up
22. Readable as plain HTML (not only a PDF)   (Standard: wcag)
23. A printable or downloadable version   (Practice)

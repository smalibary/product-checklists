PRICING PAGE (WEBSITE) CHECKLIST: 23 items
Shows the plans and their prices so a visitor can pick one and start.
Backing: 8 Rule, 4 Standard, 8 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLANS
 1. Two to four plans side by side, one marked as most popular   (Research: nng-pricing)
 2. Each plan: name, who it is for, price, billing period and the main limits   (Research: nng-pricing)
 3. Price in the visitor's currency, with tax shown or stated   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)
 4. Monthly and yearly switch, with the yearly saving as an amount, not only a percent   (Practice)
 5. Yearly price also shown per month only if the full yearly charge is stated beside it   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 6. All mandatory fees inside the price shown   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees)
 7. A free trial or free plan stated with what happens at the end   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 8. "Contact sales" only for the biggest plan; prices for the rest   (Research: nng-pricing)

COMPARING
 9. A full feature table below the plan cards   (Research: nng-comparison)
10. Feature names in plain words with a short explanation on demand   (Research: nng-pricing)
11. Ticks also have words for screen readers   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
12. The table header stays visible while scrolling   (Research: nng-comparison)

DOUBTS
13. FAQ: changing plans, cancelling, refunds, payment methods, discounts   (Research: nng-faq)
14. How to cancel, and that it is as easy as signing up   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
15. Money-back promise, if any, with its terms   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
16. Customer logos or quotes near the plans   (Research: nng-trust)

ACTION
17. One button per plan with a clear verb (Start free trial, Buy Pro)   (Practice)
18. The chosen plan carries into sign-up and checkout   (Practice)

HONESTY
19. No pre-ticked add-ons, no fake discounts with made-up "was" prices   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns, eu-omnibus)
20. A struck-out old price only if it was the real price in the last 30 days (EU)   (Rule: eu-omnibus)

ACCESS
21. Plan cards stack on phones, the recommended one first   (Standard: wcag-reflow)
22. The monthly and yearly switch is a real control with a name and state   (Standard: apg-switch, wcag-name-role)
23. Contrast meets WCAG, including the highlighted plan   (Standard: wcag-contrast)

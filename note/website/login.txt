LOGIN PAGE (WEBSITE) CHECKLIST: 29 items
Lets returning people sign in quickly and safely, and recover when they cannot.
Backing: 0 Rule, 20 Standard, 2 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE FORM
 1. Email (or username) and password on one page, unless the site uses passkeys or single sign-on first   (Standard: webdev-signin)
 2. Real form with a form tag and a submit button, so Enter works and password managers save it   (Standard: webdev-signin)
 3. Visible labels, not placeholder-only   (Standard: wcag-labels, nng-placeholder)
 4. autocomplete set to username and current-password   (Standard: webdev-signin, mdn-autocomplete, wcag-purpose)
 5. Email field uses type email; no automatic capitals or spelling fixes   (Standard: webdev-signin)
 6. Pasting into the password field is allowed   (Standard: nist-63b, wcag-auth)
 7. Password managers and autofill are never blocked   (Standard: nist-63b, wcag-auth)
 8. Show password button with a clear name and state   (Research: baymard-show, govuk-show)
 9. Remember me (stay signed in) choice, with its length stated   (Practice)

WAYS IN
10. Passkeys offered, with the browser's passkey autofill on the email field   (Standard: webauthn, fido-ux)
11. Sign in with Google, Apple or work single sign-on where the audience uses them   (Practice)
12. Email magic link or one-time code as a choice for people without a password   (Practice)
13. The same ways shown on sign-up and sign-in, so people remember which they used   (Practice)

ERRORS AND SAFETY
14. One error for a wrong email or password, never which one was wrong   (Standard: owasp-auth)
15. Errors shown next to the field and focused, in words   (Standard: wcag-error, govuk-error-message)
16. Caps Lock warning on the password field   (Practice)
17. Limits on repeated wrong tries (slow down, then lock) with a message saying what to do   (Standard: owasp-auth, nist-63b)
18. A robot check only after suspicious tries, and an accessible one   (Standard: wcag-auth)
19. Two-step check after the password when the account has it   (Standard: owasp-mfa)
20. Signs in over HTTPS only; session cookie secure and httpOnly   (Standard: owasp-session)
21. New sign-ins from a new device announced by email   (Standard: owasp-auth)

RECOVERY
22. Forgot password link right beside the password field   (Standard: webdev-signin)
23. A link to sign up for people without an account   (Practice)
24. Help or contact link for locked-out people   (Standard: wcag-help)

AFTER SIGN-IN
25. Back to the page they were on before signing in   (Practice)
26. No redirect to an outside address taken from the link   (Standard: owasp-redirect)

ACCESS AND LAYOUT
27. Focus starts on the first field, not stolen later   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
28. Page title says "Sign in" with the site name   (Standard: wcag-title)
29. Use the same words everywhere: either "Sign in" or "Log in", not both   (Research: nng-heuristics)

COOKIE CONSENT CHECKLIST: 12 items
Asking before using cookies and trackers that are not strictly needed.
Backing: 8 Rule, 4 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN
 1. Consent before any non-essential cookie or tracker is set   (Rule: eprivacy, gdpr-7)
 2. Strictly necessary cookies need no consent   (Rule: eprivacy)

THE BANNER
 3. Accept and Reject equally easy, on the first layer   (Rule: edpb-cookies)
 4. No ticked boxes in advance   (Rule: gdpr-7, edpb-cookies)
 5. Plain words on what each kind does, with a link to details   (Rule: gdpr-12)
 6. Does not block the page more than needed   (Standard: govuk-cookie)

CHOICES
 7. Choice by purpose (analytics, marketing)   (Rule: edpb-cookies)
 8. Changing your mind is as easy as giving consent, from every page   (Rule: gdpr-7)
 9. The choice remembered and not asked again on every visit   (Standard: govuk-cookie)

RECORDS
10. Consent recorded with time and version   (Rule: gdpr-7)

ACCESS
11. The banner reachable by keyboard and read by screen readers   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)
12. Focus not trapped and content not hidden behind it   (Standard: wcag-obscured)

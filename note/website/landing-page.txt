LANDING PAGE CHECKLIST: 23 items
The page people arrive on from an ad, a link or a search; it has one job: get them to take one action.
Backing: 4 Rule, 7 Standard, 7 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ABOVE THE FOLD
 1. A headline that says what the visitor gets, in their words   (Research: nng-scanning)
 2. A sub-line that says how, or for whom   (Research: nng-scanning)
 3. One main action button with a verb (Start free, Get the guide)   (Practice)
 4. A product image or short clip that shows the real thing   (Research: nng-trust)
 5. The page matches the ad or link that brought the visitor (same promise, same words)   (Practice)

BODY
 6. Three to five benefits, each a short heading, a line and a picture   (Research: nng-scanning)
 7. Proof: customer logos, numbers or quotes, with permission   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
 8. How it works in three steps   (Practice)
 9. Answers to the main doubts (price, time, risk) in a short FAQ   (Research: nng-faq)  -> see website/faq
10. The main action repeated after each big section   (Practice)
11. Few or no links that lead away from the action   (Practice)

FORMS
12. Ask only what is needed; every extra field costs sign-ups   (Research: baymard-fields)  -> see website/sign-up
13. Labels above fields, the right keyboard and autofill   (Standard: wcag-labels, mdn-autocomplete, webdev-signup)  -> see design-system/form-layout, design-system/accessibility
14. Errors in words next to the field   (Standard: wcag-error)
15. Marketing consent unticked by default where the law needs consent   (Rule: gdpr-7)

SPEED
16. Largest image or text shows in under 2.5 seconds   (Standard: webdev-lcp)
17. The page does not jump while loading   (Standard: webdev-cls)
18. Responds to taps in under 200 milliseconds   (Standard: webdev-inp)
19. Faster pages convert better (Vodafone, Rakuten and others measured it)   (Research: webdev-vitals-business)

HONESTY
20. No fake countdowns, fake stock counts or hidden prices   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns, ftc-junk-fees)
21. Prices and terms in the page, not only in small print   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees)

ACCESS AND PHONES
22. Video has captions, starts muted and can be paused   (Standard: wcag-captions, wcag-audio, wcag-pause)
23. A page title and description written for search and sharing   (Standard: google-title, google-snippet)

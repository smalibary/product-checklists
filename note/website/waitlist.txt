WAITLIST PAGE CHECKLIST: 16 items
Lets people join a queue for access to a product that is not open to everyone yet.
Backing: 5 Rule, 5 Standard, 2 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE OFFER
 1. What they are joining and what they get when let in   (Practice)
 2. How the waitlist works: first come, by invitation, by referral   (Practice)
 3. A rough wait time, if the team can keep it   (Practice)
 4. Real numbers only; never invent a queue length   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

THE FORM
 5. Email only, or email plus one question that helps decide who goes first   (Research: baymard-fields)
 6. Visible labels, the right keyboard and autofill   (Standard: wcag-labels, mdn-autocomplete)
 7. Errors in words next to the field   (Standard: wcag-error)
 8. What emails will be sent and a link to the privacy policy   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 9. Consent for marketing separate from joining the list, unticked   (Rule: gdpr-7)

AFTER JOINING
10. A confirmation screen with the place in line, if shown   (Research: govuk-confirm)
11. A confirmation email, with a way to leave the list   (Rule: can-spam)
12. An optional "move up by inviting friends" with the referral rules written out   (Practice)
13. Rewards for referrals that are honest and delivered   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

WHEN ACCESS OPENS
14. An email invite with a clear first step and a link that works once   (Standard: owasp-auth)
15. Invite links expire after a stated time   (Standard: owasp-auth)

ACCESS
16. Updates to a place-in-line counter announced without moving focus   (Standard: wcag-status)

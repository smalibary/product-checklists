FAQ PAGE CHECKLIST: 18 items
Answers the questions people really ask, so they do not have to contact support.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 10 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Questions taken from real support tickets and searches, not marketing points   (Research: nng-faq)  -> see support/help-desk
 2. Written in the customer's words   (Research: nng-faq)
 3. Short, direct answers; the first sentence answers the question   (Research: nng-faq)
 4. Links to longer help articles where needed   (Research: nng-faq)
 5. Grouped by topic with headings   (Research: nng-faq)
 6. Most asked questions first   (Research: nng-faq)
 7. Reviewed and dated; out-of-date answers removed   (Practice)

FINDING AN ANSWER
 8. A search box over the questions on long pages   (Research: nng-faq)  -> see website/search
 9. A list of all questions at the top that jumps to each answer   (Research: nng-faq)
10. Each question has its own link that can be shared   (Practice)

LAYOUT
11. Short lists may show all answers; long lists can fold into an accordion   (Research: nng-accordion)  -> see design-system/accordion
12. Accordions are buttons with expanded and collapsed state   (Standard: apg-accordion)  -> see design-system/accordion
13. Find-in-page still works with folded answers (hidden="until-found" or all open)   (Practice)
14. Headings in order   (Standard: wcag-headings)  -> see design-system/accessibility

NEXT STEP
15. "Still need help?" with a contact link at the end   (Research: nng-help)
16. "Was this helpful?" on each answer to find weak ones   (Practice)

SEARCH
17. FAQ structured data where it still applies (Google now shows it mainly for government and health sites)   (Standard: google-faq)  -> see growth/technical-seo
18. Contrast and phone width meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast, wcag-reflow)  -> see design-system/accessibility

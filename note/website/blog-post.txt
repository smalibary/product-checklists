BLOG POST CHECKLIST: 25 items
A single article page, built for reading, sharing and finding the next article.
Backing: 3 Rule, 11 Standard, 6 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

TOP OF THE ARTICLE
 1. Title as the only top-level heading   (Standard: wcag-headings)
 2. Author name with photo and a link to their page   (Research: nng-blog)
 3. Date published and date updated   (Research: nng-blog, google-article)
 4. Reading time   (Practice)
 5. A summary or key points for scanners   (Research: nng-scanning)

READING
 6. Body text at least 16 pixels, line height about 1.5   (Standard: wcag-spacing)
 7. Lines of about 50 to 75 characters   (Practice: butterick-line)
 8. Headings every few paragraphs, in order   (Standard: wcag-headings)
 9. Short paragraphs, lists and bold key words   (Research: nng-scanning)
10. A contents list for long posts, with jump links   (Practice)
11. Links say where they go   (Standard: wcag-link)
12. Images with alt text and captions; charts also described in words   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
13. Code blocks scroll on their own on phones, never the whole page   (Standard: wcag-reflow)
14. Text resizes to 200% without breaking   (Standard: wcag-resize)
15. Dark mode respected if the site has one   (Practice: mdn-color-scheme)

AROUND THE ARTICLE
16. Share buttons that are links, not heavy trackers   (Practice)
17. Related posts at the end   (Research: nng-blog)
18. A sign-up for new posts, with consent rules   (Rule: gdpr-7)
19. Category and tags linking to more   (Research: nng-blog)
20. Comments, if any, moderated   (Rule: eu-dsa)

SEARCH AND SPEED
21. Article structured data: headline, author, dates, image   (Standard: google-article)
22. A unique title and description   (Standard: google-title, google-snippet)
23. Breadcrumbs for deep sites   (Standard: google-breadcrumb)
24. Images sized and lazy-loaded; the page does not jump   (Standard: webdev-images, webdev-cls)
25. Sponsored or affiliate links disclosed   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)

STATUS PAGE CHECKLIST: 20 items
Shows whether the service is working right now, and tells people about problems and planned maintenance.
Backing: 1 Rule, 8 Standard, 2 Research, 9 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

NOW
 1. One line at the top: "All systems working" or what is broken   (Research: nng-status)
 2. Each part of the service listed (website, app, API, payments) with its state   (Practice)
 3. States in words and colour together: Working, Slow, Partly down, Down, Maintenance   (Standard: wcag-color)
 4. Time of the last check, with the time zone   (Research: nng-status)

INCIDENTS
 5. An incident post within minutes of a problem, even before the cause is known   (Standard: atlassian-incident)  -> see operations/incident-response
 6. Updates on a steady rhythm (for example every 30 minutes) until fixed   (Standard: atlassian-incident)
 7. Each update says what is affected, what users can do, and when the next update comes   (Standard: atlassian-incident)
 8. A short review after big incidents: cause, fix, how it will not happen again   (Standard: atlassian-incident)  -> see operations/incident-response
 9. Plain words; no blame and no hiding   (Practice)  -> see design-system/content-voice

HISTORY AND PLANS
10. Uptime for the last 90 days per part, with the number   (Practice)
11. Past incidents listed by date   (Practice)  -> see operations/incident-response
12. Planned maintenance announced ahead with start, end and what is affected   (Practice)

GETTING TOLD
13. Subscribe by email, text, RSS or a chat app   (Practice)
14. Unsubscribe in one step   (Rule: can-spam, rfc-unsubscribe)  -> see flows/unsubscribing-from-emails
15. A machine-readable feed (API or RSS) for customers' own tools   (Practice)

RELIABILITY OF THE PAGE ITSELF
16. Hosted apart from the main service, so it stays up when the service is down   (Practice)
17. Fast and light; works on a slow phone connection   (Standard: webdev-vitals)
18. Status changes announced to screen readers without moving focus   (Standard: wcag-status)
19. Contrast and text size meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
20. Linked from the footer, the help centre and error pages   (Practice)  -> see website/footer, web-app/help-center, web-app/error-pages

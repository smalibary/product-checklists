BILLING PAGE (WEBSITE) CHECKLIST: 18 items
The public page that explains how billing works: payment methods, invoices, taxes, refunds and cancelling.
Backing: 8 Rule, 2 Standard, 2 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

HOW YOU PAY
 1. Payment methods accepted, with logos and words (cards, Apple Pay, mada, bank transfer, invoice)   (Practice)
 2. Currencies offered and which one the customer will be charged in   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 3. When the charge happens: at sign-up, at trial end, monthly or yearly on the same date   (Rule: ftc-negative-option, eu-consumer-rights)
 4. Taxes (VAT) explained: whether prices include tax and how tax IDs are added   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)

RENEWALS AND CHANGES
 5. Auto-renewal stated plainly, with the price after any discount ends   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 6. What happens on upgrade (charged the difference now) and downgrade (from next period)   (Practice)
 7. Reminder email before a yearly renewal or trial end   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 8. How to cancel, in as few steps as it took to sign up   (Rule: ftc-negative-option, eu-consumer-rights)

REFUNDS
 9. Refund policy in plain words, including the EU 14-day right to withdraw where it applies   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
10. How long a refund takes to arrive   (Practice)

INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
11. Where to find invoices, and that they can be downloaded as PDF   (Practice)
12. How to add a company name, address and tax ID to invoices   (Practice)
13. Invoices that meet the local e-invoice rules (for Saudi Arabia, ZATCA)   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)

PROBLEMS
14. What happens if a payment fails: retries, grace period, when access stops   (Practice)
15. Card security: card details handled by a PCI-certified payment provider   (Standard: pci-dss)
16. A contact for billing questions   (Research: nng-contact)
17. FAQ for common questions   (Research: nng-faq)

ACCESS
18. Headings, contrast and plain words meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-headings, wcag-contrast)

PRESS / MEDIA PAGE CHECKLIST: 15 items
Gives journalists what they need to write about the company quickly: news, facts, images and a contact.
Backing: 0 Rule, 2 Standard, 10 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTACT FIRST
 1. A named press contact with email and phone, near the top   (Research: nng-press)
 2. Expected reply time   (Research: nng-press)

FACTS
 3. Company fact sheet: founded, founders, headquarters, size, funding, what it does   (Research: nng-press)
 4. Short and long company descriptions ready to copy   (Research: nng-press)
 5. Leadership names, roles, bios and photos   (Research: nng-press)

NEWS
 6. Press releases listed newest first, each with a date and as HTML (not only PDF)   (Research: nng-press)
 7. Coverage: links to articles about the company   (Practice)

MEDIA KIT
 8. Logos in several formats (SVG, PNG) on light and dark backgrounds   (Research: nng-press)
 9. Product screenshots and photos at high resolution   (Research: nng-press)
10. One download for the whole kit, with its file size   (Research: nng-press)
11. Rules for using the logo and the brand name   (Practice)
12. Image credits and permission to use stated   (Practice)

ACCESS
13. Photos and logos have alt text   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
14. Downloads labelled with type and size   (Standard: wcag-link)
15. Reachable from the footer and About page   (Research: nng-footers)

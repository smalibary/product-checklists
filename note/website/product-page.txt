PRODUCT PAGE CHECKLIST: 13 items
The page for one product in a shop: pictures, price, options and the button to buy.
Backing: 2 Rule, 3 Standard, 8 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PICTURES
 1. Several large images, including scale and in-use shots   (Research: baymard-product)
 2. Zoom on tap or hover   (Research: baymard-product)
 3. Images described for screen readers   (Standard: wcag-nontext)  -> see design-system/accessibility

FACTS
 4. Price with VAT and any fees shown before checkout   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees, eu-consumer-rights)  -> see web-app/checkout
 5. Options (size, colour) as visible choices, with unavailable ones marked   (Research: baymard-product)
 6. Stock and delivery date near the button   (Research: baymard-product)
 7. Specifications in a scannable list   (Research: nng-product-page)
 8. Returns and warranty summary near the price   (Research: baymard-product)

BUYING
 9. One clear Add to cart button that stays in reach on phones   (Research: baymard-product)  -> see website/cart
10. Confirmation after adding, without taking the person away   (Research: baymard-cart)

REVIEWS
11. Real reviews, with negative ones shown too   (Rule: ftc-reviews)

SEARCH ENGINES
12. Product structured data (price, availability, rating)   (Standard: google-product)  -> see growth/technical-seo

ACCESS
13. Option choices work by keyboard and say which is selected   (Standard: wcag-keyboard, wcag-name-role)  -> see design-system/accessibility

404 PAGE CHECKLIST: 12 items
The page shown when an address does not exist; its job is to get the visitor back on track.
Backing: 0 Rule, 3 Standard, 8 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

MESSAGE
 1. Plain words: "We cannot find that page", without the word "404" as the headline   (Research: nng-404, govuk-404)
 2. A short reason: the address may be mistyped, or the page moved   (Research: nng-404, govuk-404)
 3. No blame on the visitor   (Research: nng-errors)
 4. Calm humour only if it does not hide the message   (Research: nng-404)

WAYS FORWARD
 5. A search box   (Research: nng-404)
 6. Links to the home page and the most used pages   (Research: nng-404)
 7. A contact link to report a broken link   (Research: nng-404, govuk-404)
 8. The site's normal header and footer so navigation still works   (Research: nng-404)

TECHNICAL
 9. Returns the status 404 (or 410 for gone forever), not 200   (Standard: google-404)
10. Moved pages redirect (301) to their new home instead of showing a 404   (Standard: google-404)
11. Broken links found in logs and fixed   (Practice)
12. The page title says "Page not found" with the site name   (Standard: wcag-title)

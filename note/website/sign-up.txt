SIGN UP PAGE (WEBSITE) CHECKLIST: 29 items
Creating an account on a website. The phone version (mobile/sign-up) has the full 65 items; this one adds what differs on a website.
Backing: 4 Rule, 11 Standard, 9 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE THE FORM
 1. What the person gets, in one line above the form   (Practice)
 2. Let people try or buy without an account where possible   (Research: baymard-guest, nng-login-walls)
 3. Price and trial terms visible before the form if sign-up starts a trial   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)

WAYS TO SIGN UP
 4. Sign up with Google, Apple, Microsoft or work single sign-on, matched to the audience   (Practice)
 5. Passkey offered at or right after sign-up, with a fallback   (Standard: webauthn, fido-ux)
 6. Email and password as the always-there choice   (Practice)

FIELDS
 7. Ask only what is needed now; everything else later   (Research: baymard-fields, webdev-signup)
 8. One name field ("Full name"), not first and last, for international names   (Standard: w3c-names)
 9. Visible labels; placeholders only as examples   (Standard: wcag-labels, nng-placeholder)  -> see design-system/accessibility
10. autocomplete set: name, email, new-password   (Standard: webdev-signup, mdn-autocomplete, wcag-purpose)
11. Optional fields marked "(optional)"; required ones not starred   (Research: nng-required, govuk-question)
12. Email typo hint ("Did you mean gmail.com?")   (Practice)

PASSWORD
13. At least 8 characters (15 if it is the only factor), up to at least 64, any characters   (Standard: nist-63b)
14. No rules like "one capital, one symbol"; check against known leaked passwords instead   (Standard: nist-63b)
15. Requirements shown before typing, ticked as met   (Research: baymard-inline)
16. Show password button   (Research: baymard-show)
17. Paste and password managers allowed   (Standard: nist-63b, wcag-auth)

ERRORS
18. Checked when the person leaves a field, not while typing   (Research: baymard-inline)
19. Errors next to the field and in a summary at the top after Submit   (Standard: govuk-error-summary, wcag-error)
20. What was typed is kept after an error   (Standard: wcag-redundant)
21. Submit is never disabled; it explains what is missing instead   (Research: govuk-button)

LEGAL AND CONSENT
22. Terms and privacy links beside the button   (Rule: gdpr-13)  -> see website/terms
23. Marketing consent separate and unticked   (Rule: gdpr-7)
24. Age check where children's law applies   (Rule: coppa)

SENDING AND AFTER
25. The button says what it does ("Create account") and shows progress   (Research: nng-status)
26. Email verification by code or link, with resend and change-email   (Practice)
27. Account-exists case handled without revealing which emails have accounts   (Standard: owasp-auth)
28. A welcome screen that leads to the first useful step   (Research: nng-onboarding)

ACCESS
29. Robot checks accessible, or replaced by invisible checks   (Standard: wcag-auth)

FOOTER CHECKLIST: 20 items
The bottom of every page: a safety net of links, contact and legal information.
Backing: 5 Rule, 9 Standard, 2 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Links grouped under short headings (Product, Company, Resources, Legal)   (Research: nng-footers)
 2. Contact: email, phone or a link to the contact page   (Research: nng-footers, nng-contact)
 3. Company name, registration and address where the law asks   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 4. Privacy policy, terms and cookie settings links   (Rule: gdpr-13, eprivacy)
 5. "Do not sell or share my personal information" where California law applies   (Rule: ccpa)
 6. Accessibility statement link   (Rule: eu-accessibility-act)
 7. Copyright line with the current year   (Practice)
 8. Social links with names, not only icons   (Standard: wcag-link, wcag-nontext)
 9. Language and region switcher   (Practice)
10. Newsletter sign-up, short, with consent rules   (Rule: gdpr-7)
11. Status page and help centre links   (Practice)

CONSISTENCY
12. The same footer, in the same order, on every page   (Standard: wcag-nav)
13. Help link in the same place on every page   (Standard: wcag-help)

STRUCTURE AND ACCESS
14. Marked as the page's footer landmark (contentinfo)   (Standard: apg-landmarks)
15. Link groups are lists with headings   (Standard: wcag-info)
16. Link text says where it goes, no "click here"   (Standard: wcag-link)
17. Contrast meets 4.5 to 1, even on dark footers   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
18. Touch targets at least 24 pixels apart or in size   (Standard: wcag-target)
19. Groups stack or fold into sections on phones   (Standard: wcag-reflow)
20. Back to top link on long pages   (Practice)

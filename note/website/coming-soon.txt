COMING SOON PAGE CHECKLIST: 18 items
A holding page before a launch that says what is coming and collects interest.
Backing: 5 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. What is coming and who it is for, in one line   (Practice)
 2. A rough launch date, or "early 2027", only if the team will keep it   (Practice)
 3. One clear action: leave an email to be told at launch   (Practice)
 4. What the person will get by signing up (early access, a discount)   (Practice)
 5. Company name and a way to contact   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)

THE EMAIL FORM
 6. One field: email, with a visible label and the right input type   (Standard: wcag-labels, mdn-autocomplete)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 7. Say exactly what emails will be sent and how often   (Rule: gdpr-13, can-spam)
 8. Consent box unticked by default where the law needs consent for marketing   (Rule: gdpr-7)
 9. A link to the privacy policy beside the form   (Rule: gdpr-13)  -> see website/privacy
10. Confirmation on screen and by email; double opt-in where required   (Practice)
11. Errors shown in words next to the field   (Standard: wcag-error)

COUNTDOWN, IF USED
12. A countdown that is real and never resets   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
13. Countdown numbers not read aloud every second by screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)

SEARCH AND STATUS
14. Returns a normal page (status 200), with a real title and description   (Standard: google-title, google-snippet)
15. If the main site is down for a short while, use a maintenance page instead (status 503)   (Standard: google-maintenance)  -> see web-app/maintenance

LOOK AND ACCESS
16. Contrast and text size meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
17. Any background video or animation can be paused and respects reduced motion   (Standard: wcag-pause, mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility, motion/motion-system
18. Works at phone width with no sideways scroll   (Standard: wcag-reflow)  -> see design-system/accessibility

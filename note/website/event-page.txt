EVENT PAGE CHECKLIST: 28 items
A page for one event (talk, conference, webinar, launch) that tells people what, when and where, and gets them registered.
Backing: 4 Rule, 7 Standard, 4 Research, 13 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE ESSENTIALS ABOVE THE FOLD
 1. Event name and one line on why to attend   (Practice)
 2. Date and start and end time with the time zone written out   (Standard: iso-8601)
 3. Times shown in the visitor's own time zone for online events, with the original beside it   (Standard: w3c-forms-i18n)
 4. Place: venue name and full address for in person, "Online" with the platform for virtual   (Practice)
 5. One clear Register or Get tickets button, repeated at the end   (Research: nng-forms)
 6. Price, or "Free", visible before the button is pressed   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees, eu-consumer-rights)
 7. Seats left or "Sold out" shown honestly; never fake scarcity   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

DETAILS
 8. Agenda with times for each part   (Practice)
 9. Speakers with photo, name, role and a short bio   (Practice)
10. Who it is for and what they will take away   (Practice)
11. Language of the event   (Practice)
12. Accessibility at the venue: step-free entry, captions, sign language, quiet room   (Rule: ada-web, eu-accessibility-act)
13. Directions, map, parking and public transport   (Practice)
14. What to bring and dress code if any   (Practice)
15. Refund and transfer policy for tickets   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)

REGISTRATION
16. A short form: only the fields needed to attend   (Research: baymard-fields)
17. Confirmation screen and email with the date, place and a calendar file   (Research: govuk-confirm)
18. Add to calendar for Google, Apple and Outlook   (Practice)
19. A way to cancel a registration   (Practice)
20. Reminder email before the event, with the join link for online events   (Practice)

AFTER THE EVENT
21. The page changes once the event has passed: "This event has ended" with the recording or slides   (Practice)
22. No Register button on a past event   (Research: nng-heuristics)

SEARCH AND SHARING
23. Event structured data (name, start date, location, offers, status) so search shows it   (Standard: google-event, schema-event)
24. Event status marked if cancelled, postponed or moved online   (Standard: google-event)
25. A share image and title for social links   (Practice)

ACCESSIBILITY
26. Captions and transcripts for any video   (Standard: wcag-captions)
27. Colour contrast and readable text sizes   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
28. Page title includes the event name and date   (Standard: wcag-title)

ABOUT PAGE CHECKLIST: 20 items
Tells visitors who the company is, why it exists and why they can trust it.
Backing: 2 Rule, 5 Standard, 9 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHO AND WHY
 1. One sentence on what the company does and for whom, at the top   (Research: nng-about)
 2. The story: when and why it started, in a few short paragraphs   (Research: nng-about)
 3. Mission or purpose in plain words, not slogans   (Research: nng-about)
 4. What makes the company different, with facts   (Research: nng-trust)

PEOPLE
 5. Founders and leaders with real photos, names and roles   (Research: nng-about, nng-trust)
 6. Team size or a link to the Team page   (Practice)
 7. Photos of real people and places, not stock images   (Research: nng-trust)

PROOF
 8. Numbers that matter: customers, years, countries, with the date they were true   (Research: nng-trust)
 9. Customer logos or quotes used with permission   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
10. Awards, press mentions and partners, linked to the source   (Practice)
11. Investors or ownership, if relevant to trust   (Practice)

CONTACT AND LEGAL
12. Physical address and registered company name   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
13. A way to get in touch from the page   (Research: nng-contact)
14. Links to Careers, Press and Contact   (Practice)

WRITING AND LAYOUT
15. Short paragraphs, headings and lists for scanning   (Research: nng-scanning)
16. Headings that say what each part is about   (Standard: wcag-headings)
17. Images have alt text that describes them; decorative ones are hidden from screen readers   (Standard: wcag-nontext, wai-images)
18. Text contrast meets 4.5 to 1   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
19. Organization structured data (name, logo, address, links)   (Standard: schema-org)
20. Page title that names the company   (Standard: wcag-title)

CAREERS PAGE CHECKLIST: 18 items
Shows open jobs and what it is like to work at the company, and takes applications.
Backing: 3 Rule, 3 Standard, 11 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

JOBS LIST
 1. Open jobs listed with title, team, place (or remote) and type (full time, contract)   (Research: nng-careers)
 2. Filters by team, place and type when there are many   (Research: nng-careers)  -> see flows/filtering-items
 3. Each job has its own page and address that can be shared   (Research: nng-careers)
 4. Closed jobs removed promptly   (Research: nng-careers)

JOB PAGE
 5. What the job is, what the person will do in the first months, and must-have versus nice-to-have skills   (Research: nng-careers)
 6. Pay range where the law asks for it (many US states, EU from 2026)   (Rule: eu-pay-transparency)
 7. Benefits, working hours and leave   (Research: nng-careers)
 8. Hiring steps and how long they take   (Research: nng-careers)
 9. Job structured data so the post appears in job search   (Standard: google-jobs)  -> see growth/technical-seo

APPLYING
10. A short form; upload a CV instead of retyping it   (Research: baymard-fields)  -> see design-system/file-upload
11. No account needed to apply   (Research: nng-login-walls)
12. File upload accepts common types with size limits stated   (Standard: govuk-file-upload, owasp-upload)  -> see design-system/file-upload
13. Labels, errors and autofill work   (Standard: wcag-labels, wcag-error, mdn-autocomplete)  -> see design-system/accessibility
14. What happens to the applicant's data and for how long it is kept   (Rule: gdpr-13)
15. Confirmation on screen and by email   (Research: govuk-confirm)

CULTURE
16. Real photos and words from staff, with permission   (Research: nng-trust)
17. Values, diversity and accessibility commitments, with facts   (Practice)
18. Adjustments offered for applicants with disabilities   (Rule: ada-web)

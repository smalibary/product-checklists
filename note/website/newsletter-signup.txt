NEWSLETTER SIGN-UP CHECKLIST: 11 items
The small form where people give an email address to get news.
Backing: 5 Rule, 5 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FORM
 1. One email field with a clear label and type email   (Standard: wcag-labels, mdn-autocomplete)
 2. Says what they will get and how often   (Research: nng-forms)
 3. Consent wording clear, not hidden in terms   (Rule: gdpr-7, eprivacy)
 4. Separate from other consent (no bundled boxes)   (Rule: gdpr-7)

AFTER
 5. Double opt-in by confirmation email where the law or deliverability needs it   (Standard: google-sender)
 6. A success message in the page   (Standard: wcag-status)

EMAILS
 7. An unsubscribe link in every email, working with one click   (Rule: can-spam, rfc-unsubscribe)
 8. The sender's name and postal address   (Rule: can-spam)

ABUSE
 9. Protection against bots without hard puzzles   (Standard: wcag-auth)
10. Rate-limited by address   (Standard: owasp-dos)

PRIVACY
11. Link to the privacy notice   (Rule: gdpr-13)

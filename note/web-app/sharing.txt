SHARING CHECKLIST: 11 items
Giving other people access to a document, project or file.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 0 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SHARING
 1. Share by email or name, with a role (view, comment, edit)   (Practice)
 2. A link option with clear access (anyone with the link, or only people invited)   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 3. Who already has access listed, with roles changeable   (Practice)

LINKS
 4. Links can expire and be switched off   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 5. Link addresses are random and long   (Standard: rfc-uuid)
 6. Copy link with confirmation   (Standard: wcag-status)

TELLING PEOPLE
 7. Invitees notified with what was shared and by whom   (Practice)
 8. Owners see when access is requested   (Practice)

SAFETY
 9. Sharing outside the organisation can be limited by admins   (Practice)
10. Shares and removals logged   (Standard: owasp-logging)  -> see operations/logging

ACCESS
11. The sharing dialog follows dialog rules (focus, Escape, labels)   (Standard: apg-dialog)  -> see design-system/modal

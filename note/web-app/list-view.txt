LIST VIEW CHECKLIST: 19 items
A simple list of items (tasks, files, contacts) for scanning and opening, lighter than a data table.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 7 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ITEMS
 1. Each row: the name first, then two or three key facts   (Research: nng-scanning)
 2. Icon or photo to tell types apart   (Practice)
 3. Status in words or icons with names, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
 4. The whole row opens the item; actions in a menu at the end   (Practice)
 5. Hover shows quick actions, which are also reachable by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-hover, wcag-keyboard)

ORDER AND GROUPING
 6. Sort choice (name, date, priority), remembered   (Practice)
 7. Grouping by date, status or owner, with headings and counts   (Practice)
 8. Groups can collapse   (Standard: apg-accordion)

FINDING
 9. Filter and search above the list   (Research: nng-filters)
10. Active filters shown with remove   (Research: nng-filters)

ACTING
11. Select several with checkboxes, with a bulk action bar   (Practice)
12. Drag to reorder where order matters, with a keyboard way too   (Standard: wcag-dragging)
13. Swipe actions on touch screens, with a visible alternative   (Research: nng-swipe)

LOTS OF ITEMS
14. Pagination or load more, with the total   (Research: nng-infinite)
15. Back keeps the scroll position   (Practice)

STATES
16. Skeleton rows while loading   (Research: nng-skeleton)
17. Empty and no-results states   (Research: nng-empty)

ACCESS
18. A real list (or table if there are columns)   (Standard: wcag-info)
19. Row links read well alone   (Standard: wcag-link)

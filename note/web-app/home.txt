HOME (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 17 items
The first screen after signing in, which helps people pick up where they left off and see what needs them.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 4 Research, 9 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHAT IT SHOWS
 1. A greeting only if short; the useful parts above the fold   (Research: nng-scanning)
 2. Continue where you left off: recent items   (Practice)
 3. What needs me: assigned, due soon, mentions, approvals   (Practice)
 4. Key numbers for the person's role, if relevant   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 5. Quick actions for the most common jobs (New project)   (Practice)
 6. Announcements kept small and dismissible   (Practice)

NEW USERS
 7. Getting-started checklist until done, then it goes away   (Practice)
 8. Templates and sample content   (Practice)
 9. Empty sections teach, not just say "nothing here"   (Research: nng-empty)

NAVIGATION
10. Main menu in the same place as every other screen   (Standard: wcag-consistent)
11. Workspace or team switch visible if the person is in several   (Practice)
12. Search reachable from here   (Research: nng-search)

PERSONAL
13. Order or hide sections; the choice is remembered   (Practice)
14. Different home for different roles (admin, member)   (Practice)

SPEED
15. Loads fast; sections load on their own with skeletons   (Standard: webdev-lcp, nng-skeleton)
16. No layout jumping as sections arrive   (Standard: webdev-cls)

ACCESS
17. Page title names the app and "Home"   (Standard: wcag-title)

ANALYTICS CHECKLIST: 25 items
Charts and numbers that show how something is performing over time, for people to understand and decide.
Backing: 1 Rule, 6 Standard, 7 Research, 11 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

OVERVIEW
 1. The few numbers that matter most at the top, each with change from the last period   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 2. Change shown as number and arrow or word, not only green and red   (Standard: wcag-color)
 3. Date range picker with common presets (7 days, 30 days, this month) and compare to previous   (Practice)
 4. Time zone of the data stated   (Practice)
 5. When the data was last updated   (Research: nng-status)

CHARTS
 6. The right chart for the question: lines for trends, bars for comparison, no 3D or pies with many slices   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 7. Axes labelled with units; y-axis starts at zero for bars   (Practice)
 8. Tooltip on hover and focus showing exact values   (Standard: wcag-hover)
 9. Legend near the lines, or labels on the lines   (Practice)
10. Colours that work for colour-blind people and in dark mode   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast, wcag-color)
11. A table view of the same data for screen readers and export   (Standard: wcag-nontext)

EXPLORING
12. Filter by segment (country, plan, channel)   (Research: nng-filters)
13. Click a chart point to see the records behind it   (Practice)
14. Filters and range kept in the address, so views can be shared   (Practice)
15. Save a view or report   (Practice)

TRUST
16. Definitions of each number on demand ("Active user: signed in within 7 days")   (Practice)
17. Incomplete periods (today) marked   (Practice)
18. Data gaps or delays shown, not drawn as zero   (Practice)

STATES AND SPEED
19. Loading skeletons in chart shapes   (Research: nng-skeleton)
20. No data state explains why (no events yet, filter too narrow)   (Research: nng-empty)
21. Slow queries show progress and can be cancelled   (Research: nng-response)

ACCESS AND EXPORT
22. Export to CSV and image   (Practice)
23. Animated charts respect reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion, wcag-animation)
24. Works at phone width (charts scale, tables scroll)   (Standard: wcag-reflow)
25. Personal data aggregated or anonymised where possible   (Rule: gdpr-25)

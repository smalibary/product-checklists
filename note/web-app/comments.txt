COMMENTS CHECKLIST: 22 items
Notes people leave on an item or on a part of it, with replies, mentions and resolving.
Backing: 1 Rule, 6 Standard, 1 Research, 14 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WRITING
 1. A box with a clear label and a Send button; Enter behaviour stated (Enter sends or adds a line)   (Standard: wcag-labels)
 2. Mention people with @, with suggestions as you type   (Standard: apg-combobox)
 3. Basic formatting, links, files and emoji   (Practice)
 4. Draft kept if the person leaves before sending   (Practice)
 5. Comment on a selection of text or a spot on an image   (Practice)

READING
 6. Each comment: author, photo, time, text, and actions (reply, edit, delete, react)   (Practice)
 7. Threads: replies grouped under the comment   (Practice)
 8. Edited comments marked "edited"   (Practice)
 9. New comments since last visit marked   (Practice)
10. Long threads collapse older replies   (Practice)
11. Sort by oldest or newest   (Practice)

RESOLVING
12. Resolve a thread to hide it; show resolved ones on demand   (Practice)
13. Re-open a resolved thread   (Practice)

NOTIFICATIONS
14. Mentioned people are told; others can follow or mute the thread   (Research: nng-notifications)
15. Email replies post back as comments where supported   (Practice)

RULES
16. Only the author edits; author and admins delete   (Standard: owasp-authz)
17. Deleted comment leaves "Comment deleted" so replies make sense   (Practice)
18. Report or hide abuse in public comments   (Rule: eu-dsa)
19. Text escaped so it cannot run code (no script injection)   (Standard: owasp-xss)

ACCESS
20. New comments announced politely   (Standard: wcag-status)
21. Each comment readable as one unit; the thread works by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-keyboard, wcag-info)
22. Times readable as exact times   (Practice)

CALENDAR CHECKLIST: 12 items
Showing and planning events by day, week and month.
Backing: 0 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

VIEWS
 1. Day, week, month and list views, with today marked   (Practice)
 2. The person's time zone shown and changeable   (Standard: iana-tz)
 3. Week start and date format by locale; Hijri where expected   (Standard: cldr)

EVENTS
 4. Create by clicking a time slot or a button   (Practice)
 5. Events show title, time and colour with text, not colour alone   (Standard: wcag-color)
 6. Overlapping events stay readable   (Practice)
 7. Repeating events with "this one or all" when changing   (Standard: rfc-icalendar)

SHARING
 8. Export and subscribe through iCalendar   (Standard: rfc-icalendar)  -> see web-app/import-export
 9. Invites show who is coming   (Practice)  -> see flows/inviting-a-teammate

ACCESS
10. Keyboard moves between days and events   (Standard: apg-grid)
11. Each event has an accessible name with its date and time   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
12. Drag to move also has a form alternative   (Standard: wcag-dragging)

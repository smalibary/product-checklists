DOCUMENT VIEW CHECKLIST: 22 items
Reading and editing one document (a page, a note, a file) inside the app, often with others.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 2 Research, 10 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

READING
 1. Title as the main heading, with owner, last edited time and by whom   (Practice)
 2. Contents list from headings for long documents   (Standard: wcag-headings)
 3. Comfortable line length (about 60 to 80 characters) and line height   (Standard: butterick-line, wcag-text-spacing)
 4. Readable at 200% zoom and in dark mode   (Standard: wcag-reflow, mdn-color-scheme)
 5. Images, tables and code blocks fit the width or scroll inside their box   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

EDITING
 6. Clear difference between view and edit mode, or edit in place with a visible cursor   (Practice)
 7. Saving automatic with "Saved" or "Saving..." shown   (Research: nng-status)
 8. Offline edits kept and synced later   (Practice)
 9. Undo and redo with keyboard shortcuts   (Research: nng-control)
10. Formatting toolbar with named buttons, also by keyboard shortcut   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
11. Paste from other apps keeps simple formatting   (Practice)

TOGETHER
12. Who else is here, with photos and coloured cursors plus names   (Practice)
13. Comments on selected text   (Practice)
14. Suggest changes mode for reviewers   (Practice)
15. Version history   (Practice)

SHARING
16. Share button showing who has access and their level (view, comment, edit)   (Standard: owasp-authz)
17. Link sharing off by default, or limited to the workspace   (Standard: owasp-authz)
18. Export to PDF, Word, Markdown   (Practice)
19. Print layout   (Practice)

ACCESS
20. Editor works with screen readers and keyboard only   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)
21. Headings, lists and tables are real structure, not just bold text   (Standard: wcag-info)
22. Language of the document set, with right-to-left for Arabic   (Standard: w3c-dir)

WORKSPACE SWITCHER CHECKLIST: 9 items
Moving between teams, accounts or organisations in one app.
Backing: 0 Rule, 2 Standard, 2 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SHOWING
 1. Current workspace name and logo always visible   (Research: nng-indicators)
 2. The list shows each workspace with name and role   (Practice)
 3. Search when there are many   (Practice)

SWITCHING
 4. Switch in one or two clicks   (Practice)
 5. Lands on the same kind of page in the new workspace where possible   (Practice)
 6. Each workspace's data fully separated   (Standard: owasp-multitenant)

ADDING
 7. Create or join a workspace from the switcher   (Practice)

CLARITY
 8. Clear signs of which workspace you are in before changing anything   (Research: nng-indicators)

ACCESS
 9. Works as a menu button with keyboard   (Standard: apg-menubutton)

SOURCES
- nng-indicators: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/indicators-validations-notifications/
- owasp-multitenant: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Multi_Tenant_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html

COMMAND PALETTE CHECKLIST: 11 items
A search box that opens with a shortcut (Ctrl+K) and runs any command or jumps anywhere.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 3 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

OPENING
 1. Ctrl+K or Cmd+K, plus a visible button for people who do not know the shortcut   (Research: nng-shortcuts)
 2. Opens with focus in the search field   (Standard: apg-combobox)

RESULTS
 3. Results update as you type, forgiving typos   (Practice)
 4. Grouped (pages, actions, recent) with group names   (Standard: apg-listbox)
 5. Each result shows its shortcut where one exists   (Research: nng-shortcuts)
 6. Recent and suggested items shown before typing   (Practice)

KEYS
 7. Arrow keys move, Enter runs, Escape closes   (Standard: apg-combobox)
 8. The active result announced to screen readers   (Standard: apg-combobox, wcag-name-role)

SAFETY
 9. Only commands the person may use are listed   (Standard: owasp-authz)
10. Dangerous commands still ask to confirm   (Research: nng-confirm)

AFTER
11. Focus returns to where it was when closed   (Standard: apg-dialog)

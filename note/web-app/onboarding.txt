ONBOARDING (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 18 items
The first minutes in the app after sign-up, getting the person to their first real success.
Backing: 1 Rule, 4 Standard, 7 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SHAPE
 1. Lead to the first useful result fast ("aha moment"), not a tour of every feature   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 2. Ask a few setup questions only if the answers change what they see   (Practice)
 3. Each question skippable, with a sensible default   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 4. A progress indicator for multi-step setup   (Research: nng-progress)  -> see web-app/multi-step-form, design-system/progress
 5. A checklist of first tasks that ticks itself as they are done   (Practice)

HELP ALONG THE WAY
 6. Hints at the moment a feature is first used, not all at once   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 7. Tours short (3 to 5 steps), skippable, and restartable from help   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 8. Templates or sample data to start from, easy to delete   (Practice)
 9. Empty states that teach (see the Empty State note)   (Research: nng-empty)  -> see web-app/empty-state

TEAM PRODUCTS
10. Invite teammates offered after the person has done something useful   (Practice)  -> see flows/inviting-a-teammate
11. Import from other tools (CSV, competitors) offered early   (Practice)

RESPECT
12. No forced long tutorials or video before use   (Research: nng-onboarding)
13. Permissions (notifications, location) asked only when needed, with why   (Standard: webdev-permissions)  -> see web-app/notifications
14. Marketing consent separate from setup   (Rule: gdpr-7)
15. Onboarding state saved, so leaving and coming back continues where they were   (Practice)

ACCESS
16. Tour popovers reachable by keyboard, with focus moved in and back out   (Standard: apg-dialog, wcag-focus-order)  -> see design-system/popover
17. Tour highlights do not hide the focused element   (Standard: wcag-obscured)
18. Animations respect reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility, motion/motion-system

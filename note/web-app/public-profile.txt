PUBLIC PROFILE CHECKLIST: 17 items
The page other people see about a user: who they are and what they have made or shared.
Backing: 5 Rule, 5 Standard, 0 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Name, photo, short bio and role   (Practice)
 2. What the person has made or shared (posts, projects, reviews)   (Practice)
 3. Join date and simple activity numbers   (Practice)
 4. Links to their other profiles, marked as external   (Standard: wcag-link)
 5. Verified badges explained on hover or tap   (Standard: wcag-hover)

ACTIONS FOR VISITORS
 6. Follow, message or contact, if the product has them   (Practice)
 7. Share profile link   (Practice)
 8. Report or block the person, easy to find   (Rule: eu-dsa)

PRIVACY FOR THE OWNER
 9. The owner sees what is public, with a "View as visitor" preview   (Rule: gdpr-25)
10. Choose what is public field by field; private by default for sensitive data   (Rule: gdpr-25)
11. No email, phone or exact location shown unless the owner chose it   (Rule: gdpr-25)
12. Hide profile from search engines option   (Practice)
13. Children's profiles private by default   (Rule: coppa)

ADDRESS AND SEARCH
14. A readable address (/@name) that does not change silently   (Practice)
15. Person structured data where useful   (Standard: schema-person)
16. Page title with the person's name   (Standard: wcag-title)

ACCESS
17. Photo has alt text with the name, or is decorative beside the name   (Standard: wai-images)

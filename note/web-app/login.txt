LOGIN (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 22 items
Signing in to the app itself, including single sign-on for teams, passkeys and staying signed in. The website login note covers the basics.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 0 Research, 8 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WAYS IN
 1. Email or username, then password or passkey   (Standard: nist-63b, webauthn)
 2. Passkeys offered and filled in by the browser (autofill on the username field)   (Standard: webauthn, fido-ux)
 3. Sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, matching how the account was made   (Practice)
 4. Single sign-on for companies: type the work email and the company's sign-in opens   (Practice)
 5. Magic link by email as an option, with the code too for other devices   (Practice)

FIELDS
 6. Labels, autocomplete username and current-password   (Standard: mdn-autocomplete, wcag-labels)
 7. Paste and password managers allowed   (Standard: nist-63b, wcag-auth)
 8. Show password button   (Standard: nist-63b)
 9. Caps Lock warning   (Practice)

ERRORS AND SAFETY
10. Same message for wrong email or wrong password ("Email or password is wrong")   (Standard: owasp-auth)
11. Limited tries per account and per address, then slow down; no permanent lockout by others   (Standard: owasp-auth, nist-63b)
12. Two-step sign-in after the password where turned on   (Standard: owasp-mfa)
13. Robot checks only after suspicious attempts, never the first time   (Standard: wcag-auth)
14. Sign-in from a new device emailed   (Standard: owasp-auth)
15. All over HTTPS, cookies Secure and HttpOnly   (Standard: owasp-session)

STAYING SIGNED IN
16. "Keep me signed in" for trusted devices   (Practice)
17. Sessions end after a stated idle time, with a warning before   (Standard: owasp-session, wcag-timing)
18. After signing in, return to the page the person wanted   (Practice)
19. Signed out in another tab: the others notice   (Practice)

HELP
20. Forgot password link next to the password field   (Standard: owasp-forgot)
21. Link to sign up and to help   (Practice)

ACCESS
22. Errors next to fields and in a summary, focus moved there   (Standard: wcag-error, govuk-error-summary)

UPGRADE (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 20 items
The moment and screen where a signed-in user moves to a bigger plan.
Backing: 6 Rule, 3 Standard, 4 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO OFFER IT
 1. At the moment of need: when a limit is hit or a paid feature is tried   (Practice)
 2. Say which limit was hit and what the upgrade unlocks   (Research: nng-heuristics)
 3. Not on every screen; never block work already done   (Research: nng-control)
 4. Admins see the offer; members get "Ask your admin" with a one-click request   (Practice)

THE SCREEN
 5. Current plan and the suggested plan side by side   (Research: nng-comparison)
 6. The exact price, billing period and tax, in the account's currency   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)
 7. What is charged today (prorated amount) and on the next bill   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 8. When the new features start (now)   (Practice)
 9. Seats or usage that change the price, with a running total   (Practice)
10. Saved payment method shown, with a way to change it   (Practice)

PAYING
11. One confirm button that names the action and the amount ("Pay 120 SAR and upgrade")   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
12. Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) handled inside the flow where cards need it   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)
13. Busy state on the button; no double charge on double click   (Practice)
14. Card details through a PCI-certified provider   (Standard: pci-dss)  -> see flows/making-a-card-payment

AFTER
15. Success screen and email receipt   (Research: govuk-confirm)
16. New features visible at once, with a short pointer to them   (Practice)
17. Downgrade and cancel paths as easy to find as the upgrade   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)

HONESTY
18. No pre-ticked add-ons or hidden recurring charges   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
19. Trial-to-paid terms stated before the card is taken   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)

ACCESS
20. Focus moves to the dialog and back; Escape closes it   (Standard: apg-dialog)  -> see design-system/modal

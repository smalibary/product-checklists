BILLING (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 20 items
The in-app page where an account owner sees their plan, payment method, invoices and usage, and changes or cancels.
Backing: 6 Rule, 6 Standard, 0 Research, 8 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLAN
 1. Current plan, price, billing period and next charge date and amount   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 2. Usage against limits (seats, storage, calls) with a bar and numbers   (Practice)
 3. Change plan, with the price difference shown before confirming   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 4. Cancel subscription, as easy to find and do as signing up was   (Rule: ftc-negative-option, apple-subscriptions)
 5. What happens on cancel: access until the period ends, data kept for how long   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 6. Turn auto-renew on again after cancelling   (Practice)

PAYMENT
 7. Saved payment method (brand and last 4 digits, expiry), with change and remove   (Standard: pci-dss)
 8. Card expiring soon warned ahead   (Practice)
 9. Card details collected by a PCI-certified provider, never stored by the app   (Standard: pci-dss)
10. Strong customer authentication when the bank asks for it   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)
11. Failed payment: banner with what to do and the date access stops   (Practice)

INVOICES
12. List of invoices with date, amount, status (paid, failed, refunded) and PDF download   (Practice)
13. Billing details (company, address, tax ID) editable and shown on invoices   (Practice)
14. Invoices meet local rules (VAT, Saudi e-invoicing)   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)
15. Billing email can differ from the account email   (Practice)

PERMISSIONS
16. Only owners and billing admins see and change billing   (Standard: owasp-authz)
17. Others see "Ask your admin"   (Practice)

ACCESS
18. Invoice list is a real table with headers   (Standard: wai-tables)
19. Amounts read clearly by screen readers (currency named)   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
20. Confirmations for charges name the amount   (Standard: wcag-prevent)

TIMELINE / GANTT VIEW CHECKLIST: 21 items
Shows tasks as bars along a time line, with their dates, overlaps and dependencies.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 1 Research, 14 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

LAYOUT
 1. Task names in a fixed left column, bars on the right along time   (Practice)
 2. Today marked with a line   (Practice)
 3. Zoom levels: days, weeks, months, quarters   (Practice)
 4. Weekends and holidays shaded   (Practice)
 5. Grouping by project, person or status, with collapse   (Practice)
 6. Milestones as diamonds, distinct from bars   (Practice)

BARS
 7. Bar shows start and end; label with name inside or beside   (Practice)
 8. Colour by status or owner, with a legend, plus text or pattern   (Standard: wcag-color)
 9. Progress inside the bar   (Practice)
10. Dependencies as arrows; conflicts (task starts before its blocker ends) flagged   (Practice)  -> see operations/dependencies
11. Critical path highlight for project plans   (Practice)

EDITING
12. Drag to move, drag edges to change dates, snapping to days   (Practice)
13. Every drag has a non-drag way: edit dates in a form or with keys   (Standard: wcag-dragging)
14. Undo after a move   (Research: nng-control)  -> see flows/undoing-an-action
15. Moving a task offers to move its dependents   (Practice)

NAVIGATION
16. Scroll sideways in time, jump to today, jump to a date   (Practice)
17. Header with dates stays visible   (Practice)

ACCESS
18. A list or table view with the same data, for screen readers and phones   (Standard: wcag-info)  -> see web-app/data-table, design-system/accessibility
19. Keyboard reaches each bar and shows its dates   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)  -> see design-system/accessibility
20. Tooltips on hover also show on focus   (Standard: wcag-hover)  -> see design-system/tooltip

SPEED
21. Smooth with thousands of tasks (draw only what is visible)   (Standard: webdev-inp)

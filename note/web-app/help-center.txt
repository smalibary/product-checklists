HELP CENTER CHECKLIST: 23 items
The self-help library of articles that answers how-to questions and fixes problems.
Backing: 0 Rule, 5 Standard, 12 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FINDING
 1. A big search box at the top, with suggestions while typing   (Research: nng-help, baymard-autocomplete)  -> see web-app/search-results
 2. Topics grouped by task (getting started, billing, account), with article counts   (Research: nng-help)
 3. Popular articles listed   (Research: nng-help)
 4. Search forgives typos and synonyms   (Research: baymard-autocomplete)
 5. No-results page offers contact and related topics   (Research: nng-empty)

ARTICLES
 6. Titles that match the task ("Change your email")   (Research: nng-help)
 7. Steps numbered, one action each, with screenshots kept current   (Research: nng-help)
 8. Short, focused articles; one task per article   (Research: nng-help)
 9. Last updated date   (Practice)
10. Which plan or platform the article applies to   (Practice)
11. Videos with captions and a text version   (Standard: wcag-captions)
12. Related articles at the end   (Research: nng-help)
13. "Was this helpful?" with an optional comment   (Practice)

GETTING MORE HELP
14. Contact support easy to find, never hidden behind forced searches   (Research: nng-help)  -> see flows/contacting-support
15. Status page link for outages   (Practice)  -> see website/status, operations/incident-response
16. Community forum link, if any   (Practice)  -> see support/community-forum
17. In-app help opens the right article for the screen the person is on   (Research: nng-help)

STRUCTURE AND ACCESS
18. Breadcrumbs from topic to article   (Research: nng-breadcrumbs)  -> see design-system/breadcrumb
19. Headings in order, contents list on long articles   (Standard: wcag-headings)  -> see design-system/accessibility
20. Images and GIFs have alt text; animations can be paused   (Standard: wcag-nontext, wcag-pause)  -> see motion/motion-system, design-system/accessibility
21. Help link in the same place on every page of the app   (Standard: wcag-help)
22. Available in the app's languages   (Practice)
23. Articles indexable by search engines   (Standard: google-helpful)

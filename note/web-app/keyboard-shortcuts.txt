KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Key combinations that let regular users work faster.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DESIGN
 1. Shortcuts for the most frequent actions only   (Research: nng-shortcuts)
 2. Platform conventions followed (Ctrl on Windows, Cmd on Mac)   (Standard: hig-keyboards)
 3. No clash with browser or screen-reader keys   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 4. Single-letter shortcuts can be turned off or changed   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)  -> see design-system/accessibility

FINDING
 5. A help screen listing all shortcuts, opened with ?   (Research: nng-shortcuts)
 6. Shortcuts shown next to menu items and in tooltips   (Research: nng-shortcuts)  -> see design-system/tooltip

BEHAVIOUR
 7. Shortcuts do not fire while typing in a field   (Practice)
 8. Destructive shortcuts can be undone   (Research: nng-control)

ACCESS
 9. Everything a shortcut does can also be done another way   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)  -> see design-system/accessibility

MULTI-STEP FORM CHECKLIST: 22 items
A long form split into steps (a wizard), so people answer a few questions at a time.
Backing: 0 Rule, 16 Standard, 3 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SHAPE
 1. Split only when the form is long or later questions depend on earlier answers   (Research: nng-wizards)
 2. One topic per step; one question per page for hard or unfamiliar questions   (Standard: govuk-question)
 3. Steps named, with "Step 2 of 5" and a progress indicator   (Research: nng-progress)  -> see design-system/progress
 4. Before starting, say how long it takes and what to have ready   (Standard: govuk-question)
 5. A task list for long applications, so steps can be done in any order   (Standard: govuk-task-list)

MOVING
 6. Continue and Back buttons in the same places on every step   (Standard: wcag-consistent)
 7. Browser Back works and keeps answers   (Practice)
 8. Answers saved as people go; leaving and coming back continues   (Practice)
 9. Save and come back later, for long forms   (Standard: govuk-question)
10. Steps that do not apply are skipped, not shown greyed out   (Standard: govuk-question)

CHECKING
11. Errors checked when leaving a field or on Continue, never while typing the first time   (Research: baymard-inline)
12. Error summary at the top of the step, linking to each field   (Standard: govuk-error-summary)
13. Error message next to each field in words   (Standard: govuk-error-message, wcag-error)  -> see flows/showing-input-error
14. Answers already given are never asked again   (Standard: wcag-redundant)

THE END
15. A check-your-answers page with Change links for each answer   (Standard: govuk-check)
16. A clear final button naming the action ("Submit application")   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
17. Confirmation page with a reference number and what happens next   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
18. Confirmation by email   (Practice)

ACCESS
19. Focus moves to the step heading after Continue   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
20. Page title changes per step ("Step 2 of 5: Address")   (Standard: wcag-title)
21. Labels, autofill and the right keyboard on every field   (Standard: wcag-labels, mdn-autocomplete, mdn-inputmode)  -> see design-system/accessibility
22. No time limit, or one that can be extended   (Standard: wcag-timing)

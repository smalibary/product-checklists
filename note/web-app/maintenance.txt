MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST: 18 items
What people see when the app is down for planned work or an outage, and the warnings before it.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 11 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE PLANNED WORK
 1. Warned ahead in the app and by email: date, start time with time zone, how long, what stops working   (Practice)
 2. Planned at a quiet time for most users   (Practice)
 3. A banner a day before and an hour before, which can be closed   (Practice)
 4. Listed on the status page   (Standard: atlassian-incident)

THE PAGE ITSELF
 5. Says plainly that the app is down for maintenance, and that data is safe   (Practice)
 6. When it should be back, with time zone   (Practice)
 7. Link to the status page and support contact   (Standard: atlassian-incident)
 8. Branded but light: loads without the main app's servers   (Practice)
 9. Returns HTTP status 503 with a Retry-After header, so search engines do not drop pages   (Standard: google-maintenance, rfc-retry-after)
10. Checks by itself and reloads when the app is back   (Practice)

DURING
11. Work in progress saved before the cutover, or people warned to save   (Practice)
12. Read-only mode where possible instead of fully down   (Practice)
13. Sign-ins and payments never half-finished   (Standard: owasp-transaction)

AFTER
14. Notice that it is done, with what changed   (Practice)
15. If it ran late, say so honestly   (Standard: atlassian-incident)

ACCESS
16. Text readable, contrast meets WCAG, no animation-only message   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
17. Auto reload does not move focus or surprise screen readers   (Standard: wcag-timing)
18. In the app's languages   (Practice)

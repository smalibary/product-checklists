IMPORT AND EXPORT CHECKLIST: 12 items
Bringing data in from files or other tools, and taking it out.
Backing: 1 Rule, 5 Standard, 2 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

IMPORT
 1. Supported formats listed, with a template to download   (Standard: govuk-file-upload)
 2. A preview with column matching before anything is saved   (Practice)
 3. Errors listed by row and field, with a way to fix and retry   (Standard: govuk-error-summary)
 4. Large imports run in the background with progress   (Research: nng-progress)
 5. A summary at the end: added, updated, skipped   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
 6. Undo or delete the whole import   (Research: nng-control)  -> see flows/undoing-an-action

EXPORT
 7. Common formats (CSV, JSON, PDF)   (Practice)
 8. Exports of all personal data available to the person   (Rule: gdpr-20)  -> see backend/data-privacy
 9. Large exports emailed or ready to download later   (Practice)
10. CSV safe against formula injection   (Practice)  -> see code/secure-coding

SAFETY
11. Only data the person may see is exported   (Standard: owasp-authz)
12. Exports logged in the audit trail   (Standard: owasp-logging)  -> see web-app/audit-log

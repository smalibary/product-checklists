AUDIT LOG CHECKLIST: 19 items
The record of who did what, when and from where, for admins to check security and changes.
Backing: 1 Rule, 12 Standard, 2 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHAT IS RECORDED
 1. Who (person or key), what action, on which item, when, and from where (IP, device)   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 2. Sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password and 2FA changes   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 3. Permission and role changes, member added or removed   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 4. Settings, billing and data export changes   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 5. Before and after values for changes   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 6. Admin "view as user" sessions   (Standard: owasp-logging)

READING IT
 7. Newest first, with exact time and time zone (ISO 8601 on export)   (Standard: iso-8601)
 8. Filters for person, action type, item and date range   (Research: nng-filters)
 9. Search   (Research: nng-tables)
10. Each entry opens to the full detail   (Practice)
11. Plain-language descriptions ("Sara changed Ali's role from Member to Admin")   (Practice)

TRUST
12. Entries cannot be edited or deleted by anyone in the app   (Standard: owasp-logging)
13. Kept for a stated time per plan   (Practice)
14. Export to CSV and stream to outside tools (SIEM)   (Practice)
15. No passwords, tokens or full card numbers ever in the log   (Standard: owasp-logging, pci-dss)
16. Personal data in logs handled like any personal data (retention, access)   (Rule: gdpr-5, gdpr-32)

ACCESS
17. Only owners and security admins can see it   (Standard: owasp-authz)
18. Viewing the audit log is itself logged   (Standard: owasp-logging)
19. Real table with headers, works by keyboard   (Standard: wai-tables, wcag-keyboard)

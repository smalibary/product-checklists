PRICING (INSIDE THE APP) CHECKLIST: 20 items
The plans page shown to signed-in users, with their current plan and what changing would cost.
Backing: 6 Rule, 5 Standard, 2 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLANS
 1. Current plan marked clearly   (Practice)
 2. 2 to 4 plans side by side, one suggested   (Research: nng-comparison)
 3. Price per month and per year, with the yearly saving   (Practice)
 4. Prices include or exclude VAT, stated clearly   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)
 5. Per-seat prices show the total for the team's size   (Practice)
 6. Currency of the account (SAR for Saudi customers)   (Practice)

COMPARING
 7. The features that differ, not a wall of ticks   (Research: nng-comparison)
 8. Feature names with short explanations on hover or tap   (Standard: wcag-hover)
 9. Limits stated in numbers (projects, storage, members)   (Practice)
10. The person's own usage shown next to each plan's limit   (Practice)

CHANGING
11. Upgrade shows prorated charge today and the next bill   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)  -> see web-app/upgrade
12. Downgrade shows what will be lost or locked, and when it takes effect   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)  -> see web-app/upgrade
13. Owners change plans; members can request   (Standard: owasp-authz)  -> see web-app/upgrade
14. Contact sales for large plans, without hiding the price of smaller ones   (Practice)

HONESTY
15. No fake countdowns or "only 2 left"   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
16. Trial terms and auto-renewal stated before payment   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
17. Cancellation easy to find from here   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)  -> see flows/canceling-subscription

ACCESS
18. Comparison table with headers; ticks have text for screen readers   (Standard: wai-tables, wcag-nontext)  -> see web-app/data-table, design-system/accessibility
19. Works at phone width (plans stack, table scrolls)   (Standard: wcag-reflow)  -> see web-app/data-table, design-system/accessibility
20. Monthly/yearly switch is a real switch or radio group   (Standard: apg-radio)  -> see design-system/radio

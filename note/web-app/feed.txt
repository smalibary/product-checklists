FEED CHECKLIST: 20 items
A stream of posts or updates from people and things the user follows.
Backing: 5 Rule, 6 Standard, 4 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

POSTS
 1. Each post: author with photo and name, time, content, and actions (like, comment, share)   (Practice)
 2. Long posts cut with "Show more"   (Practice)
 3. Images and video sized before they load, so the feed does not jump   (Standard: webdev-cls)
 4. Video starts muted, with captions and controls   (Standard: wcag-captions, wcag-audio)
 5. Links show a preview card   (Practice)

ORDER AND CONTROL
 6. Order explained (latest or recommended), with a choice of latest where possible   (Rule: eu-dsa)
 7. Why am I seeing this, on recommended posts   (Rule: eu-dsa)
 8. Hide, mute, unfollow and report from each post   (Rule: eu-dsa)
 9. Ads and sponsored posts labelled clearly   (Rule: ftc-endorsements, eu-dsa)

LOADING
10. Infinite scroll keeps position on Back and has an end or a pause point   (Research: nng-infinite)
11. Footer still reachable (or links moved elsewhere) with infinite scroll   (Research: nng-infinite)
12. New posts shown as "5 new posts" button, not pushed in while reading   (Practice)
13. Skeleton posts while loading   (Research: nng-skeleton)
14. Empty feed suggests people or topics to follow   (Research: nng-empty)

ACCESS
15. Feed pattern: each post an article, keyboard moves post to post   (Standard: apg-feed)
16. Images have alt text, with a way for authors to add it   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
17. Relative times also readable as exact times   (Practice)
18. Animations and auto-play respect reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion, wcag-pause)

WELLBEING
19. No autoplay of next videos without a way to stop   (Standard: wcag-pause)
20. Teen accounts get safer defaults   (Rule: coppa, eu-dsa)

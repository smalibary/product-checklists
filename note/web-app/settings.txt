SETTINGS (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 20 items
The place where people change how the app works for them, or for their whole workspace.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 7 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STRUCTURE
 1. Personal settings separate from workspace (team) settings   (Practice)
 2. A side menu of sections (General, Account, Notifications, Billing, Members, Security, Integrations)   (Practice)
 3. Each section has its own address so it can be linked and bookmarked   (Practice)
 4. Search across settings for apps with many   (Practice)
 5. Most used settings first; dangerous ones last in a "Danger zone"   (Practice)

CONTROLS
 6. One control type per job: switch for on/off taking effect at once, checkbox when a Save button follows   (Research: nng-toggle)
 7. Each setting has a label and one line explaining what it changes   (Research: nng-heuristics)
 8. Defaults that suit most people   (Research: nng-heuristics)
 9. Disabled settings say why (plan, permission)   (Practice)

SAVING
10. Either save on change (with a "Saved" message) or one Save button per section, not a mix   (Research: nng-heuristics)
11. Saved message announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)
12. Unsaved changes warned before leaving   (Research: nng-control)
13. Undo, or reset to default   (Research: nng-control)
14. Failed save says why and keeps what was typed   (Standard: wcag-redundant)

DANGEROUS ACTIONS
15. Deleting a workspace or transferring ownership needs typing the name or re-entering the password   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
16. Say exactly what will be lost   (Research: nng-confirm)

ACCESS
17. Side menu is navigation with the current section marked   (Standard: apg-landmarks)
18. Headings, labels and keyboard work   (Standard: wcag-headings, wcag-labels, wcag-keyboard)
19. Works at phone width (menu becomes a list or a drop-down)   (Standard: wcag-reflow)
20. Dark mode and text size settings respected   (Practice: mdn-color-scheme)

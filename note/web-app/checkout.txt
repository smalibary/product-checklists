CHECKOUT (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 22 items
Paying inside the app for a plan, credits or an order, from review to confirmation.
Backing: 5 Rule, 8 Standard, 6 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ORDER REVIEW
 1. What is bought, quantity, price, tax, discount and total, before paying   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)
 2. Recurring charges stated: amount, how often, first charge date, how to cancel   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 3. Currency shown and fixed; SAR for Saudi customers   (Practice)
 4. Promo code field present but quiet (a link that opens it)   (Research: baymard-coupon)
 5. Edit the order without starting again   (Research: baymard-abandon)

DETAILS
 6. Only the fields needed; company and tax ID optional, for invoices   (Research: baymard-fields)
 7. Billing address only if the payment provider or tax needs it   (Research: baymard-fields)
 8. Autofill for name, card and address   (Standard: mdn-autocomplete, webdev-payment)
 9. Already-known details filled in   (Standard: wcag-redundant)

PAYMENT
10. Card fields from a PCI-certified provider   (Standard: pci-dss)
11. Card number with spaces, the brand detected, numeric keyboard   (Research: baymard-card)
12. Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay) and local methods (mada, STC Pay) where they suit   (Practice)
13. Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) inside the flow   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)
14. Trust signs near the card fields (lock, provider name)   (Research: baymard-trust)

SUBMITTING
15. Button names the action and amount ("Pay 120 SAR")   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
16. Busy state; double click never charges twice   (Standard: owasp-transaction)
17. Declined card: plain reason and what to try, details kept   (Standard: wcag-error)

AFTER
18. Confirmation page with what was bought, a receipt number and next steps   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
19. Receipt and tax invoice by email   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)
20. New access available at once   (Practice)

HONESTY AND ACCESS
21. No pre-ticked extras; terms and refund policy linked   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns, eu-consumer-rights)
22. Labels, errors in words, keyboard and phone width meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-labels, wcag-error, wcag-keyboard, wcag-reflow)

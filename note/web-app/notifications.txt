NOTIFICATIONS (WEB APP) CHECKLIST: 19 items
The in-app list (often a bell) of things that happened which the person may need to act on.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 5 Research, 8 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE BELL
 1. An icon button with a name and the unread count ("Notifications, 3 unread")   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
 2. Count capped (9+) and shown as a badge   (Research: nng-indicators)
 3. New notifications announced politely, not interrupting   (Standard: wcag-status)

THE LIST
 4. Newest first, grouped by today, this week, older   (Practice)
 5. Each item: who, what, on which item, and when (relative time, exact on hover)   (Practice)
 6. Unread items marked by more than colour (a dot plus bold)   (Standard: wcag-color)
 7. Clicking opens the item and marks it read   (Practice)
 8. Mark one or all as read   (Practice)
 9. Quick actions inline (approve, reply) for common jobs   (Practice)
10. Filters: all, mentions, assigned to me   (Practice)
11. Link to notification settings   (Practice)

VOLUME
12. Similar notifications combined ("Sara and 4 others commented")   (Research: nng-notifications)
13. Only things the person cares about; let them mute a thread   (Research: nng-notifications)
14. Old notifications cleared after a stated time   (Practice)

STATES
15. Empty state: "You're all caught up"   (Research: nng-empty)
16. Loading and error states   (Research: nng-skeleton)

ACCESS
17. The panel is a dialog or popover with focus moved in and Escape to close   (Standard: apg-dialog)
18. List items reachable by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)
19. Works at phone width (full-screen panel)   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

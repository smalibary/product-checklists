KANBAN BOARD CHECKLIST: 23 items
Cards in columns by stage (To do, Doing, Done), moved across as work goes on.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 5 Research, 11 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

COLUMNS
 1. Column name with a count of cards   (Practice)
 2. Work-in-progress limit per column, warned when passed   (Practice)
 3. Add, rename, reorder and hide columns   (Practice)
 4. Collapse a column   (Practice)
 5. Swimlanes (rows) by person, priority or project   (Practice)

CARDS
 6. Title, assignee photo, due date, labels and a small set of icons (comments, files)   (Research: nng-cards)
 7. Overdue shown by icon and word, not only red   (Standard: wcag-color)
 8. Labels have text, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
 9. Click opens the card detail in a side panel or page with its own address   (Practice)
10. Quick add at the bottom of each column   (Practice)

MOVING
11. Drag a card between columns and up and down   (Research: nng-drag)
12. A clear drop place shown while dragging   (Research: nng-drag)
13. Every drag has another way: a "Move to" menu or keyboard keys   (Standard: wcag-dragging, apg-menubutton)
14. Moving to Done may ask for a reason or set fields, only when needed   (Practice)
15. Undo after a move   (Research: nng-control)
16. Changes from others appear live without moving the card under the person's hand   (Practice)

FINDING
17. Filter by person, label and due date; search   (Research: nng-filters)
18. Quick "Only my cards" filter   (Practice)
19. Switch to list or table view   (Practice)

ACCESS
20. Moving by keyboard announced ("Card moved to Doing, position 2")   (Standard: wcag-status)
21. Columns are lists with headings; cards are list items   (Standard: wcag-info)
22. On phones: one column at a time, swipe or tabs between columns   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

SPEED
23. Smooth with hundreds of cards   (Standard: webdev-inp)

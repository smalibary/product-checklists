REPORTING CHECKLIST: 22 items
The tool for building, saving, scheduling and sharing reports (the Report View note covers reading one).
Backing: 2 Rule, 6 Standard, 5 Research, 9 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BUILDING A REPORT
 1. Start from templates for common questions   (Practice)
 2. Choose data, measures (sum, count, average) and grouping in plain words   (Practice)
 3. Preview updates as choices change   (Research: nng-response)
 4. Filters and date range with presets   (Research: nng-filters)
 5. Choose the chart type, with a sensible default   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 6. Calculated fields with a clear formula editor and errors in words   (Standard: wcag-error)

SAVING AND ORGANISING
 7. Name, describe and save reports into folders   (Practice)
 8. Mark favourites; recent reports listed   (Practice)
 9. Who owns each report and when it was last run   (Practice)

SHARING AND SCHEDULES
10. Share with people or teams, with view or edit rights   (Standard: owasp-authz)
11. Scheduled email or chat delivery, with time zone and format (PDF, CSV)   (Practice)
12. Recipients can unsubscribe from scheduled reports   (Rule: can-spam)
13. Report data follows the viewer's permissions (row-level access)   (Standard: owasp-authz)

SPEED AND LIMITS
14. Long reports run in the background with progress and a notice when done   (Research: nng-response)
15. Large exports emailed as a link   (Practice)
16. Limits stated (rows, date range)   (Practice)

TRUST
17. Definitions of each measure visible   (Practice)
18. Data freshness shown   (Research: nng-status)
19. Personal data limited to who needs it   (Rule: gdpr-25)

ACCESS
20. Builder works by keyboard; drag-and-drop has another way   (Standard: wcag-dragging, wcag-keyboard)
21. Charts with a data table view   (Standard: wcag-nontext)
22. Works at tablet width at least; reading works at phone width   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

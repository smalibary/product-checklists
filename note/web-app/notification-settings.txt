NOTIFICATION SETTINGS CHECKLIST: 19 items
Where people choose what they are told about, and how (email, in-app, push, text).
Backing: 3 Rule, 5 Standard, 1 Research, 10 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STRUCTURE
 1. Grouped by topic (mentions, comments, billing, security, product news)   (Practice)
 2. A grid: topics down the side, channels across the top (email, push, in-app)   (Practice)
 3. Each row says in plain words what triggers it   (Practice)
 4. Defaults set to what most people want, never to everything   (Research: nng-notifications)

CONTROLS
 5. Switches or checkboxes with clear names that include the topic and channel   (Standard: wcag-name-role, apg-switch)
 6. Changes saved at once, with a short "Saved" message announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)
 7. Pause all for a set time (1 hour, until tomorrow, a week)   (Practice)
 8. Quiet hours with the time zone   (Practice)
 9. How often: right away, a daily summary, a weekly summary   (Practice)

RULES
10. Security and billing alerts cannot be turned off, and say why   (Practice)
11. Marketing emails off by default where the law needs consent   (Rule: gdpr-7)
12. One-click unsubscribe in every marketing email, honoured quickly   (Rule: can-spam, rfc-unsubscribe)
13. Email senders follow the bulk sender rules (one-click unsubscribe, low spam rate)   (Rule: google-sender)
14. Push asked for only after the person has a reason to want it   (Standard: webdev-push)
15. If browser push is blocked, explain how to turn it back on   (Practice)

PER ITEM
16. Follow or mute a single project, thread or item from where it is   (Practice)
17. Links in each email to change the setting for that topic   (Practice)

ACCESS
18. Grid is a real table or list with headers so screen readers read topic and channel   (Standard: wai-tables, wcag-info)
19. Works at phone width (the grid becomes a list)   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

A/B TESTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Comparing two versions with real people to see which works better.
Backing: 1 Rule, 0 Standard, 7 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE
 1. One clear question and one main measure chosen up front   (Research: kohavi-book)
 2. Guard measures (speed, errors, cancels) that must not get worse   (Research: kohavi-book)
 3. Sample size worked out before starting   (Research: kohavi-book, nng-ab)

RUNNING
 4. People split at random and keep their version   (Research: kohavi-book)
 5. Split checked: groups the expected sizes   (Research: kohavi-book)
 6. Run full weeks; no stopping early on a good day   (Research: kohavi-book)

AFTER
 7. Results read with their uncertainty, not just the winner   (Research: kohavi-book)
 8. Losing and flat tests recorded too   (Practice)

ETHICS
 9. No tests that trick or harm people   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

SOURCES
- kohavi-book: https://experimentguide.com/
- nng-ab: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ab-testing/
- ftc-dark-patterns: https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light

BLOG CHECKLIST: 11 items
Articles that bring readers, answer questions and stay findable.
Backing: 1 Rule, 7 Standard, 2 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ARTICLE PAGE
 1. A clear title and summary at the top   (Research: nng-microcontent)
 2. Author, publish date and update date shown   (Standard: gsc-date)
 3. Article structured data   (Standard: google-article)  -> see growth/technical-seo
 4. Headings, short paragraphs and a table of contents for long posts   (Research: nng-web-writing)
 5. Images with alt text and sizes set   (Standard: wcag-nontext, webdev-images)  -> see design-system/accessibility

LISTING
 6. Posts by topic or tag, with page-by-page lists   (Standard: gsc-links)
 7. Related posts at the end   (Practice)

SHARING
 8. Link preview tags on every post   (Standard: ogp)  -> see growth/link-previews
 9. An RSS or Atom feed   (Standard: rfc-atom)  -> see web-app/feed

UPKEEP
10. Old posts reviewed; out-of-date ones updated or redirected   (Standard: google-helpful, gsc-redirects)
11. Comments, if any, moderated and marked ugc   (Rule: gsc-qualify)  -> see web-app/comments

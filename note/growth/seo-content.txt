SEO CONTENT CHECKLIST: 11 items
Writing pages people search for and find useful.
Backing: 2 Rule, 7 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BASICS
 1. Written for people first, helpful and original   (Standard: google-helpful)
 2. A unique page title naming the topic   (Standard: google-title, nng-microcontent)
 3. A meta description that sums the page up   (Standard: google-snippet)  -> see growth/technical-seo
 4. One clear main heading, then headings by topic   (Standard: gsc-starter)

WRITING
 5. Front-load key words in headings and first lines   (Research: nng-web-writing)
 6. Short paragraphs and lists people can scan   (Research: nng-web-writing)
 7. Plain words people actually search with   (Standard: govuk-content)  -> see design-system/content-voice

IMAGES
 8. Descriptive alt text and file names   (Standard: gsc-images, wcag-nontext)  -> see design-system/accessibility

RULES
 9. No keyword stuffing, hidden text or copied pages   (Rule: gsc-spam)
10. Paid and sponsored links marked as such   (Rule: gsc-qualify)
11. Author and date shown; date updated only when content changes   (Standard: gsc-date)

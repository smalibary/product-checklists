ANALYTICS TRACKING CHECKLIST: 10 items
Measuring what people do so decisions use evidence, with their consent.
Backing: 3 Rule, 3 Standard, 2 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLAN
 1. Goals first, then the few events that show them   (Research: heart, nng-analytics)
 2. One named list of events and their details, kept in the repo   (Standard: ga4-events)
 3. Events named the same way everywhere   (Standard: ga4-events)

CONSENT
 4. No tracking before consent where the law requires it   (Rule: gdpr-7, eprivacy)
 5. Consent mode passes the choice to every tag   (Standard: ga4-consent)
 6. Global Privacy Control respected   (Rule: gpc)

DATA
 7. No personal data (names, emails) in events   (Rule: gdpr-5)
 8. Internal and test traffic filtered out   (Practice)

USE
 9. Numbers paired with research before big changes   (Research: nng-analytics)
10. Each dashboard names its owner and question   (Practice)

APP STORE LISTING CHECKLIST: 10 items
The store page that turns browsers into downloads.
Backing: 5 Rule, 5 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WORDS
 1. Name and subtitle say what it does   (Standard: apple-product-page, play-listing)
 2. First lines of the description carry the main value   (Standard: apple-product-page)
 3. Keywords used honestly, no competitor names   (Rule: apple-review, play-policy)

PICTURES
 4. Screenshots in every required size, showing real screens   (Rule: apple-screenshots)
 5. First two screenshots tell the story   (Standard: apple-product-page)
 6. An optional short preview video of real use   (Standard: apple-product-page)

TRUST
 7. Privacy details filled in truthfully   (Rule: apple-privacy-labels, play-data-safety)
 8. Age rating answered honestly   (Rule: apple-age-rating)
 9. Support and privacy policy addresses working   (Rule: apple-review)  -> see website/privacy

UPKEEP
10. Replies to reviews   (Standard: apple-review)

EMAIL MARKETING CHECKLIST: 10 items
Newsletters and offers people asked for and can easily leave.
Backing: 6 Rule, 1 Standard, 2 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SIGNING UP
 1. Clear opt-in, never a pre-ticked box   (Rule: gdpr-7, eprivacy)
 2. Says what is sent and how often   (Research: nng-email)
 3. Double opt-in confirms the address   (Practice)

EACH EMAIL
 4. True sender and subject line   (Rule: can-spam)
 5. Marked as an ad where it is one   (Rule: can-spam)
 6. Postal address included   (Rule: can-spam)
 7. Scannable layout that works on phones   (Research: nng-email)

LEAVING
 8. An unsubscribe link in every email, working in one step   (Rule: can-spam, nng-unsubscribe)  -> see flows/unsubscribing-from-emails
 9. Unsubscribes honoured within 10 business days (quicker is better)   (Rule: can-spam)  -> see flows/unsubscribing-from-emails

DELIVERY
10. Sender authentication set up and bulk-sender rules met   (Standard: google-sender, gmail-postmaster)

SOCIAL MEDIA LINKS CHECKLIST: 8 items
Linking the product to its social accounts and letting people share.
Backing: 3 Rule, 3 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PROFILES
 1. Same name, logo and bio on every account   (Practice)
 2. Profile links back to the site   (Practice)
 3. Site links to accounts in the footer with clear labels   (Standard: wcag-link)

SHARING
 4. Share buttons use the system share sheet on phones   (Standard: webshare)
 5. Link previews set on shared pages   (Standard: ogp, x-cards)
 6. No tracking scripts from social buttons before consent   (Rule: eprivacy)

RULES
 7. Paid posts and partnerships disclosed   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
 8. Reviews and testimonials real   (Rule: ftc-reviews)

INVITING A TEAMMATE (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 10 items
Adding someone to a workspace by email or link.
Backing: 1 Rule, 6 Standard, 0 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

INVITING
 1. Enter one or many email addresses, with checks   (Standard: govuk-email)
 2. Pick a role, with what each role can do   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 3. A link option, with expiry   (Standard: owasp-authz)

SENDING
 4. Invite email says who invited, to what, with one button   (Practice)
 5. Pending invites listed, can be resent or cancelled   (Practice)
 6. Limits on invites per day   (Standard: owasp-dos)

ACCEPTING
 7. Existing accounts join with one click; new people sign up and land in the workspace   (Practice)  -> see website/sign-up, web-app/workspace-switcher
 8. The invite link works once and expires   (Standard: owasp-forgot)

BILLING
 9. Seat costs shown before inviting on paid plans   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees)

ACCESS
10. Results announced; errors linked to fields   (Standard: wcag-status, wcag-error)

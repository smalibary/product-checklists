UNSUBSCRIBING FROM EMAILS (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 7 items
Stopping marketing emails in one step.
Backing: 4 Rule, 0 Standard, 0 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

UNSUBSCRIBING
 1. One click from the email (List-Unsubscribe)   (Rule: rfc-unsubscribe, google-sender)
 2. Works without signing in   (Rule: can-spam)
 3. Honoured quickly (within 2 days for bulk senders)   (Rule: google-sender, yahoo-sender)

PAGE
 4. Confirms it is done   (Practice)
 5. Optional: choose which emails to keep   (Practice)
 6. No guilt wording or tricks   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

ACCOUNT EMAILS
 7. Security and receipt emails still sent; this is explained   (Practice)

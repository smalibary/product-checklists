SUBMITTING A FORM (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 17 items
The moment a form is sent: checking, sending, and telling the person what happened.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 2 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE SUBMIT
 1. Button always active; checks run on submit   (Research: baymard-inline)
 2. Button names the action ("Send request"), not "Submit"   (Research: nng-buttons)
 3. For legal, money or data changes: review step or a way to correct   (Standard: wcag-prevent)

ON SUBMIT
 4. All fields checked; error summary at the top with links   (Standard: wcag-error, govuk-error-summary)
 5. Focus moves to the summary or first wrong field   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
 6. Busy state on the button; no double send   (Standard: owasp-transaction)
 7. Works if the connection is slow: what was typed is kept   (Standard: wcag-timing)

SUCCESS
 8. Confirmation page or message saying what happens next and when   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
 9. Reference number and email copy where useful   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
10. Form not resent on refresh (redirect after sending)   (Practice)

FAILURE
11. Server error: plain message, data kept, Try again   (Standard: wcag-error)
12. Session expired: keep the data and sign in again   (Standard: wcag-timing)  -> see web-app/login, backend/sessions

SAFETY
13. Server checks everything again   (Standard: owasp-authz)
14. Spam protection without robot puzzles that block people (honeypot, rate limits)   (Standard: wcag-auth)  -> see api/rate-limits
15. CSRF protection   (Standard: owasp-csrf)

ACCESS
16. Enter submits from a text field   (Standard: wcag-on-input)
17. Result announced   (Standard: wcag-status)

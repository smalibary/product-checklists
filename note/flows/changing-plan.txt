CHANGING PLAN (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 8 items
Upgrading or downgrading a subscription.
Backing: 2 Rule, 3 Standard, 2 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Plans compared side by side, current plan marked   (Research: nng-pricing)
 2. The new price, when it starts and any prorated charge shown before confirming   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees, ftc-negative-option)

DOWNGRADING
 3. What will be lost said plainly   (Practice)
 4. Data kept or exportable   (Rule: gdpr-20)

CONFIRMING
 5. One clear button naming the action   (Research: nng-buttons)
 6. Confirmation page and email with the new terms   (Standard: govuk-confirm)

STORES
 7. Plan changes for store purchases go through the store   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)

SERVER
 8. Plan change confirmed by the payment provider before access changes   (Standard: stripe-webhooks)

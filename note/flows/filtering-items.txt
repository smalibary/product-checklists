FILTERS
 1. Filters that match how people choose (price, size, brand, date, status)   (Research: baymard-filters, nng-filters)
 2. Count of results per option   (Research: baymard-filters)
 3. Options with no results hidden or marked   (Research: baymard-filters)
 4. Price and number ranges with fields, not only a slider   (Research: nng-sliders)

APPLYING
 5. Desktop: results update at once on each change   (Research: nng-filters)
 6. Phone: filters in a sheet with "Show 42 results" button   (Research: baymard-filters)
 7. Loading shown without jumping the page   (Standard: wcag-on-input)

SEEING WHAT IS APPLIED
 8. Applied filters as removable chips above results   (Research: baymard-filters)
 9. "Clear all"   (Research: nng-filters)
10. Filters kept in the page address so back, share and refresh keep them   (Practice)

RESULTS
11. Result count updates and is announced   (Standard: wcag-status)
12. No results: show which filters to remove   (Research: nng-empty)
13. Sort kept separate from filters   (Research: nng-filters)

ACCESS
14. Filter groups as fieldsets with names   (Standard: wcag-info)
15. Checkboxes and radios are real controls   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
16. Focus does not jump when results update   (Standard: wcag-on-input)
17. Targets at least 24 px web / 44 pt iOS / 48 dp Android   (Standard: wcag-target, hig-layout, m3-layout)

DELETING ACCOUNT (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 17 items
Permanently removing an account and its data, by the person's own choice.
Backing: 6 Rule, 6 Standard, 2 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FINDING IT
 1. In account settings, clearly named   (Standard: apple-deletion, play-deletion)
 2. Inside the app if accounts are made in the app (App Store); a web page too (Google Play)   (Standard: apple-deletion, play-deletion)

BEFORE DELETING
 3. Say what will be deleted, what is kept by law and for how long   (Rule: gdpr-17)
 4. Offer to download data first   (Rule: gdpr-20)
 5. Active subscriptions: say they must be cancelled, with a link (store plans in the store)   (Standard: apple-deletion)
 6. Owned shared spaces: transfer or delete them   (Practice)
 7. Alternatives offered once (deactivate, sign out), not pushed   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)

CONFIRMING
 8. Password or a fresh sign-in   (Standard: owasp-auth)
 9. Typed confirmation for big accounts ("type DELETE")   (Research: nng-confirm)
10. Final button names the action ("Delete my account") in danger style   (Research: nng-confirm)

AFTER
11. Signed out, confirmation screen and email   (Practice)
12. Grace period to undo (for example 30 days) said clearly, with how to undo   (Practice)
13. Data deleted from all systems and backups within a stated time   (Rule: gdpr-17)
14. Third parties told to delete   (Rule: gdpr-17)

RULES
15. GDPR right to erasure; Saudi PDPL also gives the right to destruction of personal data   (Rule: gdpr-17, pdpl)

ACCESS
16. Every step works by keyboard and screen reader   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)
17. No time limit on the confirmation   (Standard: wcag-timing)

ADDING TO CART (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 17 items
From choosing a product's options to the item being in the cart, with clear feedback.
Backing: 1 Rule, 8 Standard, 5 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE ADDING
 1. Required options (size, colour) chosen first; the button says what is missing   (Research: baymard-cart)
 2. Price, stock and delivery estimate visible near the button   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 3. Out of stock: button replaced by "Notify me", not hidden   (Practice)
 4. Quantity choice where it makes sense   (Practice)

ADDING
 5. Button shows busy state; no double add on double tap   (Standard: owasp-transaction)
 6. Works without signing in (guest cart)   (Research: baymard-guest)

FEEDBACK
 7. Confirmation without leaving the page: a small panel or message with the item, "View cart" and "Checkout"   (Research: baymard-cart)
 8. Cart count updates at once   (Standard: m3-badges)
 9. Message announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)
10. Haptic on phones   (Standard: hig-feedback)

PROBLEMS
11. Failure explained ("Only 2 left") with a way forward   (Standard: wcag-error)
12. Price changed since the page loaded: say so   (Practice)

AFTER
13. Person stays where they were to keep shopping   (Research: baymard-cart)
14. Cart kept across visits and devices   (Research: baymard-cart)

ACCESS
15. Option pickers are real buttons or radios with names   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
16. Focus stays sensible after adding   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
17. Targets at least 24 px web / 44 pt iOS / 48 dp Android   (Standard: wcag-target, hig-layout, m3-layout)

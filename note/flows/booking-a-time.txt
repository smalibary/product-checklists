BOOKING A TIME (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 10 items
Choosing a date and time slot for an appointment or meeting.
Backing: 1 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Available days and times only, in the person's time zone, shown clearly   (Standard: iana-tz)
 2. Time zone changeable   (Standard: iana-tz)
 3. Date picker that works by keyboard   (Standard: apg-datepicker)  -> see design-system/date-picker

DETAILS
 4. Only the fields needed   (Rule: gdpr-5)
 5. Summary before confirming: what, when, where, price   (Standard: govuk-check)

CONFIRMING
 6. Slot held while finishing, with a clear time limit that can be extended   (Standard: wcag-timing)
 7. Confirmation page and email with a calendar file   (Standard: govuk-confirm, rfc-icalendar)  -> see web-app/calendar

CHANGING
 8. Reschedule and cancel links in the email   (Practice)
 9. Reminders before the time   (Practice)

ACCESS
10. Slots announced with full date and time   (Standard: wcag-name-role)

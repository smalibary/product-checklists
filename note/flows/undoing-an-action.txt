UNDOING AN ACTION (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 7 items
Reversing a mistake right after making it.
Backing: 0 Rule, 3 Standard, 4 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

OFFERING UNDO
 1. Undo for deletes, moves and sends instead of asking "Are you sure?" each time   (Research: nng-control)
 2. An Undo button in a toast for a few seconds   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
 3. Time long enough to read and act, or until dismissed   (Standard: wcag-timing)

BEHAVIOUR
 4. Undo restores everything exactly   (Research: nng-control)
 5. Ctrl+Z works where people expect it   (Research: nng-shortcuts)
 6. A bin or history for later undo   (Research: nng-control)

ACCESS
 7. Toast announced and reachable by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-status, wcag-keyboard)

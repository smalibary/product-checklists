MAKING A CARD PAYMENT (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 18 items
Typing card details, passing the bank's check, and getting a result.
Backing: 2 Rule, 9 Standard, 6 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FIELDS
 1. Card number, expiry, security code, name only if needed   (Research: baymard-card)
 2. Card fields from a certified provider (hosted fields) so card data never touches your servers   (Standard: pci-dss)
 3. Numbers keyboard and autofill (cc-number, cc-exp, cc-csc)   (Standard: mdn-autocomplete, mdn-inputmode)
 4. Spaces in card number added automatically and allowed when pasted   (Research: baymard-card)
 5. Card brand detected and shown, including mada   (Practice)
 6. Expiry as MM/YY in one field   (Research: baymard-card)

CHECKS
 7. Number checked as typed (Luhn), errors on leaving the field   (Research: baymard-inline)
 8. Security code help: where to find it   (Research: baymard-card)

PAYING
 9. Button with the amount ("Pay 245 SAR")   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
10. Busy state; no double charge (one payment per order key)   (Standard: owasp-transaction, stripe-idempotency)
11. Bank check (3-D Secure) opens in the page, explained   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)

RESULTS
12. Declined: a plain reason where known, and what to try (another card)   (Standard: wcag-error)
13. Details kept except the security code   (Standard: pci-dss)
14. Success: confirmation with order number; receipt and VAT invoice by email   (Rule: govuk-confirm, zatca-einvoice)

TRUST
15. Secure checkout signs near the fields, without clutter   (Research: baymard-trust)
16. Saved cards only with consent, shown by brand and last 4   (Standard: pci-dss)

ACCESS
17. Errors announced and linked to fields   (Standard: wcag-error)
18. The bank page in the check is reachable by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)

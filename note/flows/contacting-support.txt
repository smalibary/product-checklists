CONTACTING SUPPORT (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 18 items
Getting help from a person or a bot, from the first question to an answer.
Backing: 2 Rule, 5 Standard, 5 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FINDING HELP
 1. Help link in the same place on every page   (Standard: wcag-consistent-help)
 2. Search help articles first, but never hide the way to a person   (Research: nng-help)
 3. Ways shown with response times (chat now, email in 24 hours)   (Research: nng-contact)

ASKING
 4. Choose a topic, then a short form   (Practice)
 5. Account details and the page they came from attached automatically, with notice   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 6. Attach screenshots   (Practice)
 7. Suggested articles as they type, without blocking sending   (Research: nng-help)

CHAT AND BOTS
 8. Bot says it is a bot   (Rule: eu-ai-act)
 9. "Talk to a person" always available   (Research: nng-chatbots)
10. Chat history kept, transcript by email   (Practice)
11. Works when the person leaves and comes back   (Practice)

AFTER SENDING
12. Confirmation with a ticket number and when to expect a reply   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
13. Replies by email and in the app, threaded   (Practice)
14. Status of open requests   (Practice)
15. Rating after it is solved, short and optional   (Research: nng-surveys)

ACCESS
16. Chat works with screen readers and keyboard; new messages announced   (Standard: wcag-status, wcag-keyboard)
17. Phone not the only way   (Standard: wcag-consistent-help)
18. No time-outs that lose a typed message   (Standard: wcag-timing)

CHANGING EMAIL (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 8 items
Switching the email address an account signs in with.
Backing: 1 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ASKING
 1. Current password or fresh sign-in required   (Standard: owasp-auth)
 2. The new address entered with checks   (Standard: govuk-email)

CONFIRMING
 3. A link or code sent to the new address; the change only applies after it   (Standard: owasp-auth)
 4. A notice sent to the old address with a way to undo   (Standard: owasp-auth)

AFTER
 5. Sessions on other devices reviewed   (Standard: owasp-session)
 6. Marketing consent carried over only when right   (Rule: gdpr-7)

ERRORS
 7. Address already used: say so without leaking other accounts   (Standard: owasp-auth)

ACCESS
 8. Status announced on each step   (Standard: wcag-status)

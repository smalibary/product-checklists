EXPORTING DATA (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 8 items
Downloading a copy of your data.
Backing: 2 Rule, 4 Standard, 1 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ASKING
 1. Choose what to export and the format   (Practice)
 2. Personal data export available to every person   (Rule: gdpr-20, pdpl)

WAITING
 3. Large exports prepared in the background with a notice when ready   (Research: nng-progress)

GETTING IT
 4. Download link that expires and needs sign-in   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 5. Common, readable formats (CSV, JSON)   (Rule: gdpr-20)

SAFETY
 6. Fresh sign-in for full account exports   (Standard: owasp-session)
 7. The export logged   (Standard: owasp-logging)

ACCESS
 8. Status and errors announced   (Standard: wcag-status)

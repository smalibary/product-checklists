SETTING UP TWO-STEP SIGN-IN (FLOW) CHECKLIST: 9 items
Adding a second factor like an authenticator app, passkey or SMS code.
Backing: 0 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Options listed: passkey, authenticator app, then SMS as a fallback   (Standard: nist-63b-auth, owasp-mfa)

AUTHENTICATOR APP
 2. QR code plus a text key to type   (Standard: rfc-totp)
 3. Confirm with a code before turning it on   (Standard: owasp-mfa)

RECOVERY
 4. Recovery codes shown once, with copy and download   (Standard: owasp-mfa)
 5. The person confirms they saved them   (Practice)

AFTER
 6. Email confirmation that two-step is on   (Standard: owasp-mfa)
 7. Turning it off needs a fresh sign-in and a code   (Standard: owasp-mfa)

ACCESS
 8. Code fields allow paste and autofill   (Standard: wcag-auth, webdev-otp)
 9. No time pressure on entering codes beyond the code's life   (Standard: wcag-timing)

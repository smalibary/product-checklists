DATA AND CHART ANIMATION CHECKLIST: 8 items
Animating charts, numbers and lists so changes are easy to follow.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 3 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHARTS
 1. Animate from old values to new so people see what changed   (Research: heer-transitions)
 2. One kind of change at a time (axes, then data)   (Research: heer-transitions)
 3. Short and simple; no bouncing bars   (Standard: hig-charts)

NUMBERS
 4. Counting-up numbers only on first view and short   (Practice)
 5. Final value readable immediately by screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)

LISTS
 6. Added, removed and reordered items move so people can track them   (Standard: m3-transitions, android-animation)
 7. Sorting animates once; filtering does not jump the page   (Research: nng-animation-usability)

ACCESS
 8. Reduced motion shows the final state directly   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility

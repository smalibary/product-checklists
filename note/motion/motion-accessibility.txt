MOTION ACCESSIBILITY CHECKLIST: 9 items
Making sure animation never makes anyone ill or locks them out.
Backing: 2 Rule, 6 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SETTINGS
 1. Reduce motion setting respected on web, iPhone and Android   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion, hig-accessibility)
 2. Reduced motion swaps movement and zoom for fades, not nothing at all   (Standard: wcag-c39, hig-motion)
 3. An in-app switch to turn animation down too   (Practice)

HARM
 4. Large zooms, spins and parallax avoided; they trigger dizziness   (Standard: wcag-animation, a11yproject-vestibular)
 5. Nothing flashes more than three times a second   (Rule: wcag-flash)
 6. Auto-playing motion over 5 seconds can be paused, stopped or hidden   (Rule: wcag-pause)

USE
 7. No information only available while animating   (Standard: wcag-c39)
 8. Focus is never lost or moved by an animation   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
 9. Time limits never shortened by animation   (Standard: wcag-timing)

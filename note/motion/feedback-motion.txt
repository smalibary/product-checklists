FEEDBACK MOTION CHECKLIST: 8 items
Animation that says success, error or "look here".
Backing: 0 Rule, 3 Standard, 3 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SUCCESS AND ERROR
 1. Success shown briefly with a tick or colour change, then gets out of the way   (Research: nng-microinteractions)
 2. A small shake on a wrong code or password, with a text message too   (Practice)
 3. Errors never shown by motion or colour alone   (Standard: wcag-color, wcag-error)

ATTENTION
 4. New items or changes highlighted once, briefly   (Research: nng-animation-usability)
 5. Badges and dots animate in once, not forever   (Practice)
 6. Nothing blinks; anything moving over 5 seconds can be stopped   (Standard: wcag-pause)

READING ALOUD
 7. The message behind the motion is announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)

EVIDENCE
 8. Animation helps only when it shows change people need to follow   (Research: tversky-animation, gonzalez-animation)

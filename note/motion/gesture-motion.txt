GESTURE MOTION CHECKLIST: 8 items
Movement that follows the finger: dragging, swiping, pulling, flicking.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FOLLOWING
 1. The item stays under the finger while dragging   (Standard: hig-gestures, m3-gestures)
 2. Gestures can be stopped and reversed midway   (Standard: hig-motion, apple-springs)
 3. Released items keep their speed and settle with a spring   (Standard: apple-springs, android-animation)

FEEDBACK
 4. Edges resist with a soft rubber band   (Standard: hig-motion)
 5. Swipe actions show what will happen before release   (Research: nng-swipe)
 6. Pull to refresh shows progress while pulling   (Standard: m3-progress)

ALTERNATIVES
 7. Every gesture has a button or menu alternative   (Standard: wcag-gestures, wcag-dragging)

BACK
 8. Back gesture animates the current screen away and shows the previous one   (Standard: android-predictive-back, hig-gestures)

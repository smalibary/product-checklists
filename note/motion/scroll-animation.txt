SCROLL ANIMATION CHECKLIST: 8 items
Things that move as the page scrolls: reveals, sticky headers, parallax.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

USE
 1. Scroll effects support reading, never hide content until it animates   (Research: nng-animation-usability)
 2. Content readable and usable if the animation never runs   (Standard: wcag-c39)
 3. Scroll-linked effects built with scroll-driven animation, not scroll events   (Standard: mdn-scroll-driven)

LIMITS
 4. No scroll hijacking: the page scrolls at the person's speed   (Research: nng-animation-usability)
 5. Parallax small and turned off for reduced motion   (Standard: wcag-animation, a11yproject-vestibular)
 6. Sticky headers shrink smoothly and do not cover focused items   (Standard: wcag-obscured)

PERFORMANCE
 7. Only transform and opacity animated during scroll   (Standard: webdev-anim-perf)
 8. No layout shift as items reveal   (Standard: webdev-cls)

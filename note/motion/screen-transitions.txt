SCREEN TRANSITIONS CHECKLIST: 10 items
Moving between pages and screens so people keep their place.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING THE PATTERN
 1. Container transform when an item grows into its detail screen   (Standard: m3-transitions)
 2. Shared axis for steps and tabs side by side   (Standard: m3-transitions)  -> see design-system/tabs
 3. Fade through for unrelated screens   (Standard: m3-transitions)
 4. Push and pop direction matches going deeper and coming back   (Standard: hig-motion)

SHARED ELEMENTS
 5. The same picture or title travels from list to detail   (Standard: android-shared-element, mdn-view-transitions)

WEB
 6. View transitions between pages where supported, with a plain fallback   (Standard: mdn-view-transitions, chrome-view-transitions)
 7. Entry animations for newly shown elements set with starting styles   (Standard: mdn-starting-style)

BACK
 8. Back gesture previews the screen underneath   (Standard: android-predictive-back)  -> see motion/gesture-motion
 9. Right-to-left languages flip the direction   (Standard: hig-right-to-left)  -> see design-system/rtl-localisation

EVIDENCE
10. Animated transitions help people track what changed   (Research: heer-transitions, chang-ungar)

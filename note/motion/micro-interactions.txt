MICRO-INTERACTIONS CHECKLIST: 9 items
The small animated responses to a single action: press, toggle, like, copy.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PARTS
 1. A trigger, a response, rules and an end state, all designed   (Research: nng-microinteractions)
 2. Response starts within 100 ms of the action   (Research: nng-response)

COMMON ONES
 3. Button press shows a pressed state   (Standard: m3-buttons, hig-buttons)
 4. Toggles slide and change colour together   (Standard: m3-switch, hig-toggles)
 5. Like or favourite gives a short, clear reaction   (Research: nng-microinteractions)
 6. Copy shows "Copied" briefly next to the button   (Practice)
 7. Hover and focus states animate, not jump   (Standard: mdn-transitions)

RESTRAINT
 8. Delight only where it helps; never slows a repeated task   (Research: nng-animation-usability)
 9. Focus state always visible, even mid-animation   (Standard: wcag-focus)

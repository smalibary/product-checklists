LOADING MOTION CHECKLIST: 9 items
Animation that shows work in progress and makes waiting clearer.
Backing: 0 Rule, 3 Standard, 5 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Under 1 second: no indicator   (Research: nng-progress)
 2. 1 to 10 seconds: spinner or skeleton   (Research: nng-progress, nng-skeleton)
 3. Over 10 seconds: a progress bar with percent or time left   (Research: nng-progress)  -> see design-system/progress

SKELETONS
 4. Shapes match the real layout, so nothing jumps when content arrives   (Research: nng-skeleton, webdev-cls)
 5. A gentle shimmer, paused for reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility

PROGRESS
 6. Progress bars move steadily, never backwards   (Research: nng-progress)  -> see design-system/progress
 7. Determinate when the length is known   (Standard: m3-progress, hig-progress-indicators)

ACCESS
 8. Busy state announced to screen readers   (Standard: wcag-status)
 9. Spinners stop when done; nothing spins forever on error   (Practice)

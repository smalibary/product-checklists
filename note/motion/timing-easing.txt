TIMING AND EASING CHECKLIST: 10 items
How long each animation takes and how it speeds up and slows down.
Backing: 0 Rule, 8 Standard, 1 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DURATION
 1. Small changes (a toggle, a hover) about 100 to 200 ms   (Standard: m3-easing, nng-animation-duration)
 2. Medium changes (a menu, a card opening) about 200 to 300 ms   (Standard: m3-easing)
 3. Big changes (a full screen) up to about 400 to 500 ms, never longer without a reason   (Standard: m3-easing, carbon-motion)
 4. Things leaving faster than things arriving   (Standard: m3-easing, fluent-motion)
 5. Bigger distance, slightly longer time   (Standard: m3-easing, carbon-motion)

EASING
 6. Ease-out for things entering, ease-in for things leaving, standard for moves on screen   (Standard: m3-easing, mdn-easing)
 7. No linear easing for movement, except progress and spinners   (Standard: mdn-easing, carbon-motion)  -> see motion/loading-motion
 8. A small named set of curves used everywhere   (Standard: m3-easing, fluent-motion)

TESTING
 9. Timing checked slowed down, then at real speed   (Practice)
10. Repeated animations felt after the 20th time, not just the first   (Research: nng-animation-duration)

MOTION SYSTEM CHECKLIST: 9 items
Writing motion down so every screen moves the same way.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

TOKENS
 1. Durations and easing curves stored as named tokens   (Standard: m3-easing, dtcg-tokens)
 2. A short list: a few durations, a few curves   (Standard: carbon-motion, fluent-motion)

PATTERNS
 3. One named transition for each job (open, close, push, expand)   (Standard: m3-transitions)
 4. Written rules for what moves and what never moves   (Standard: carbon-motion)

HANDOFF
 5. Each animation specified with trigger, property, duration, curve and delay   (Practice)
 6. A recording or live demo beside each spec   (Standard: storybook)

PRINCIPLES
 7. Two or three motion principles written in plain words   (Standard: fluent-motion, carbon-motion)
 8. Reduced motion version defined for every pattern   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)

REVIEW
 9. Motion reviewed in design review, like colour and type   (Practice)

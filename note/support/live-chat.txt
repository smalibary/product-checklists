LIVE CHAT CHECKLIST: 10 items
Real-time help in the product, by a person or a bot.
Backing: 0 Rule, 1 Standard, 9 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

FINDING IT
 1. Labelled "Chat", in the usual bottom corner   (Research: nng-chat)
 2. Not the only way to get help   (Research: nng-chat, nng-contact)

STARTING
 3. Says if a bot or a person is answering   (Research: nng-chat, nng-chatbots)
 4. Expected wait shown as a time, not a queue number   (Research: nng-chat)
 5. Opening hours shown when nobody is there, with another way to reach out   (Research: nng-chat)

DURING
 6. Handover from bot to person keeps the conversation   (Research: nng-chat)
 7. Timestamps on messages   (Research: nng-chat)

AFTER
 8. A copy of the conversation offered   (Research: nng-chat)
 9. Can resume after a disconnect   (Research: nng-chat)

ACCESS
10. Works with keyboard and screen readers; new messages announced   (Standard: wcag-status, wcag-keyboard)

BUG REPORTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Making it easy to report problems and turning reports into fixes.
Backing: 0 Rule, 5 Standard, 0 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

REPORTING
 1. A form with steps, expected and actual result   (Standard: github-issue-forms)
 2. Screenshots and app version attached automatically where possible   (Practice)
 3. Duplicate check before submitting   (Practice)

HANDLING
 4. Each report labelled by severity and area   (Practice)
 5. Reporter told the status   (Practice)
 6. Security problems sent to a private channel, not public   (Standard: iso-29147, rfc-securitytxt)

FIXING
 7. A failing test written before the fix   (Standard: fowler-test-pyramid)
 8. Fix noted in the release notes   (Standard: keepachangelog)  -> see website/changelog

TRENDS
 9. Crash and error rates watched alongside reports   (Standard: sre-monitoring)

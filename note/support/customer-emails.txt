CUSTOMER EMAILS CHECKLIST: 9 items
The emails the product sends to people: receipts, alerts, account messages.
Backing: 2 Rule, 6 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Subject says what it is and what to do   (Research: nng-microcontent)
 2. Plain words; one main action   (Standard: govuk-email)
 3. Sender name people recognise   (Rule: can-spam)

KINDS
 4. Account and receipt emails kept separate from marketing   (Rule: can-spam)
 5. Security emails for sign-in, password and email changes   (Standard: owasp-auth)

DELIVERY
 6. SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up   (Standard: rfc-spf, rfc-dkim, rfc-dmarc)
 7. Bulk-sender rules met, one-click unsubscribe on marketing   (Standard: google-sender, rfc-unsubscribe)

ACCESS
 8. Works without images, readable on phones, in light and dark   (Standard: wcag-contrast)

SAFETY
 9. No passwords or full card numbers in email   (Standard: owasp-auth)

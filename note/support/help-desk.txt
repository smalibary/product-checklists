HELP DESK CHECKLIST: 10 items
Receiving, answering and tracking every support question.
Backing: 1 Rule, 6 Standard, 3 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

INTAKE
 1. Contact options easy to find from every screen   (Research: nng-contact, govuk-contact)
 2. A form that asks the right details up front   (Standard: govuk-question)
 3. Every request gets a reference and a reply time   (Standard: iso-10002)

HANDLING
 4. Response and fix time targets by priority   (Standard: zendesk-sla)
 5. Requests sorted and routed to the right team   (Standard: iso-10002)
 6. Customers told who is helping and what happens next   (Standard: iso-10002)

RECORDS
 7. History kept so nobody has to repeat themselves   (Research: nng-chat)
 8. Personal data limited and deleted on schedule   (Rule: gdpr-5)

LEARNING
 9. Common questions turned into help articles   (Research: nng-help)
10. Complaints counted and reviewed for root causes   (Standard: iso-10002)

USER RESEARCH CHECKLIST: 10 items
Learning what people need by watching and talking to them.
Backing: 2 Rule, 2 Standard, 6 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLANNING
 1. A clear question for each round of research   (Research: nng-interviews, govuk-research)
 2. Participants who match real users, including people with disabilities   (Standard: govuk-research)
 3. Consent and data handling explained before starting   (Rule: gdpr-7)

METHODS
 4. Interviews for needs and context   (Research: nng-interviews)
 5. Usability tests on real tasks with about five people per round   (Research: nng-usability-testing, nng-five-users)
 6. Test early drafts, not only finished screens   (Research: nng-usability-testing)

RUNNING
 7. Neutral questions; no leading or selling   (Research: nng-interviews)
 8. Notes and recordings stored securely and deleted on schedule   (Rule: gdpr-5)

SHARING
 9. Findings written up with the problems and their severity   (Research: nng-usability-testing)
10. Fixes tracked to the next round   (Standard: govuk-research)

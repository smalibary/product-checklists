PRIORITISING CHECKLIST: 9 items
Choosing what to build next in a way people can see and trust.
Backing: 0 Rule, 0 Standard, 5 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

METHOD
 1. One written method used every time   (Research: nng-prioritization)
 2. Scores for reach, impact, confidence and effort   (Practice: rice)
 3. Value against effort shown on a simple grid   (Research: nng-prioritization)

INPUTS
 4. Goals and roadmap themes come first   (Research: nng-roadmaps)  -> see product/roadmap
 5. Research and feedback, not the loudest voice   (Research: nng-feedback)  -> see product/user-feedback
 6. Risk, legal and security work counted, not crowded out   (Practice)

OUTPUT
 7. A ranked list with the reasons beside each item   (Research: nng-prioritization)
 8. What was not chosen, and why, written down   (Practice)

RHYTHM
 9. Re-ranked on a set rhythm   (Practice)

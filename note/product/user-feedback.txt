USER FEEDBACK CHECKLIST: 9 items
Hearing from people while they use the product, and acting on it.
Backing: 1 Rule, 2 Standard, 5 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHANNELS
 1. A feedback link on every page or screen   (Standard: govuk-feedback)
 2. Short in-product surveys at natural moments, never during a task   (Research: nng-surveys, nng-feedback)
 3. In-app review prompts only after a success, within store limits   (Rule: apple-review-prompt, play-in-app-review)  -> see launch/app-store-submission

ASKING WELL
 4. One or two questions, the rating scale kept the same   (Research: nng-surveys)
 5. An open box for "what would make this better"   (Standard: govuk-feedback)

USING IT
 6. Feedback read on a schedule and sorted into themes   (Research: nng-feedback)
 7. Paired with analytics and research before acting   (Research: nng-analytics)  -> see growth/analytics-tracking
 8. Results told back to the people who gave them   (Practice)

MEASURES
 9. A standard satisfaction or ease score tracked over time   (Research: sus, heart)

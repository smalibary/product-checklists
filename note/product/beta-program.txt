BETA PROGRAM CHECKLIST: 9 items
Letting a small group try new work early so problems show before everyone gets it.
Backing: 1 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SETUP
 1. Clear goal for the beta and when it ends   (Standard: govuk-beta)
 2. Testers chosen and invited; joining and leaving easy   (Standard: apple-testflight, play-testing)
 3. Testers told what is unfinished and what data is collected   (Rule: gdpr-13)

RUNNING
 4. An easy way to send feedback and screenshots   (Standard: apple-testflight)
 5. Crash and error reports collected   (Standard: sre-monitoring)
 6. Features behind flags so they can be turned off   (Standard: fowler-flags)

CLOSING
 7. Feedback answered and testers thanked   (Practice)
 8. Readiness checked against the goal before release   (Standard: govuk-beta)
 9. Staged rollout after the beta   (Standard: play-staged, apple-phased)

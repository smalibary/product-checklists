PRODUCT METRICS CHECKLIST: 9 items
The few numbers that show if the product is working for people and the business.
Backing: 0 Rule, 2 Standard, 3 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Goals, then signals, then measures for each   (Research: heart)
 2. A mix of happiness, engagement, adoption, retention and task success   (Research: heart)
 3. One north-star measure plus guard measures   (Practice)

STANDARD MEASURES
 4. Task completion rate, cost per transaction, satisfaction and digital take-up   (Standard: govuk-measure)
 5. Retention by sign-up group over time   (Practice)

QUALITY
 6. Each measure defined in writing: what counts, from where   (Standard: govuk-measure)
 7. Test and internal traffic left out   (Practice)

USE
 8. Reviewed on a rhythm with someone who owns each one   (Practice)
 9. Numbers explained with research, not guessed at   (Research: nng-analytics)

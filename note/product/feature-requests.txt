FEATURE REQUESTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Collecting, sorting and answering what people ask the product to do.
Backing: 1 Rule, 0 Standard, 3 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

COLLECTING
 1. One place to send ideas, linked from help and settings   (Research: nng-feedback)
 2. A form that asks what they are trying to do, not just what to build   (Research: nng-interviews)
 3. Duplicates merged; votes or counts kept   (Practice)

SORTING
 4. Each request tagged by problem, customer type and size   (Practice)
 5. Requests weighed against goals, not just votes   (Research: nng-prioritization)

ANSWERING
 6. Every request acknowledged   (Practice)
 7. Status shown (under review, planned, shipped, not planned)   (Practice)
 8. People told when what they asked for ships   (Practice)

RECORDS
 9. Personal data in requests handled under the privacy policy   (Rule: gdpr-5)

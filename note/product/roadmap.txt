ROADMAP CHECKLIST: 10 items
A living plan of the problems the product will solve, now, next and later.
Backing: 0 Rule, 0 Standard, 6 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Themes and problems, not a list of features with dates   (Research: nng-roadmaps)
 2. Columns by time horizon: now, next, later   (Research: nng-roadmaps)
 3. Each theme tied to a goal and how success is measured   (Research: nng-roadmaps, govuk-measure)
 4. Evidence behind each item (research, feedback, numbers)   (Research: nng-roadmaps)

MAKING IT
 5. Built from goals, research and feedback, then prioritised   (Research: nng-roadmaps, nng-prioritization)
 6. Priority method written down (for example reach, impact, confidence, effort)   (Practice: rice)

SHARING
 7. A public version promises less than the internal one   (Practice)
 8. Owners named for each theme   (Practice)

UPKEEP
 9. Reviewed on a set rhythm, weekly to monthly   (Research: nng-roadmaps)
10. Changes explained to the people who rely on it   (Practice)

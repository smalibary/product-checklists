RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENTS CHECKLIST: 8 items
Telling people what changed, in places they will see it.
Backing: 1 Rule, 5 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

RELEASE NOTES
 1. Written for people, grouped as added, changed, fixed, removed   (Standard: keepachangelog)
 2. Version number follows the agreed scheme   (Standard: semver)
 3. Breaking changes and what to do listed first   (Standard: semver, keepachangelog)

IN PRODUCT
 4. New features introduced in context, once, and easy to dismiss   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 5. No pop-ups that block the task   (Research: nng-modal)

OTHER CHANNELS
 6. Email only to people who opted in   (Rule: gdpr-7, can-spam)
 7. Store "what's new" text updated each release   (Standard: apple-product-page, play-listing)  -> see website/changelog

TIMING
 8. Announced after the rollout is complete   (Standard: sre-canary)

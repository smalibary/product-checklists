RETIRING A FEATURE CHECKLIST: 10 items
Removing a feature or product without leaving people stranded.
Backing: 3 Rule, 6 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DECIDING
 1. Usage and cost checked before deciding   (Standard: govuk-retire)
 2. Who relies on it, and on what, listed   (Standard: govuk-retire)

TELLING PEOPLE
 3. Announced well ahead with a date and the reason   (Standard: govuk-retire)
 4. In-product notices where the feature lives   (Practice)
 5. API users told with Deprecation and Sunset headers   (Standard: rfc-deprecation, rfc-sunset)

LEAVING
 6. A way to export their data   (Rule: gdpr-20)
 7. An alternative suggested, or help to move   (Standard: govuk-retire)
 8. Paid plans adjusted or refunded fairly   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)

AFTER
 9. Old addresses redirected   (Standard: gsc-redirects)
10. Code and data removed; data deleted on schedule   (Rule: gdpr-5)

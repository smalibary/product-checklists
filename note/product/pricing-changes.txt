PRICING CHANGES CHECKLIST: 9 items
Changing prices or plans fairly and within store and consumer rules.
Backing: 5 Rule, 0 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLANNING
 1. Reason for the change written down   (Practice)
 2. Who is affected, and by how much, worked out   (Practice)

TELLING PEOPLE
 3. Notice in advance, with the date and the new price   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-negative-option)
 4. Subscription price rises follow the store's consent rules   (Rule: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)
 5. Total price shown with all fees   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees)

CHOICES
 6. Easy cancel before the change   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
 7. Old price kept for a time where promised   (Practice)

DISPLAY
 8. Former prices shown honestly in sales   (Rule: eu-omnibus)
 9. Pricing page clear and comparable   (Research: nng-pricing)

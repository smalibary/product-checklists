PLAY STORE SUBMISSION CHECKLIST: 11 items
Getting an Android app published on Google Play.
Backing: 8 Rule, 3 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ACCOUNT AND BUILD
 1. Developer account verified; new personal accounts run a closed test first   (Rule: play-new-accounts)
 2. Target API level meets the current requirement   (Rule: play-target-api)
 3. Tested in internal and closed testing tracks   (Standard: play-testing)

LISTING
 4. Title, short and full description, icon, screenshots   (Rule: play-listing)
 5. Data safety form answered truthfully   (Rule: play-data-safety)
 6. Content rating questionnaire done   (Rule: play-policy)
 7. Privacy policy link working   (Rule: play-user-data)

RULES
 8. Account deletion offered in the app and on the web   (Rule: play-deletion)
 9. Digital goods through Play billing   (Rule: play-billing)
10. Permissions asked only when needed   (Standard: hig-privacy, webdev-permissions)

RELEASE
11. Staged rollout with crash rates watched   (Standard: play-staged)

DOMAIN AND EMAIL SETUP CHECKLIST: 9 items
The address, security headers and email records every product needs.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DOMAIN
 1. Domain registered with auto-renew and locked   (Practice)
 2. HTTPS everywhere, with HSTS   (Standard: mdn-hsts)  -> see operations/security-headers
 3. One main address; others redirect   (Standard: gsc-canonical, gsc-redirects)

SECURITY
 4. Security headers checked (CSP, framing, content type)   (Standard: mdn-csp, owasp-headers, mozilla-observatory)  -> see operations/security-headers
 5. security.txt published   (Standard: rfc-securitytxt)

EMAIL
 6. SPF, DKIM and DMARC records   (Standard: rfc-spf, rfc-dkim, rfc-dmarc)
 7. Sending reputation watched   (Standard: gmail-postmaster)
 8. Support and no-reply addresses chosen; replies go somewhere read   (Practice)

SEARCH
 9. Site verified in the search console, sitemap sent   (Standard: gsc-sitemaps)  -> see growth/technical-seo

LAUNCH DAY CHECKLIST: 11 items
The checklist for the day a product or big feature goes public.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 1 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BEFORE
 1. Rollback plan written and tested   (Standard: sre-release)
 2. Monitoring, alerts and on-call in place   (Standard: sre-monitoring, sre-alerting)  -> see operations/monitoring-alerts
 3. Load expected and tested   (Standard: sre-overload)
 4. Status page ready   (Practice)  -> see website/status

CONTENT
 5. Launch post, help articles and release notes ready   (Standard: keepachangelog)  -> see website/changelog
 6. Link previews set on the launch page   (Standard: ogp)  -> see growth/link-previews
 7. Launch page listed (Product Hunt, community sites) following each site's rules   (Practice: ph-launch)

DURING
 8. Rollout in stages, watching errors   (Standard: sre-canary)
 9. Someone answering questions in every channel   (Practice: ph-launch)

AFTER
10. Results against goals reviewed   (Research: heart)
11. Launch again with each meaningful update   (Practice: yc-launch)

APP STORE SUBMISSION CHECKLIST: 12 items
Getting an iPhone or iPad app through Apple's review.
Backing: 10 Rule, 2 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ACCOUNT AND BUILD
 1. Developer account, certificates and bundle id set   (Rule: apple-review)
 2. Tested with TestFlight on real devices   (Standard: apple-testflight)

LISTING
 3. Name, subtitle, description, keywords and screenshots   (Rule: apple-screenshots, apple-product-page)
 4. Privacy details answered truthfully   (Rule: apple-privacy-labels)
 5. Age rating set   (Rule: apple-age-rating)
 6. Privacy policy and support addresses working   (Rule: apple-review)  -> see website/privacy

REVIEW RULES
 7. Demo account and notes for the reviewer   (Rule: apple-review)
 8. Account deletion inside the app if sign-up exists   (Rule: apple-deletion)  -> see website/sign-up, flows/deleting-account
 9. Sign in with Apple offered when other social sign-ins are   (Rule: apple-4.8)
10. Purchases of digital goods through Apple's system   (Rule: apple-subscriptions)
11. Tracking permission asked before tracking   (Rule: apple-att)

RELEASE
12. Phased release for updates   (Standard: apple-phased)

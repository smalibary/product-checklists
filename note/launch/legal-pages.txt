LEGAL PAGES PACK CHECKLIST: 11 items
The pages a product must publish before it goes live.
Backing: 6 Rule, 2 Standard, 0 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

MUST-HAVES
 1. Privacy policy: what is collected, why, how long, who it is shared with   (Rule: gdpr-13, pdpl)
 2. Terms of use   (Practice)
 3. Cookie notice and choices where cookies need consent   (Rule: eprivacy)

WHEN THEY APPLY
 4. Refund and cancellation policy for paid products   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-negative-option)
 5. Accessibility statement   (Standard: w3c-a11y-statement)
 6. Imprint or company details in the EU   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 7. Security contact (security.txt)   (Standard: rfc-securitytxt)
 8. Community rules for user content   (Rule: eu-dsa)
 9. Notice of AI use where people talk to an AI   (Rule: eu-ai-act)

UPKEEP
10. Dated, with changes announced before they apply   (Practice)
11. Linked from the footer and the sign-up screen   (Practice)

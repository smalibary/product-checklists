DATES, TIMES AND MONEY CHECKLIST: 12 items
Storing and showing times, currencies and numbers correctly for every country.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 0 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

TIME
 1. Stored in UTC; shown in the person's time zone   (Standard: rfc-datetime, iana-tz)
 2. Time zones by name (Asia/Riyadh), not by fixed offset   (Standard: iana-tz)
 3. Text dates in RFC 3339 or ISO 8601   (Standard: rfc-datetime, iso-8601)
 4. Hijri and Gregorian calendars shown where people expect them   (Standard: cldr, mdn-intl)
 5. Daylight saving handled for countries that use it   (Standard: iana-tz)

MONEY
 6. Whole numbers in the smallest unit or exact decimals, never floating point   (Standard: iso-4217)
 7. The currency code stored with every amount   (Standard: iso-4217)
 8. Rounding rules stated and applied once   (Practice)
 9. Exchange rates stored with the time they were used   (Practice)

SHOWING
10. Numbers, dates and currency formatted by locale (Intl)   (Standard: mdn-intl, cldr)
11. Arabic-Indic or Western digits by the person's choice   (Standard: cldr)

TESTING
12. Tests at month ends, leap years and time-zone changes   (Practice)

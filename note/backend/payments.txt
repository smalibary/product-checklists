PAYMENTS (SERVER) CHECKLIST: 13 items
Taking money safely: payment intents, webhooks, refunds and records.
Backing: 2 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CARD DATA
 1. Card details collected by the payment provider; your servers never see them   (Standard: pci-dss)
 2. Card fields hosted by the provider keep you at the lightest PCI level   (Standard: pci-dss)

FLOW
 3. One payment intent per order, created on the server with the amount set by the server   (Standard: stripe-payment-intents)
 4. An idempotency key on every create call   (Standard: stripe-idempotency)
 5. Bank checks (3-D Secure) handled where required   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)
 6. The order marked paid only from a signed webhook, not from the browser   (Standard: stripe-webhooks)

MONEY
 7. Amounts as whole numbers in the smallest unit with a currency code   (Standard: iso-4217)
 8. VAT added and shown correctly; e-invoices where required   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)

AFTER PAYING
 9. Refunds through the provider, linked to the original payment   (Standard: stripe-refunds)
10. Disputes tracked with evidence and deadlines   (Standard: stripe-disputes)
11. Store subscriptions checked on the server with the store's notifications   (Standard: apple-server-notif, play-rtdn)

RECORDS
12. A ledger of every charge, refund and fee, matched to the provider each day   (Practice)
13. Logs never contain card numbers or full bank details   (Standard: pci-dss, owasp-logging)

CACHING CHECKLIST: 12 items
Keeping copies of answers so repeated requests are fast and cheap, without serving wrong data.
Backing: 0 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

HTTP CACHING
 1. Cache-Control on every response: public, private or no-store   (Standard: rfc-caching, mdn-cache-control)
 2. Personal pages marked private or no-store   (Standard: rfc-caching)
 3. ETag or Last-Modified so clients can ask "changed since?"   (Standard: mdn-etag)
 4. Static files with content hashes in their names, cached for a year   (Standard: mdn-cache-control)

APP CACHING
 5. Cache keys include everything that changes the answer (user, language, tenant)   (Practice)
 6. A time limit on every cached item   (Practice)
 7. Clear invalidation when the source changes   (Practice)
 8. Protection against many requests rebuilding the same item at once   (Practice)

SAFETY
 9. Never cache answers with another user's data under a shared key   (Standard: owasp-multitenant)
10. The app still works when the cache is empty or down   (Standard: sre-cascading)

COSTS
11. Expensive results (AI answers, outside lookups) cached   (Standard: aws-cost)

WATCHING
12. Hit rate and size measured   (Standard: sre-monitoring)

DATA PRIVACY (SERVER) CHECKLIST: 13 items
Handling personal data the way GDPR and Saudi PDPL require.
Backing: 13 Rule, 0 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

BASICS
 1. A list of what personal data is kept, why, where and for how long   (Rule: gdpr-30, pdpl)
 2. A legal reason for each use   (Rule: gdpr-6, pdpl)
 3. Collect only what is needed   (Rule: gdpr-5)
 4. Privacy built into the design from the start   (Rule: gdpr-25)

PEOPLE'S RIGHTS
 5. Access and a copy of their data   (Rule: gdpr-15, gdpr-20)
 6. Correction and deletion, including from backups within a stated time   (Rule: gdpr-17, pdpl)
 7. Objection to marketing and profiling   (Rule: gdpr-21)

SECURITY
 8. Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited by role   (Rule: gdpr-32)
 9. Breaches reported within 72 hours where required   (Rule: gdpr-33)

SUPPLIERS
10. A data processing agreement with each supplier   (Rule: gdpr-28)
11. Transfers outside the country only under the rules   (Rule: gdpr-44, pdpl)

RISK
12. An impact assessment for high-risk processing   (Rule: gdpr-35)
13. Saudi cybersecurity controls checked where they apply   (Rule: nca-ecc)

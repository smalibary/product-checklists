SEARCH (SERVER) CHECKLIST: 12 items
Finding records by words quickly and fairly.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

INDEX
 1. A full-text index rather than LIKE scans   (Standard: pg-fts)
 2. Arabic and English handled: language rules, letter forms and diacritics   (Standard: pg-fts, unicode-normalization)
 3. The index updated when records change   (Standard: pg-fts)

QUERIES
 4. Typos forgiven where possible   (Practice)
 5. Results ranked by match and then by freshness or popularity   (Standard: pg-fts)
 6. Filters use indexes too   (Standard: pg-indexes)
 7. Results paged with a maximum   (Standard: google-aip-pagination)

SAFETY
 8. Each person sees only results they may open   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 9. Query length and complexity limited   (Standard: owasp-dos)
10. Search input passed as a parameter, never joined into a query   (Standard: owasp-sql)

QUALITY
11. Searches with no results logged to improve the index   (Practice)
12. Slow searches measured   (Standard: pg-explain)

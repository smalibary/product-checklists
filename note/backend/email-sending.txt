EMAIL SENDING CHECKLIST: 13 items
Sending account and product emails that arrive in the inbox and follow the law.
Backing: 4 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DOMAIN
 1. SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for the sending domain   (Standard: rfc-spf, rfc-dkim, rfc-dmarc)
 2. Gmail and Yahoo rules met: alignment, low spam rate, one-click unsubscribe for bulk mail   (Rule: google-sender, yahoo-sender)  -> see flows/unsubscribing-from-emails
 3. Account emails and marketing emails sent from separate addresses or streams   (Standard: google-sender)  -> see growth/email-marketing

CONTENT
 4. A plain-text version with every HTML email   (Practice)
 5. The sender's name and postal address in marketing emails   (Rule: can-spam)  -> see growth/email-marketing

CONSENT
 6. Marketing only with consent where the law needs it   (Rule: eprivacy, gdpr-6)
 7. One-click unsubscribe header and a working link, honoured quickly   (Rule: rfc-unsubscribe, can-spam)  -> see flows/unsubscribing-from-emails

DELIVERY
 8. Sending through a queue with retries   (Standard: aws-backoff)  -> see backend/background-jobs
 9. Bounces and complaints handled: stop sending to bad addresses   (Standard: google-sender)
10. Daily caps per user and in total   (Standard: aws-cost)

SAFETY
11. No secrets or full card numbers in emails   (Standard: pci-dss)  -> see operations/secrets
12. Links in emails expire when they sign someone in   (Standard: owasp-forgot)

WATCHING
13. Delivery, bounce and complaint rates measured   (Standard: google-sender)

NOTIFICATION DELIVERY CHECKLIST: 12 items
Getting push, email and in-app messages to the right person once, at the right time.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHANNELS
 1. Push through FCM and APNs with tokens kept per device   (Standard: fcm, apns)
 2. Dead device tokens removed when the service reports them   (Standard: fcm, apns)
 3. Each person's channel choices stored and respected   (Standard: nng-notifications)

SENDING
 4. One place decides what to send on which channel   (Practice)
 5. Sent from a queue with retries; never twice for one event   (Standard: aws-idempotent)
 6. Quiet hours and the person's time zone respected   (Standard: iana-tz)
 7. Similar messages grouped instead of many in a row   (Standard: nng-notifications)

CONTENT
 8. No private details on lock-screen text unless the person allows it   (Standard: hig-notifications)
 9. A link that opens the right screen   (Standard: android-app-links, apple-universal-links)

LIMITS
10. Caps per person per day   (Practice)
11. SMS and email counted against daily budgets   (Standard: aws-cost)

WATCHING
12. Delivery, open and failure rates measured   (Standard: sre-monitoring)

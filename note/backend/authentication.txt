AUTHENTICATION (SERVER) CHECKLIST: 14 items
How the server checks who someone is: passwords, codes, passkeys and sign-in limits.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PASSWORDS
 1. At least 8 characters (15 when it is the only factor), up to at least 64, all characters allowed   (Standard: nist-63b)
 2. Checked against lists of leaked and common passwords   (Standard: nist-63b)
 3. No forced rules about symbols and no forced regular changes   (Standard: nist-63b)
 4. Stored with a slow, salted hash (Argon2id, scrypt or bcrypt)   (Standard: owasp-passwords)

OTHER WAYS IN
 5. Passkeys offered where supported   (Standard: webauthn, passkeys-dev)
 6. A second factor available, required for admins   (Standard: owasp-mfa, nist-63b-auth)
 7. One-time codes expire quickly, work once and are limited in tries   (Standard: rfc-totp, owasp-mfa)
 8. Sign in with Apple or Google through standard OAuth with PKCE   (Standard: rfc-oauth-native, rfc-pkce)

ATTACKS
 9. Attempts limited per account and per network address, with growing delays   (Standard: owasp-auth)
10. The same answer whether or not the account exists   (Standard: owasp-auth)
11. Password reset links random, single-use and short-lived   (Standard: owasp-forgot)

RECORDS
12. Sign-ins, failures, resets and factor changes logged without the secrets   (Standard: owasp-logging)
13. The user told by email about a new device or a changed password   (Standard: owasp-auth)

CHECKS
14. The design checked against OWASP ASVS level 2   (Standard: owasp-asvs)

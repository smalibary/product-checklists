AUDIT TRAIL CHECKLIST: 11 items
A permanent record of who did what, for security, support and the law.
Backing: 1 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHAT
 1. Sign-ins, permission changes, data exports, deletes and admin actions recorded   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 2. Each entry: who, what, which record, when, from where, and the result   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 3. Before and after values for important changes   (Practice)

INTEGRITY
 4. Entries cannot be changed or deleted by the app   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 5. Stored apart from normal data, with its own access rules   (Standard: owasp-logging)
 6. Times in UTC from a synced clock   (Standard: rfc-datetime)

PRIVACY
 7. No passwords, tokens or full card numbers in entries   (Standard: owasp-logging, pci-dss)
 8. Kept for a stated period, then removed   (Rule: gdpr-5)

USE
 9. Customers can see their own account's trail where it helps   (Practice)
10. Searchable by user, record and time   (Practice)
11. Alerts on suspicious patterns   (Standard: owasp-logging)

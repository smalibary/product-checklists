GESTURE NAVIGATION CHECKLIST: 17 items
Moving around an app with swipes and other gestures, alongside the system's own gestures.
Backing: 0 Rule, 13 Standard, 4 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SYSTEM GESTURES COME FIRST
 1. Do not take over the system's edge swipes (home, back, notifications)   (Standard: hig-gestures, android-back)
 2. Android back gesture: use the predictive back so people see where they go   (Standard: android-back)
 3. iOS swipe from the left edge goes back in navigation stacks; keep it working   (Standard: hig-gestures)
 4. Content drawn edge to edge, with controls kept away from gesture areas   (Standard: android-edge, hig-layout)

APP GESTURES
 5. Use standard gestures with their usual meaning (tap, swipe, pinch, long press, pull to refresh)   (Standard: hig-gestures, m3-gestures)  -> see mobile/pull-to-refresh
 6. Swipe actions on list rows (archive, delete) shown with colour, icon and text   (Research: nng-swipe)
 7. Every gesture has a visible alternative (button or menu)   (Standard: wcag-dragging, wcag-gestures)
 8. Gestures that need two fingers or a path have a single-tap way too   (Standard: wcag-gestures)
 9. Destructive swipes can be undone   (Research: nng-control)

TEACHING
10. Hint at hidden gestures (a peek of the swipe action, a handle on sheets)   (Research: nng-swipe)
11. No reliance on gestures nobody can discover   (Research: nng-swipe)

FEEL
12. Movement follows the finger and can be cancelled mid-way   (Standard: hig-gestures)
13. Light haptics confirm a completed gesture, where supported   (Standard: hig-feedback)
14. Reduced motion respected for the animations   (Standard: hig-accessibility)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility, motion/motion-system

ACCESS
15. Works with VoiceOver and TalkBack (custom actions for swipe actions)   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
16. Accidental activation avoided (actions on release, not on touch down)   (Standard: wcag-cancel)
17. Shake or tilt never the only way to do something   (Standard: wcag-motion-act)

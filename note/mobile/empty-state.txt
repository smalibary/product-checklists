EMPTY STATE (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 6 items
What a phone screen shows when there is nothing in it yet.
Backing: 0 Rule, 2 Standard, 3 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Says why it is empty and what to do   (Research: nng-empty)
 2. One main action button   (Research: nng-empty)
 3. A small picture only if it helps, not big blocks of art   (Practice)

KINDS
 4. First use, no results, cleared and error states each handled   (Research: nng-empty)

LAYOUT
 5. Fits small screens and large text without scrolling far   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

ACCESS
 6. Pictures marked as decorative or described   (Standard: wcag-nontext)

SOURCES
- nng-empty: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/
- wcag-reflow: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow
- wcag-nontext: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-content

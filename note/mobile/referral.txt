REFERRAL (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 15 items
Inviting others with a personal link or code, where both sides may get a reward.
Backing: 2 Rule, 7 Standard, 0 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE OFFER
 1. What both sides get, in one line ("You both get 25 SAR")   (Practice)
 2. Conditions short and clear (when the reward comes, limits, expiry) with full terms linked   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)
 3. Reward shown in the person's currency   (Practice)

SHARING
 4. Personal link and code, with Copy (and a confirmation)   (Practice)
 5. System share sheet so people choose the app themselves   (Standard: hig-share, android-sharesheet)
 6. Ready message they can edit; no messages sent without their action   (Standard: apple-review)
 7. Contacts access only if they choose "pick from contacts", with a reason   (Standard: hig-privacy, android-permissions)

TRACKING
 8. List of invited people and the state of each (joined, rewarded)   (Practice)
 9. Total earned and where to use it   (Practice)

STORE RULES
10. Rewards in digital credits follow store payment rules   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)
11. No rewards for app reviews or ratings   (Standard: apple-review)

FAIRNESS
12. Fraud checks without blocking real users   (Practice)
13. Referred person told they came through a referral   (Rule: ftc-endorsements)

ACCESS
14. Code readable by screen readers letter by letter   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
15. Copy and share buttons named; at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

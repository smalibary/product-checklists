SEARCH (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 20 items
Searching inside a phone app: the search field, suggestions, results and filters.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 7 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

GETTING THERE
 1. Search in a tab, or a search field at the top of the main list   (Standard: hig-search, m3-search)
 2. Tapping it opens a focused search screen with the keyboard up   (Standard: m3-search)
 3. Pull-down reveals search on iOS lists where suited   (Standard: hig-search)

BEFORE TYPING
 4. Recent searches, with remove and clear all   (Research: nng-search)
 5. Popular or suggested searches   (Practice)
 6. Categories to browse   (Practice)

TYPING
 7. Suggestions after a character or two   (Research: baymard-autocomplete)
 8. Typo-tolerant, handles Arabic letter forms and diacritics   (Practice)
 9. Search key on the keyboard   (Standard: mdn-inputmode)
10. Clear button and Cancel (iOS) or back arrow (Android)   (Standard: hig-search, m3-search)
11. Voice search where it helps   (Practice)

RESULTS
12. Count of results, grouped by type if mixed   (Research: nng-search)
13. Matched words highlighted with weight, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
14. Filters in a sheet or chips above results, with counts   (Research: baymard-filters)
15. Sort choice   (Research: nng-search)
16. Infinite scroll keeps position when coming back   (Research: nng-infinite)

NO RESULTS
17. Say so, show the words, suggest spelling fixes and other searches   (Research: nng-empty)

ACCESS
18. Field has a name ("Search products")   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
19. Number of results announced   (Standard: wcag-status)
20. Large text supported; results rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

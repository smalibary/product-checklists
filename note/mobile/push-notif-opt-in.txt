PUSH NOTIFICATION OPT-IN CHECKLIST: 16 items
Asking a phone user for permission to send push notifications, and handling yes, no and later.
Backing: 2 Rule, 9 Standard, 3 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

TIMING
 1. Ask when the person has a reason to want alerts (after ordering: "Get delivery updates?"), not at first launch   (Standard: hig-notifications, webdev-permissions)
 2. A pre-permission screen in the app's own words first, then the system prompt only if they say yes   (Research: nng-permissions)
 3. The system prompt on iOS can be shown only once; a "Not now" on the pre-screen keeps that chance   (Standard: hig-notifications)
 4. Android 13 and later need the runtime permission too   (Standard: android-notif-perm)

THE PRE-PERMISSION SCREEN
 5. Says what kinds of alerts and how often ("Only when your order moves")   (Research: nng-permissions)
 6. One clear yes button and an equal "Not now"   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
 7. An example of a notification   (Practice)  -> see mobile/in-app-notifications

AFTER THE ANSWER
 8. Yes: confirm and offer choices of topics in settings   (Practice)  -> see mobile/settings
 9. No: the app works fully without it   (Standard: apple-review)
10. Later: ask again only at a new, relevant moment, not on every launch   (Research: nng-permissions)
11. If turned off in system settings, show how to turn it back on, with a button to the settings page   (Standard: hig-notifications)  -> see mobile/settings

IOS OPTIONS
12. Provisional authorization: quiet notifications delivered without asking, so the person can decide later   (Standard: hig-notifications)  -> see mobile/in-app-notifications
13. Time-sensitive and critical alerts only for real needs   (Standard: hig-notifications)

RULES
14. Push not used for ads unless the person opted in to marketing   (Standard: apple-review)
15. Marketing consent separate from service alerts   (Rule: gdpr-7)
16. Notification channels on Android so people can mute kinds separately   (Standard: android-notif-perm)  -> see mobile/in-app-notifications

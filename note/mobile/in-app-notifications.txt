IN-APP NOTIFICATIONS CHECKLIST: 16 items
Messages shown inside the phone app while it is open: a notifications list, banners at the top, and badges.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 4 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

THE LIST
 1. A notifications screen reached from a bell or a tab, newest first   (Practice)
 2. Each item: who, what, when, with a photo or icon   (Practice)
 3. Unread marked by a dot and bold, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
 4. Tap opens the right screen and marks it read   (Practice)
 5. Swipe to delete or mark read, with buttons too   (Research: nng-swipe, wcag-dragging)
 6. Mark all as read   (Practice)
 7. Empty state: "You're all caught up"   (Research: nng-empty)

BANNERS WHILE THE APP IS OPEN
 8. A short banner at the top for important new things, which goes away by itself   (Standard: hig-notifications)
 9. Does not cover the main action or the keyboard   (Practice)
10. Tap opens it; swipe up dismisses   (Standard: hig-gestures)
11. Not shown for the conversation the person is already in   (Practice)

VOLUME AND CONTROL
12. Similar items combined ("Sara and 3 others liked...")   (Research: nng-notifications)
13. Settings to choose topics, reachable from the list   (Research: nng-notifications)
14. App icon badge count matches the unread count   (Standard: hig-notifications)

ACCESS
15. New banners announced by VoiceOver and TalkBack without moving focus   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
16. Banners stay long enough to read, or can be found again in the list   (Standard: wcag-timing)

ACCOUNT (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 16 items
The account area of a phone app: personal details, sign-in, security, data and deleting the account.
Backing: 3 Rule, 9 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DETAILS
 1. Name, email, phone, each editable with its own screen   (Practice)
 2. Changing email or phone confirmed by a code to the new one   (Standard: owasp-auth)
 3. Current password or Face ID asked before sensitive changes   (Standard: owasp-auth)

SIGN-IN AND SECURITY
 4. Change password; add a passkey   (Standard: passkeys-dev)
 5. Two-step check on and off, with backup codes   (Standard: nist-63b)
 6. Signed-in devices with "Sign out" each and "Sign out everywhere"   (Standard: owasp-session)
 7. Linked accounts (Apple, Google) with unlink, if another way in remains   (Practice)

DATA
 8. Download my data   (Rule: gdpr-15, gdpr-20)
 9. Privacy choices (ads, tracking)   (Rule: gdpr-7, apple-att)

DELETE ACCOUNT
10. Account deletion possible inside the app if the app lets people create accounts   (Standard: apple-deletion, play-deletion)
11. A web link to delete the account too, for Google Play   (Standard: play-deletion)
12. Say what is deleted, what is kept by law and how long it takes   (Rule: gdpr-17)
13. Store subscriptions: remind to cancel in the store, with a link   (Standard: apple-deletion)
14. Confirm with password or typed word; never a one-tap delete   (Research: nng-confirm)

SIGN OUT
15. Clear Sign out at the bottom, with a confirmation   (Practice)

ACCESS
16. Rows named with their value   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

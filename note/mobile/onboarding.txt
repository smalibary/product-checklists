ONBOARDING (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 18 items
The first screens of a phone app after install, before the person starts using it.
Backing: 2 Rule, 10 Standard, 5 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

KEEP IT SHORT
 1. Get people into the app fast; onboarding as short as possible   (Standard: hig-onboarding)
 2. Skip button on every intro slide   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 3. Intro slides only if the app is new in kind; 3 at most   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 4. Teach in context (hints when a feature is first used) rather than up front   (Research: nng-onboarding)

ACCOUNT
 5. Let people look around before signing up where the app allows   (Standard: hig-onboarding)
 6. Sign up later, when needed (saving, buying)   (Standard: apple-review)
 7. Sign in with Apple offered if other social sign-ins are offered on iOS   (Standard: hig-sign-in-apple)

SETUP QUESTIONS
 8. A few questions only if the answers shape the experience (interests, goals)   (Practice)
 9. Each skippable with sensible defaults   (Research: nng-onboarding)
10. Progress shown   (Research: nng-progress)

PERMISSIONS
11. Ask one at a time, at the moment it is needed, with a pre-permission screen   (Standard: hig-privacy, webdev-permissions)
12. App works if the person says no   (Standard: apple-review)
13. App Tracking Transparency prompt only if the app tracks across apps   (Standard: apple-att)

LEGAL
14. Marketing and tracking consent separate, never pre-ticked   (Rule: gdpr-7)
15. Age checks for apps with age limits   (Rule: coppa)

ACCESS
16. Slides work with VoiceOver and TalkBack (swipe and buttons)   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
17. Animations respect reduced motion   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
18. Text readable at large sizes; buttons at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

FEEDBACK (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 13 items
Asking for ratings, reviews, bug reports and ideas inside a phone app.
Backing: 1 Rule, 5 Standard, 3 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STORE RATING
 1. Use the system review prompt (SKStoreReviewController / In-App Review API)   (Standard: apple-review-prompt, play-in-app-review)  -> see launch/app-store-submission
 2. Ask after a success moment, never on first launch or during a task   (Standard: apple-review-prompt)
 3. The system limits how often it shows (3 times a year on iOS); do not build your own star prompt that sends only happy users to the store   (Standard: apple-review-prompt, play-in-app-review)
 4. No rewards for ratings   (Standard: apple-review)

BUG REPORT AND IDEAS
 5. "Send feedback" in Settings and Help   (Practice)  -> see mobile/settings
 6. Short form: what happened, optional screenshot, optional email   (Research: nng-surveys)
 7. Screenshot shake or attach, with the chance to blur private parts   (Practice)
 8. Device, app version and system version attached automatically, with notice   (Rule: gdpr-13)
 9. Thank-you and what happens next   (Practice)

QUICK IN-APP SURVEYS
10. One question at a time, at most 2 or 3   (Research: nng-surveys)
11. Easy to close; not shown again soon after   (Research: nng-modal)

REPLIES
12. Answers come back in the app or by email; the person can follow their report   (Practice)

ACCESS
13. Ratings with buttons, not only swipes   (Standard: wcag-gestures)  -> see mobile/gesture-navigation

ACTIVITY FEED (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 18 items
A scrolling list of updates in a phone app: posts, events, or what people and things did.
Backing: 1 Rule, 6 Standard, 4 Research, 7 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ITEMS
 1. Each: who, what, when, with photo or icon and a short preview   (Practice)
 2. Times as "5 min ago" with the full date on tap or for screen readers   (Practice)
 3. Similar events grouped ("Sara and 4 others...")   (Research: nng-notifications)
 4. Tap opens the full item   (Practice)

LOADING
 5. Newest first; "New posts" pill at the top instead of jumping the list   (Research: nng-infinite)
 6. Pull to refresh   (Standard: hig-loading)
 7. Endless scroll with a loading row; position kept when coming back   (Research: nng-infinite)
 8. Skeleton rows on first load   (Research: nng-skeleton)
 9. Images loaded small first, videos play muted only when visible, if at all   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

CONTROL
10. Filters (all, mentions, following) as tabs or chips   (Practice)
11. Hide, mute, or report an item from its menu   (Standard: apple-review)
12. Chronological option where the order is ranked   (Practice)

HEALTHY USE
13. No tricks to keep scrolling (fake new items, autoplay chains)   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
14. "You're all caught up" at the end of new items   (Practice)

OFFLINE
15. Cached items shown with a notice   (Practice)

ACCESS
16. Each item a single readable unit with actions as custom actions   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
17. Autoplay can be turned off; reduced motion respected   (Standard: wcag-pause)
18. Large text supported; targets at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

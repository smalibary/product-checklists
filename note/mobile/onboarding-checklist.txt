ONBOARDING CHECKLIST CHECKLIST: 14 items
A short list of first tasks in a new account (add photo, invite a friend, turn on alerts) that ticks off as they are done.
Backing: 0 Rule, 3 Standard, 6 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. 3 to 6 tasks that lead to real value, most important first   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 2. Each task: short name, why it helps, and a button that goes straight to it   (Research: nng-onboarding)
 3. One task can already be done to start ("Account created")   (Research: nng-progress)
 4. Time to do each task, if more than a minute   (Practice)

PROGRESS
 5. "2 of 5 done" with a bar   (Research: nng-progress)
 6. Tasks tick themselves when done anywhere in the app   (Practice)
 7. A small reward or message at the end, then the checklist goes away   (Practice)

PLACEMENT
 8. On the home screen as a card, can be collapsed   (Practice)
 9. Can be hidden or skipped; reachable again from settings or help   (Research: nng-onboarding)
10. Never blocks using the app   (Research: nng-onboarding)

PERMISSIONS IN THE LIST
11. Notifications, contacts or location tasks open a pre-permission screen first   (Standard: hig-privacy, webdev-permissions)
12. Saying no does not leave the checklist stuck: the task can be skipped   (Practice)

ACCESS
13. Progress announced in words   (Standard: wcag-status)
14. Done shown by tick and word, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)

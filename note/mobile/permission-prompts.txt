PERMISSION PROMPTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Asking for camera, location, contacts or notifications at the right time.
Backing: 3 Rule, 5 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

TIMING
 1. Ask when the person uses the feature that needs it, not at launch   (Standard: android-permissions, hig-privacy)
 2. Explain why first, in the app, before the system prompt   (Standard: webdev-permissions, android-permissions)

ASKING
 3. The system prompt text (purpose string) is specific   (Rule: apple-5.1.1)
 4. Ask for the least access (approximate location, chosen photos)   (Standard: android-location, android-photo-picker)

AFTER A NO
 5. The app still works without it   (Rule: apple-5.1.1)
 6. A way to turn it on later, with a link to settings   (Standard: android-permissions)
 7. No repeated nagging   (Research: nng-permissions)

NOTIFICATIONS
 8. Android 13 and later need the notification permission   (Standard: android-notif-perm)  -> see mobile/in-app-notifications

TRACKING
 9. App Tracking Transparency before tracking on iPhone   (Rule: apple-att)

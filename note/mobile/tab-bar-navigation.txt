TAB BAR NAVIGATION CHECKLIST: 19 items
The bar at the bottom of a phone app with the main sections (also called a bottom navigation bar or dock).
Backing: 0 Rule, 16 Standard, 3 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. 3 to 5 tabs for the main top-level sections   (Standard: hig-tab-bars, m3-nav-bar)
 2. Each tab an icon and a short label, always shown   (Standard: hig-tab-bars, m3-nav-bar)
 3. Labels one word where possible   (Research: nng-mobile-nav)
 4. Tabs are places, not actions (no "New post" tab unless it is a clear exception)   (Standard: hig-tab-bars)
 5. "More" tab only if really needed   (Research: nng-mobile-nav)

LOOK
 6. Selected tab clear by filled icon, colour and label, not colour alone   (Standard: wcag-color, hig-tab-bars)
 7. Badges for counts on tabs   (Standard: m3-badges)
 8. Bar sits above the home indicator safe area   (Standard: hig-layout, android-edge)
 9. Visible on all top-level screens; hidden only in full-screen tasks   (Standard: hig-tab-bars)

BEHAVIOUR
10. Tapping a tab opens that section where the person left it   (Standard: hig-tab-bars)
11. Tapping the current tab again scrolls to top or goes back to its root   (Standard: hig-tab-bars)
12. No moving tabs around between screens   (Research: nng-consistency)
13. Each tab keeps its own navigation history   (Standard: hig-tab-bars)

PLATFORM
14. iOS: tab bar; Android: navigation bar (bottom) or rail on tablets   (Standard: hig-tab-bars, m3-nav-bar)
15. On tablets and large screens, a side rail or sidebar instead   (Standard: m3-nav-bar)

ACCESS
16. Each tab has a name and says it is selected ("Home, tab, 1 of 5, selected")   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
17. Targets at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)
18. Labels stay readable with large text (bigger labels shown on long press on iOS)   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
19. Order flips for right-to-left languages   (Standard: w3c-dir)

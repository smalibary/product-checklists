CART (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 18 items
The shopping cart screen of a phone app, before checkout.
Backing: 1 Rule, 5 Standard, 8 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

ITEMS
 1. Each item: photo, name, options (size, colour), price and quantity   (Research: baymard-cart)
 2. Quantity with minus and plus buttons; removing at zero asks or offers undo   (Research: baymard-cart)  -> see flows/undoing-an-action
 3. Remove and Save for later   (Research: baymard-cart)
 4. Out of stock or price changed items marked clearly at the top   (Research: baymard-cart)  -> see product/pricing-changes
 5. Tap item opens its product screen   (Practice)

TOTALS
 6. Subtotal, delivery, discounts, VAT and total, with delivery estimate   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights, ftc-junk-fees)
 7. Free delivery threshold progress ("Add 30 SAR for free delivery")   (Practice)
 8. Promo code field folded, not a big empty box   (Research: baymard-coupon)  -> see flows/entering-promo-code

ACTIONS
 9. Checkout button sticky at the bottom with the total   (Practice)  -> see mobile/checkout
10. Apple Pay or Google Pay express button where available   (Standard: hig-apple-pay)
11. Continue shopping link   (Research: baymard-cart)

KEEPING THE CART
12. Cart kept across sessions and devices when signed in   (Research: baymard-cart)  -> see backend/sessions
13. Cart badge on the tab or icon with the count   (Standard: m3-badges)  -> see design-system/badge
14. Empty cart: friendly message and suggestions   (Research: nng-empty)

FEEDBACK
15. Adding to cart confirms with a small message, haptic and badge change, without leaving the product   (Standard: hig-feedback)  -> see design-system/badge
16. Changes to quantity update the total at once   (Practice)

ACCESS
17. Quantity buttons named ("Increase quantity of Blue shirt")   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
18. Total changes announced   (Standard: wcag-status)

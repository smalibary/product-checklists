CAMERA CHECKLIST: 18 items
Taking photos, videos or scanning (documents, QR codes) inside a phone app.
Backing: 2 Rule, 12 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PERMISSION
 1. Ask for camera access only when the person taps to use it   (Standard: hig-privacy, android-permissions)
 2. A clear purpose string ("To scan your receipt")   (Standard: apple-usage)
 3. If refused: explain and offer a button to settings, plus another way (pick from photos, type the code)   (Standard: android-permissions)
 4. Use the system photo picker to choose photos without asking for full library access   (Standard: hig-privacy, android-photo-picker)

CAPTURE SCREEN
 5. Large shutter button reachable by thumb   (Standard: hig-camera)
 6. Flash, switch camera and close buttons with names   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
 7. Guides for scanning (a frame for documents, a square for QR)   (Practice)
 8. Auto-capture when a document or code is steady, with manual capture too   (Practice)
 9. Feedback on success (sound or haptic and a visual mark)   (Standard: hig-feedback)

AFTER CAPTURE
10. Preview with Retake and Use photo   (Standard: hig-camera)
11. Crop, rotate and simple fixes   (Practice)
12. Upload with progress; works again if the connection drops   (Research: nng-progress)

PRIVACY
13. Location in photos removed unless needed and stated   (Rule: gdpr-25)
14. Photos stored only where the person expects; say where   (Rule: gdpr-13)
15. Camera stops when leaving the screen; system indicator shows when it is on   (Standard: hig-privacy)

ACCESS
16. VoiceOver and TalkBack users get spoken hints ("Document found, hold still")   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
17. Alternatives for people who cannot use the camera (upload, type)   (Standard: wcag-gestures)
18. Works in both orientations where it makes sense   (Standard: wcag-orientation)

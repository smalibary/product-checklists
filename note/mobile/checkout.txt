CHECKOUT (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 22 items
Paying for physical goods or services in a phone app, from cart to confirmation.
Backing: 4 Rule, 11 Standard, 4 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STEPS
 1. As few screens as possible; guest checkout allowed   (Research: baymard-guest)
 2. Progress shown for multi-step checkout   (Research: nng-progress)
 3. Order summary can be expanded on each step, with the total always visible   (Research: baymard-abandon)
 4. Sticky bottom bar with the total and the main button   (Practice)

DELIVERY
 5. Address from saved addresses or autofill; map pin for places without clear addresses   (Standard: mdn-autocomplete)
 6. Delivery options with price and date   (Rule: eu-consumer-rights)
 7. Phone number with country code picker, numeric keyboard   (Standard: mdn-inputmode)

PAYMENT
 8. Apple Pay and Google Pay first where available   (Standard: hig-apple-pay, webdev-payment)
 9. Local methods (mada, STC Pay, cash on delivery) where they suit   (Practice)
10. Card fields from a certified provider, card scan with the camera   (Standard: pci-dss)
11. Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) opened inside the app   (Rule: psd2-sca, stripe-3ds)

STORE RULES
12. Physical goods and services may use any payment provider   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)
13. Digital goods and subscriptions must use in-app purchase (with regional exceptions)   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)

COSTS
14. All fees, delivery and tax shown before the final button   (Rule: ftc-junk-fees, eu-consumer-rights)
15. Promo code field quiet, not a big empty box   (Research: baymard-coupon)

SUBMITTING
16. Button names the amount ("Pay 245 SAR")   (Standard: wcag-prevent)
17. Busy state; no double charge   (Standard: owasp-transaction)
18. Declined payment explained, details kept   (Standard: wcag-error)

AFTER
19. Confirmation screen with order number and tracking   (Standard: govuk-confirm)
20. Receipt and VAT invoice by email   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)
21. Offer push alerts for delivery here (see Push Opt-in)   (Standard: hig-notifications)

ACCESS
22. Keyboard never hides the active field or the button   (Practice)

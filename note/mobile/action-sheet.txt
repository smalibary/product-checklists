ACTION SHEET CHECKLIST: 19 items
A list of actions that slides up from the bottom of the phone screen, about the thing just tapped.
Backing: 0 Rule, 17 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Choices about one item (Share, Edit, Delete) or confirming a destructive action   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)
 2. Android uses a bottom sheet or a menu for the same job   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets, m3-menus)
 3. Not for long content or forms (use a full sheet or new screen)   (Research: nng-bottom-sheet)
 4. Not for a simple yes/no that needs explanation (use an alert)   (Standard: hig-alerts)

CONTENT
 5. Optional title and message saying what the actions are about   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)
 6. Short action names, verbs first   (Standard: hig-writing)
 7. Destructive action styled red and placed at the top on iOS   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)
 8. A Cancel button at the bottom, separate from the rest   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)
 9. Few actions; long lists scroll   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)

BEHAVIOUR
10. Tap outside or swipe down closes it   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets)
11. Android back closes it   (Standard: android-back)
12. On iPad and large screens it shows as a popover next to what was tapped   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)
13. Destructive actions can be undone, or confirmed   (Research: nng-confirm)

LOOK
14. Grabber handle on draggable sheets   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets)
15. Dimmed background behind   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets)
16. Rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp tall   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

ACCESS
17. Focus moves into the sheet and returns after   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
18. Screen readers announce it as a menu or dialog with a title   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
19. Large text grows the rows instead of cutting text   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

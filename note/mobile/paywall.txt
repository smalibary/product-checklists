PAYWALL CHECKLIST: 20 items
The screen that asks people to subscribe or buy to unlock content or features in a phone app.
Backing: 4 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 6 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STORE RULES
 1. Digital content and features sold through Apple and Google in-app purchase (with regional exceptions)   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)
 2. The price, the billing period and what is included shown clearly near the buy button   (Standard: apple-review, play-subscriptions)
 3. Price per period is the most prominent price; any per-week or per-month breakdown is secondary   (Standard: apple-review)
 4. Free trial: length, what it turns into, and when the first charge happens   (Rule: apple-subscriptions, ftc-negative-option)
 5. Links to terms of use and privacy policy   (Standard: apple-review)
 6. Restore purchases button   (Standard: apple-review)

CONTENT
 7. What the person gets, in 3 to 5 short benefits   (Practice)
 8. Plans side by side (monthly, yearly) with the saving   (Practice)
 9. Social proof only if true and current   (Rule: ftc-reviews)
10. The screen is honest about what stays free   (Practice)

RESPECT
11. A visible way to close the paywall; no hidden or delayed close button   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns, apple-review)
12. Not shown on first launch before the app has shown value, unless the app is paid-only   (Practice)
13. No fake timers or "only today" prices that are not true   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns, eu-omnibus)
14. Not shown repeatedly after being closed   (Practice)

AFTER BUYING
15. Access unlocked at once, with a thank-you   (Practice)
16. Receipt from the store   (Standard: apple-subscriptions)
17. How to manage or cancel shown in settings   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)

ACCESS
18. Prices read clearly by screen readers with the currency   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
19. Close button has a name and is at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)
20. Large text supported without hiding the price or buttons   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

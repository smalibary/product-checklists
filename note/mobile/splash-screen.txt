SPLASH SCREEN CHECKLIST: 14 items
What shows while the app starts, until its first screen is ready.
Backing: 0 Rule, 12 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PLATFORM RULES
 1. iOS: the launch screen looks like the first screen without content, not a branded ad   (Standard: hig-launching)
 2. Android 12 and later: the system splash screen (icon on a colour) is shown for every app   (Standard: android-splash)
 3. Use the platform's splash tools; do not add a second splash screen after it   (Standard: android-splash)

CONTENT
 4. Only the app icon or logo on a plain background   (Standard: android-splash)
 5. No text that needs translating, no ads, no "Loading" messages   (Standard: hig-launching)
 6. Background colour matches the first screen, in light and dark mode   (Standard: hig-launching, hig-dark-mode)  -> see design-system/dark-mode

SPEED
 7. Shown as briefly as possible; start-up work moved to after the first screen   (Standard: android-splash)
 8. Kept up only while truly needed data loads, then hand off smoothly   (Standard: android-splash)
 9. The first screen comes back to where the person left off   (Standard: hig-launching)
10. Slow start-ups measured and fixed (cold start under about 2 seconds is a good aim)   (Standard: android-vitals)

MOTION
11. Optional short icon animation (under a second)   (Standard: android-splash)  -> see motion/motion-system
12. Reduced motion respected   (Standard: hig-accessibility)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility

EDGE CASES
13. Offline at start: show the app with cached content and a notice, not a stuck splash   (Practice)  -> see mobile/offline, backend/caching
14. Update required: a clear screen with a button to the store   (Practice)

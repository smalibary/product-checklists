LOGIN (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 18 items
Signing in to a phone app with password, passkey, code, biometrics or another account.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 2 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WAYS IN
 1. Passkeys offered where supported   (Standard: passkeys-dev)
 2. Sign in with Apple on iOS if other social sign-ins are offered   (Standard: hig-sign-in-apple)
 3. Google sign-in through the system Credential Manager on Android   (Standard: android-credential-manager)
 4. Phone number with SMS code, with the code autofilled from the message   (Standard: android-sms-retriever, apple-autofill)

FIELDS
 5. Email or phone field with the right keyboard and autofill type   (Standard: apple-autofill, mdn-autocomplete)
 6. Password field works with password managers (no blocked paste)   (Standard: nist-63b)
 7. Show password button   (Research: nng-password)
 8. Login button stays usable; errors after submit   (Standard: wcag-error)

REMEMBER
 9. Stay signed in by default on a personal phone   (Practice)
10. Face ID or fingerprint to unlock the app later, as an option   (Standard: hig-privacy, android-biometric)
11. Tokens kept in the Keychain or Keystore, not plain storage   (Standard: owasp-masvs)

ERRORS AND HELP
12. One general message for wrong email or password   (Standard: owasp-auth)
13. Forgot password link near the field   (Research: nng-password)
14. Too many tries: wait time shown, not a dead end   (Standard: owasp-auth)
15. Offline: say so, keep what was typed   (Practice)

SECURITY
16. Two-step check where risk is high   (Standard: nist-63b)
17. New device sign-in alert by email   (Standard: owasp-auth)

ACCESS
18. Large text supported; keyboard never covers the button   (Standard: hig-layout)

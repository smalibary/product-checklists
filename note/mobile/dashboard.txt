DASHBOARD (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 16 items
The overview screen in a phone app with key numbers, status and things to act on.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 5 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Top 2 to 4 numbers that matter most, large   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 2. Change from the last period with arrow and word, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
 3. Things needing action at the top (due, overdue, waiting)   (Research: nng-dashboards)
 4. Every card taps through to its detail   (Research: nng-dashboards)

LAYOUT
 5. One column of cards; a horizontal swipe row only for secondary items   (Practice)  -> see mobile/gesture-navigation
 6. Simple charts sized for a phone (sparklines, small bars)   (Research: nng-dashboards)  -> see design-system/charts
 7. Period choice (today, week, month) at the top   (Practice)
 8. Last updated time; pull to refresh   (Standard: hig-loading)  -> see mobile/pull-to-refresh

SPEED
 9. Cached data shown at once, then refreshed   (Practice)  -> see backend/caching
10. Cards load on their own with skeletons   (Research: nng-skeleton)  -> see design-system/skeleton
11. Works offline with cached numbers marked as old   (Practice)  -> see mobile/offline, backend/caching

PERSONAL
12. Choose and reorder cards   (Practice)
13. Home screen widgets for the key number (iOS and Android)   (Standard: hig-widgets)  -> see mobile/widgets

ACCESS
14. Charts have text summaries for screen readers   (Standard: wcag-nontext)  -> see design-system/charts, design-system/accessibility
15. Cards are headings or buttons with clear names   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
16. Large text reflows cards instead of cutting numbers   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

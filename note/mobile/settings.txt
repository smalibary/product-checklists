SETTINGS (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 20 items
The settings screen of a phone app: grouped lists of options the person can change.
Backing: 1 Rule, 14 Standard, 0 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STRUCTURE
 1. Grouped list with short section titles (Account, Notifications, Privacy, About)   (Standard: hig-settings, m3-lists)
 2. Most used first; rarely used in deeper screens   (Practice)
 3. Search in settings when there are many   (Practice)
 4. App settings in the app, not in the system Settings app (iOS)   (Standard: hig-settings)

ROWS
 5. Each row: name, current value, and a chevron or toggle   (Standard: hig-settings)
 6. Toggles take effect at once, no Save needed   (Standard: hig-toggles, nng-toggle)
 7. Choices with few options open a list with a tick   (Standard: hig-settings)
 8. Short help text under rows that need it   (Practice)
 9. Destructive rows (Log out, Delete account) at the bottom, styled red   (Standard: hig-action-sheets)

CONTENT
10. Notifications by kind   (Standard: android-notif-perm)
11. Language, with Arabic and right-to-left layout   (Standard: w3c-dir)
12. Appearance: system, light, dark   (Standard: hig-dark-mode)
13. Privacy, data download and account deletion   (Rule: gdpr-15, gdpr-17, apple-deletion)
14. App version and build at the bottom   (Practice)
15. Help, terms, privacy policy links   (Standard: apple-review)

BEHAVIOUR
16. Changes saved on the device and synced to the account   (Practice)
17. Settings that need the system (notifications off, location off) link to the system settings page   (Standard: android-permissions)

ACCESS
18. Toggles read their state ("Dark mode, switch, on")   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
19. Large text: rows grow and wrap, values move under names   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
20. Rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

INVITE (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 17 items
Inviting people into a shared space in a phone app (a team, household, group or document), not for a reward.
Backing: 1 Rule, 8 Standard, 0 Research, 8 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WAYS TO INVITE
 1. Share a link through the system share sheet   (Standard: hig-share, android-sharesheet)
 2. Type an email or phone number   (Practice)
 3. Pick from contacts only after the person chooses it, with a reason   (Standard: hig-privacy, android-permissions)
 4. QR code to scan in person   (Practice)

ROLES
 5. Choose what the invited person can do (view, edit, admin), with one-line explanations   (Practice)
 6. Default is the safest useful role   (Standard: owasp-authz)

INVITE LINK
 7. Expiry and "reset link" to stop an old link working   (Standard: owasp-authz)
 8. Approval by an admin optional for link joins   (Practice)

WAITING INVITES
 9. List of sent invites with state (waiting, accepted), resend and cancel   (Practice)
10. Accepted invites move to the members list   (Practice)

THE INVITED PERSON
11. Link opens the app (or the store, then the right screen after install)   (Standard: apple-universal-links, android-app-links)
12. Says who invited them and to what, before they join   (Practice)
13. Accept or decline   (Practice)

RESPECT
14. No messages sent to contacts without the person's action   (Standard: apple-review)
15. Contacts not uploaded or kept without consent   (Rule: gdpr-6)

ACCESS
16. Role choices as a list with radio buttons and names   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
17. Large text supported; buttons at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

CHAT (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 24 items
Messaging inside a phone app: the list of conversations and one conversation with its message box.
Backing: 0 Rule, 11 Standard, 3 Research, 10 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONVERSATION LIST
 1. Newest first: photo, name, last message, time, unread count   (Practice)
 2. Search across conversations   (Research: nng-search)
 3. Swipe for archive, mute, delete, with a menu too   (Research: nng-swipe, wcag-dragging)
 4. Pinned conversations at the top   (Practice)

CONVERSATION
 5. Messages grouped by sender and day; own messages on one side   (Practice)
 6. Status per message: sending, sent, delivered, read (with icons and words for screen readers)   (Standard: wcag-color)
 7. Failed message marked with Retry   (Standard: wcag-error)
 8. Typing indicator for the other person   (Practice)
 9. Opens at the last unread message; "jump to latest" button   (Practice)
10. Long press for reply, copy, react, forward, delete   (Standard: hig-gestures)

MESSAGE BOX
11. Grows with text; send button only active with content   (Practice)
12. Keyboard never covers the box or the last message   (Standard: hig-layout)
13. Attach photos, files, voice notes, location   (Practice)
14. Draft kept when leaving the conversation   (Practice)
15. Right-to-left and mixed Arabic and English text shown in the right direction per message   (Standard: w3c-dir)

MEDIA
16. Photos load as small previews first; tap opens full screen with zoom   (Practice)
17. Voice notes with length and a play bar   (Practice)
18. Upload progress and cancel   (Research: nng-progress)

SAFETY AND PRIVACY
19. Block and report from the conversation   (Standard: apple-review)
20. End-to-end encryption where private messages are expected; say so   (Standard: owasp-masvs)
21. Notifications can hide message text on the lock screen   (Standard: hig-notifications)

ACCESS
22. New messages announced politely   (Standard: wcag-status)
23. Each message read with sender and time   (Standard: hig-accessibility)
24. Large text supported; targets at least 44 pt / 48 dp   (Standard: hig-layout, m3-layout)

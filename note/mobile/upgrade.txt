UPGRADE (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 16 items
Moving from free to paid, or from one plan to a higher one, inside a phone app.
Backing: 4 Rule, 6 Standard, 2 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

MOMENTS
 1. Offer the upgrade when the person reaches a limit or a paid feature, saying what it unlocks   (Practice)
 2. A lock icon on paid features, with a short reason on tap   (Practice)
 3. Not interrupting a task in the middle   (Research: nng-modal)

PLAN SCREEN
 4. Current plan marked; higher plans with what is added   (Practice)
 5. Prices with period and currency from the store   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)
 6. Yearly saving shown honestly   (Rule: eu-omnibus)
 7. Free trial terms clear (length, price after, first charge date)   (Rule: ftc-negative-option, apple-subscriptions)

BUYING
 8. Through Apple or Google in-app purchase for digital features (with regional exceptions)   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)
 9. Upgrade within a subscription group takes effect at once on iOS; downgrade at renewal   (Standard: apple-subscriptions)
10. Google Play replacement modes (charge now, prorate) chosen on purpose   (Standard: play-subscriptions)

AFTER
11. Unlocked features available at once with a thank-you   (Practice)
12. Show what is new and where to find it   (Research: nng-onboarding)
13. Restore purchases for other devices   (Standard: apple-review)

RESPECT
14. Easy to close; no repeated offers right after "No thanks"   (Rule: ftc-dark-patterns)
15. Downgrade and cancel explained in settings   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)

ACCESS
16. Price and plan names read clearly   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

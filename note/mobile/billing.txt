BILLING (MOBILE) CHECKLIST: 16 items
Seeing and managing subscriptions and payments inside a phone app.
Backing: 3 Rule, 11 Standard, 0 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STORE SUBSCRIPTIONS
 1. Digital subscriptions bought through Apple or Google in-app purchase (with regional exceptions)   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)
 2. Current plan, price, renewal date and the store it was bought from   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)
 3. "Manage subscription" opens the store's own subscription page   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)
 4. Restore purchases button for people on a new phone   (Standard: apple-review)
 5. Upgrades and downgrades through the store, with the price change shown   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)  -> see mobile/upgrade, product/pricing-changes
 6. Free trial end date and the first charge shown clearly   (Rule: apple-subscriptions, ftc-negative-option)

BOUGHT ELSEWHERE
 7. If the plan was bought on the web, say so and where to manage it   (Practice)
 8. Rules on linking to the website for payment differ by country and store; follow the current guideline   (Standard: apple-review, play-payments)

PHYSICAL GOODS AND SERVICES
 9. Saved payment methods with brand and last 4 digits   (Standard: pci-dss)
10. Order and payment history with receipts   (Practice)
11. VAT invoices for Saudi customers   (Rule: zatca-einvoice)

CANCELLING
12. Cancel as easy as subscribing; the path is clear   (Rule: ftc-negative-option)
13. What happens after cancelling: access until the period ends   (Standard: apple-subscriptions)

PROBLEMS
14. Billing problem (card failed at the store): banner with a link to fix it in the store   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)  -> see design-system/banner
15. Grace period respected: access continues while the store retries   (Standard: apple-subscriptions, play-subscriptions)

ACCESS
16. Prices read clearly with currency by screen readers   (Standard: hig-accessibility)

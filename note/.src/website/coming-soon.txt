title: Coming Soon page
about: A holding page before a launch that says what is coming and collects interest.
## Content
- What is coming and who it is for, in one line | P
- A rough launch date, or "early 2027", only if the team will keep it | P
- One clear action: leave an email to be told at launch | P
- What the person will get by signing up (early access, a discount) | P
- Company name and a way to contact | L eu-consumer-rights
## The email form
- One field: email, with a visible label and the right input type | S wcag-labels mdn-autocomplete
- Say exactly what emails will be sent and how often | L gdpr-13 can-spam
- Consent box unticked by default where the law needs consent for marketing | L gdpr-7
- A link to the privacy policy beside the form | L gdpr-13
- Confirmation on screen and by email; double opt-in where required | P
- Errors shown in words next to the field | S wcag-error
## Countdown, if used
- A countdown that is real and never resets | L ftc-dark-patterns
- Countdown numbers not read aloud every second by screen readers | S wcag-status
## Search and status
- Returns a normal page (status 200), with a real title and description | S google-title google-snippet
- If the main site is down for a short while, use a maintenance page instead (status 503) | S google-maintenance
## Look and access
- Contrast and text size meet WCAG | S wcag-contrast
- Any background video or animation can be paused and respects reduced motion | S wcag-pause mdn-reduced-motion
- Works at phone width with no sideways scroll | S wcag-reflow

title: Login page (website)
about: Lets returning people sign in quickly and safely, and recover when they cannot.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The form
- Email (or username) and password on one page, unless the site uses passkeys or single sign-on first | S webdev-signin
- Real form with a form tag and a submit button, so Enter works and password managers save it | S webdev-signin
- Visible labels, not placeholder-only | S wcag-labels nng-placeholder
- autocomplete set to username and current-password | S webdev-signin mdn-autocomplete wcag-purpose
- Email field uses type email; no automatic capitals or spelling fixes | S webdev-signin
- Pasting into the password field is allowed | S nist-63b wcag-auth
- Password managers and autofill are never blocked | S nist-63b wcag-auth
- Show password button with a clear name and state | R baymard-show govuk-show
- Remember me (stay signed in) choice, with its length stated | P
## Ways in
- Passkeys offered, with the browser's passkey autofill on the email field | S webauthn fido-ux
- Sign in with Google, Apple or work single sign-on where the audience uses them | P
- Email magic link or one-time code as a choice for people without a password | P
- The same ways shown on sign-up and sign-in, so people remember which they used | P
## Errors and safety
- One error for a wrong email or password, never which one was wrong | S owasp-auth
- Errors shown next to the field and focused, in words | S wcag-error govuk-error-message
- Caps Lock warning on the password field | P
- Limits on repeated wrong tries (slow down, then lock) with a message saying what to do | S owasp-auth nist-63b
- A robot check only after suspicious tries, and an accessible one | S wcag-auth
- Two-step check after the password when the account has it | S owasp-mfa
- Signs in over HTTPS only; session cookie secure and httpOnly | S owasp-session
- New sign-ins from a new device announced by email | S owasp-auth
## Recovery
- Forgot password link right beside the password field | S webdev-signin
- A link to sign up for people without an account | P
- Help or contact link for locked-out people | S wcag-help
## After sign-in
- Back to the page they were on before signing in | P
- No redirect to an outside address taken from the link | S owasp-redirect
## Access and layout
- Focus starts on the first field, not stolen later | S wcag-focus-order
- Page title says "Sign in" with the site name | S wcag-title
- Use the same words everywhere: either "Sign in" or "Log in", not both | R nng-heuristics

title: Event page
about: A page for one event (talk, conference, webinar, launch) that tells people what, when and where, and gets them registered.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The essentials above the fold
- Event name and one line on why to attend | P
- Date and start and end time with the time zone written out | S iso-8601
- Times shown in the visitor's own time zone for online events, with the original beside it | S w3c-forms-i18n
- Place: venue name and full address for in person, "Online" with the platform for virtual | P
- One clear Register or Get tickets button, repeated at the end | R nng-forms
- Price, or "Free", visible before the button is pressed | L ftc-junk-fees eu-consumer-rights
- Seats left or "Sold out" shown honestly; never fake scarcity | L ftc-dark-patterns
## Details
- Agenda with times for each part | P
- Speakers with photo, name, role and a short bio | P
- Who it is for and what they will take away | P
- Language of the event | P
- Accessibility at the venue: step-free entry, captions, sign language, quiet room | L ada-web eu-accessibility-act
- Directions, map, parking and public transport | P
- What to bring and dress code if any | P
- Refund and transfer policy for tickets | L eu-consumer-rights >flows/requesting-a-refund
## Registration
- A short form: only the fields needed to attend | R baymard-fields
- Confirmation screen and email with the date, place and a calendar file | R govuk-confirm >web-app/calendar
- Add to calendar for Google, Apple and Outlook | P
- A way to cancel a registration | P
- Reminder email before the event, with the join link for online events | P
## After the event
- The page changes once the event has passed: "This event has ended" with the recording or slides | P
- No Register button on a past event | R nng-heuristics
## Search and sharing
- Event structured data (name, start date, location, offers, status) so search shows it | S google-event schema-event >growth/technical-seo
- Event status marked if cancelled, postponed or moved online | S google-event
- A share image and title for social links | P >growth/social-links
## Accessibility
- Captions and transcripts for any video | S wcag-captions
- Page title includes the event name and date | S wcag-title

## Reading
- Body text at least 16 pixels, line height about 1.5 | S wcag-spacing
- Lines of about 50 to 75 characters | P butterick-line
- Headings every few paragraphs, in order | S wcag-headings
- Short paragraphs, lists and bold key words | R nng-scanning
- A contents list for long posts, with jump links | P
- Links say where they go | S wcag-link
- Images with alt text and captions; charts also described in words | S wcag-nontext
- Code blocks scroll on their own on phones, never the whole page | S wcag-reflow
- Text resizes to 200% without breaking | S wcag-resize
- Dark mode respected if the site has one | P mdn-color-scheme

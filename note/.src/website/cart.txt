title: Cart page
about: Shows what the shopper is about to buy and the full cost, and moves them to checkout.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Items
- Each item: photo, name, chosen options (size, colour), unit price and quantity | R baymard-guest
- Change quantity with a field plus minus and plus buttons | R baymard-guest
- Remove an item, with Undo instead of a confirmation box | R nng-control
- A link back to each product | P
- Stock warnings that are true ("Only 2 left" only if real) | L ftc-dark-patterns
- Save for later or move to wishlist | P
## Cost
- Subtotal, shipping, tax and discounts each on their own line | R baymard-guest
- Shipping cost or an estimate shown before checkout; hidden costs are the top reason people leave | R baymard-abandon
- No surprise fees added later; all mandatory fees in the first price shown | L ftc-junk-fees eu-consumer-rights
- Total in the shopper's currency, stating whether tax is included | L eu-consumer-rights
- Delivery date estimate, not only a speed | R baymard-guest
- Promo code field present but low-key (a link that opens it) | R baymard-coupon
## Moving on
- One clear Checkout button, at the top and bottom on long carts | P
- Guest checkout offered; forcing an account makes people leave | R baymard-guest baymard-abandon
- Express pay buttons (Apple Pay, Google Pay) for speed | P
- Continue shopping link | P
- Trust signals near the button: returns policy, secure payment | R baymard-trust
## States
- An empty cart that suggests where to go next | R nng-empty
- Cart kept across visits and devices for signed-in shoppers | P
- Changes to totals announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
## Access
- Quantity buttons have names ("Increase quantity of Blue mug") | S wcag-name-role

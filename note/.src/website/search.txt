title: Search (website)
about: The search box and results on a website, so visitors find a page without browsing.
## The box
- Shown as an open box, not hidden behind an icon, on sites with lots of content | R nng-search
- At least 27 characters wide so queries are visible | R nng-search
- A magnifier icon and a label or placeholder that says what can be searched | R nng-search nng-placeholder
- Has an accessible name and sits in a search landmark | S wcag-name-role apg-landmarks
- Opens with a keyboard shortcut (/ or Ctrl+K) shown in the box | P >web-app/keyboard-shortcuts
- Enter submits; a clear button empties the box | P
## Suggestions while typing
- Suggestions after 1 to 3 letters, at most about 8-10 | R baymard-autocomplete
- Typed part plain, suggested part bold (or the other way, consistently) | R baymard-autocomplete
- Arrow keys move through suggestions, Escape closes them | S apg-combobox
- Suggestion count announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
- Recent searches offered when the box is focused | P
## Results
- The query stays in the box on the results page | R nng-search
- Number of results shown | P
- Each result: title, a short extract with the matched words highlighted, type and date | P
- Typos forgiven, with "Did you mean ...?" | R baymard-autocomplete
- Filters by type, date or section for large sites | R nng-filters >flows/filtering-items
- Sort by relevance by default | P
- Pagination or "Load more" with the position kept on Back | R nng-infinite >design-system/pagination
## No results
- Say so plainly, repeat the query, and offer fixes: spelling, fewer words, popular pages | R nng-empty
- A link to contact or browse | P
## Speed and access
- Results in under 1 second; show progress if slower | R nng-response
- Page title includes the query ("Results for pricing") | S wcag-title
- Results page is not indexed by search engines | P >growth/technical-seo

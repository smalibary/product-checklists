title: Sign up page (website)
about: Creating an account on a website. The phone version (mobile/sign-up) has the full 65 items; this one adds what differs on a website.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Before the form
- What the person gets, in one line above the form | P
- Let people try or buy without an account where possible | R baymard-guest nng-login-walls
- Price and trial terms visible before the form if sign-up starts a trial | L ftc-negative-option
## Ways to sign up
- Sign up with Google, Apple, Microsoft or work single sign-on, matched to the audience | P
- Passkey offered at or right after sign-up, with a fallback | S webauthn fido-ux
- Email and password as the always-there choice | P
## Fields
- Ask only what is needed now; everything else later | R baymard-fields webdev-signup
- One name field ("Full name"), not first and last, for international names | S w3c-names
- Visible labels; placeholders only as examples | S wcag-labels nng-placeholder
- autocomplete set: name, email, new-password | S webdev-signup mdn-autocomplete wcag-purpose
- Optional fields marked "(optional)"; required ones not starred | R nng-required govuk-question
- Email typo hint ("Did you mean gmail.com?") | P
## Password
- At least 8 characters (15 if it is the only factor), up to at least 64, any characters | S nist-63b
- No rules like "one capital, one symbol"; check against known leaked passwords instead | S nist-63b
- Requirements shown before typing, ticked as met | R baymard-inline
- Show password button | R baymard-show
- Paste and password managers allowed | S nist-63b wcag-auth
## Errors
- Checked when the person leaves a field, not while typing | R baymard-inline
- Errors next to the field and in a summary at the top after Submit | S govuk-error-summary wcag-error
- What was typed is kept after an error | S wcag-redundant
- Submit is never disabled; it explains what is missing instead | R govuk-button
## Legal and consent
- Terms and privacy links beside the button | L gdpr-13
- Marketing consent separate and unticked | L gdpr-7
- Age check where children's law applies | L coppa
## Sending and after
- The button says what it does ("Create account") and shows progress | R nng-status
- Email verification by code or link, with resend and change-email | P
- Account-exists case handled without revealing which emails have accounts | S owasp-auth
- A welcome screen that leads to the first useful step | R nng-onboarding
## Access
- Robot checks accessible, or replaced by invisible checks | S wcag-auth

title: Blog (list page)
about: The blog's front page that lists articles and helps readers find one to read.
also: design-system/accessibility
## List
- Newest posts first, with a featured post at the top if useful | R nng-blog
- Each card: title, short summary, date, author, reading time and image | R nng-blog
- The whole card opens the post, with one link inside (not many nested links) | S wcag-link
- Categories or topics to filter | R nng-blog
- Search over posts | R nng-blog
- Pagination with page numbers, or Load more that keeps position on Back | R nng-infinite
## Subscribe
- Email sign-up with what and how often | L gdpr-13 can-spam
- RSS feed linked | P
## Layout
- Cards in a grid that becomes one column on phones | S wcag-reflow
- Images have space reserved so the page does not jump | S webdev-cls
- Images lazy-loaded below the fold | S webdev-images
- Dates in a clear format with the year | P
## Access and search
- One main heading; card titles as headings | S wcag-headings
- Card images decorative (empty alt) when the title already says it | S wai-images
- Contrast meets WCAG, including over images | S wcag-contrast
- A unique title and description for the list and each category | S google-title
- Breadcrumbs on category pages | S google-breadcrumb

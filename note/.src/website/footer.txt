title: Footer
about: The bottom of every page: a safety net of links, contact and legal information.
## Content
- Links grouped under short headings (Product, Company, Resources, Legal) | R nng-footers
- Contact: email, phone or a link to the contact page | R nng-footers nng-contact
- Company name, registration and address where the law asks | L eu-consumer-rights
- Privacy policy, terms and cookie settings links | L gdpr-13 eprivacy
- "Do not sell or share my personal information" where California law applies | L ccpa
- Accessibility statement link | L eu-accessibility-act
- Copyright line with the current year | P
- Social links with names, not only icons | S wcag-link wcag-nontext
- Language and region switcher | P
- Newsletter sign-up, short, with consent rules | L gdpr-7
- Status page and help centre links | P
## Consistency
- The same footer, in the same order, on every page | S wcag-nav
- Help link in the same place on every page | S wcag-help
## Structure and access
- Marked as the page's footer landmark (contentinfo) | S apg-landmarks
- Link groups are lists with headings | S wcag-info
- Link text says where it goes, no "click here" | S wcag-link
- Contrast meets 4.5 to 1, even on dark footers | S wcag-contrast
- Touch targets at least 24 pixels apart or in size | S wcag-target
- Groups stack or fold into sections on phones | S wcag-reflow
- Back to top link on long pages | P

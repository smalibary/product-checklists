title: Affiliate page
about: Invites people to promote the product for a commission and explains how the programme works.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The offer
- Commission stated plainly: percent or amount, one-off or recurring, for how long | P
- Cookie window (how long after a click a sale still counts) | P
- When and how payouts happen, the minimum payout and the currencies | P
- A worked example: "Refer 10 customers on the Pro plan, earn X a month" | P
## Joining
- Who can join, and who cannot (for example, no paid search on the brand name) | P
- A short application form; tell people how long approval takes | R baymard-fields
- A sign-in link for existing affiliates | P
- Terms of the programme linked before applying | L eu-consumer-rights
## Rules affiliates must follow
- Affiliates must say clearly that they earn a commission | L ftc-endorsements
- No false claims, fake reviews or spam to promote the product | L ftc-reviews can-spam
- Brand guidelines and approved logos to download | P
## Tools
- Links, banners and text ready to copy | P
- A dashboard showing clicks, sign-ups, sales and money owed | P >web-app/dashboard
- A contact for affiliate questions | R nng-contact
## Trust
- Quotes or numbers from current affiliates (real, with permission) | L ftc-endorsements
- FAQ on tax forms, payment methods and returns that cancel commission | R nng-faq >website/faq
## Accessibility
- Form fields with visible labels and clear errors | S wcag-labels wcag-error >design-system/accessibility

title: About page
about: Tells visitors who the company is, why it exists and why they can trust it.
## Who and why
- One sentence on what the company does and for whom, at the top | R nng-about
- The story: when and why it started, in a few short paragraphs | R nng-about
- Mission or purpose in plain words, not slogans | R nng-about
- What makes the company different, with facts | R nng-trust
## People
- Founders and leaders with real photos, names and roles | R nng-about nng-trust
- Team size or a link to the Team page | P
- Photos of real people and places, not stock images | R nng-trust
## Proof
- Numbers that matter: customers, years, countries, with the date they were true | R nng-trust
- Customer logos or quotes used with permission | L ftc-endorsements
- Awards, press mentions and partners, linked to the source | P
- Investors or ownership, if relevant to trust | P
## Contact and legal
- Physical address and registered company name | L eu-consumer-rights
- A way to get in touch from the page | R nng-contact
- Links to Careers, Press and Contact | P
## Writing and layout
- Short paragraphs, headings and lists for scanning | R nng-scanning
- Headings that say what each part is about | S wcag-headings
- Images have alt text that describes them; decorative ones are hidden from screen readers | S wcag-nontext wai-images
- Text contrast meets 4.5 to 1 | S wcag-contrast
- Organization structured data (name, logo, address, links) | S schema-org
- Page title that names the company | S wcag-title

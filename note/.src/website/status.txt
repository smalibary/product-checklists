title: Status page
about: Shows whether the service is working right now, and tells people about problems and planned maintenance.
## Now
- One line at the top: "All systems working" or what is broken | R nng-status
- Each part of the service listed (website, app, API, payments) with its state | P
- States in words and colour together: Working, Slow, Partly down, Down, Maintenance | S wcag-color
- Time of the last check, with the time zone | R nng-status
## Incidents
- An incident post within minutes of a problem, even before the cause is known | S atlassian-incident
- Updates on a steady rhythm (for example every 30 minutes) until fixed | S atlassian-incident
- Each update says what is affected, what users can do, and when the next update comes | S atlassian-incident
- A short review after big incidents: cause, fix, how it will not happen again | S atlassian-incident
- Plain words; no blame and no hiding | P
## History and plans
- Uptime for the last 90 days per part, with the number | P
- Past incidents listed by date | P
- Planned maintenance announced ahead with start, end and what is affected | P
## Getting told
- Subscribe by email, text, RSS or a chat app | P
- Unsubscribe in one step | L can-spam rfc-unsubscribe
- A machine-readable feed (API or RSS) for customers' own tools | P
## Reliability of the page itself
- Hosted apart from the main service, so it stays up when the service is down | P
- Fast and light; works on a slow phone connection | S webdev-vitals
- Status changes announced to screen readers without moving focus | S wcag-status
- Contrast and text size meet WCAG | S wcag-contrast
- Linked from the footer, the help centre and error pages | P

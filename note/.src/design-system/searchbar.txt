title: Searchbar
about: The text box where people type to search, with its button, suggestions and clear control.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Look
- A wide box; the width hints at query length (about 27 characters visible) | R nng-search
- Placed where people expect: top of the page or app | R nng-search
- Magnifier icon and a Search button, or Enter to search, stated | R nng-search
- Placeholder can hint scope ("Search orders") but is not the label | S wcag-labels
- Clear (x) button once something is typed | S m3-search
## Suggestions
- Suggestions appear after a character or two, at most about 10 | R baymard-autocomplete
- Suggestion text highlights what is added, not what was typed | R baymard-autocomplete
- Recent searches shown when empty, with a way to remove them | R nng-search
- Arrow keys move through suggestions; Enter picks one | S apg-combobox
- Typo-tolerant | R baymard-autocomplete
## Behaviour
- Scope choice if the product has several kinds of items | R nng-search
- A shortcut opens it (/ or Ctrl+K), shown in the box | P
- Search runs on Enter, or live after a short pause for small data sets | P
- The query stays in the box on the results page | R nng-search
## Phones
- Opens full screen with the keyboard ready | S m3-search hig-search
- Search key on the keyboard (inputmode search, enterkeyhint) | S mdn-inputmode
- Cancel button returns to where the person was | S hig-search
## Access
- Search landmark (role search or the search element) | S apg-landmarks
- Real label, visible or for screen readers ("Search") | S wcag-labels
- Suggestion list follows the combobox pattern; number of suggestions announced | S apg-combobox wcag-status
- type="search" so browsers add their own features | S mdn-input-search

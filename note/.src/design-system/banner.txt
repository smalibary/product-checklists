title: Banner
about: A full-width message across the top of the app or page, about something that affects the whole product (outage, trial ending, cookies).
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- For product-wide news or problems that persist (trial ends, payment failed, read-only mode) | S m3-banner
- One banner at a time; more wait in line | S m3-banner
- Not for messages about one action (use a toast) or one section (use an alert) | R nng-indicators >design-system/toast
## Content
- One short sentence on what is happening | S govuk-notification
- One or two actions (Update payment, Learn more) | S m3-banner
- Dates and times with time zone where relevant | P
## Look
- Kind shown by colour, icon and words (info, warning, error) | S wcag-color
- Contrast for text and buttons meets WCAG | S wcag-contrast >design-system/accessibility
- Stays at the top of the content, under the header | S m3-banner
## Behaviour
- Can be closed if not critical; closed stays closed for that message | P
- Critical banners (account suspended) cannot be closed until fixed | P
- Does not cover content; the page moves down once, not while reading | S webdev-cls
- Does not cover focused items when sticky | S wcag-obscured
## Access
- Region with a name, or role status if it appears after load | S wcag-status
- Close button has a name ("Dismiss trial notice") | S wcag-name-role
- Reachable early in the tab order | S wcag-focus-order
## Cookie banner
- Accept and Reject equally easy, no pre-ticked boxes | L eprivacy gdpr-7
- Settings link for choices by purpose | L eprivacy

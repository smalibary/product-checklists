title: Motion
about: Animation rules: when things move, how fast, and for whom motion is turned off.
## Purpose
- Motion explains a change (where something came from or went) and is never only decoration | R nng-animation
- Short: about 100 to 300 ms for small changes, up to about 500 ms for big ones | S m3-motion
- Easing curves from a shared set (tokens) | S m3-motion dtcg-tokens
## Safety
- Reduced motion setting respected: swap movement for fades or nothing | S mdn-reduced-motion wcag-animation
- Nothing flashes more than 3 times a second | S wcag-flash
- Moving content over 5 seconds can be paused | S wcag-pause
## Behaviour
- Animations never block input | R nng-response
- The same motion for the same job everywhere | S m3-motion

title: Pagination
about: The control for moving between pages of a long list.
## Parts
- Previous and Next with words, not only arrows | S govuk-pagination
- Page numbers with the current one marked, and gaps shown with ... | S govuk-pagination
- Current page marked with aria-current | S govuk-pagination
## Behaviour
- Each page has its own address | P
- Focus or scroll moves to the top of the new results | P
- A "View all" option when lists are short enough | R nng-pagination
- Infinite scroll avoided where people need to find things again | R nng-infinite
## Look
- Targets big enough to tap | S wcag-target
- Inside a nav landmark with a name ("Pagination") | S apg-landmarks
## Access
- Works by keyboard; link names say the page number | S wcag-link

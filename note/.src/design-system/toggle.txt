title: Toggle
about: A switch that turns one setting on or off and takes effect at once.
## When to use
- A setting that applies immediately (Notifications on) | R nng-toggle
- Not inside a form with a Save button (use a checkbox) | R nng-toggle
- Not for choosing between two different things (use radios or a segmented control) | R nng-toggle
## Look
- Track and thumb; on and off clearly different by position and fill, not only colour | S wcag-color m3-switch
- An optional check icon inside the thumb when on | S m3-switch
- Label on the start side says what is switched, not the state ("Email alerts") | R nng-toggle
- Track border and thumb 3:1 contrast in both states | S wcag-ui-contrast
- Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 to 48 | S wcag-target hig-toggles
## Behaviour
- Changes apply at once, with a short "Saved" message if it is saved to a server | S wcag-status
- If saving fails, the toggle goes back and an error says why | R nng-errors
- If the change is slow, a busy state | R nng-response
- Labels never change meaning with the state ("On" text can confuse) | R nng-toggle
- Disabled toggles explain why | P
## Access
- role switch with aria-checked, or a native checkbox styled as a switch | S apg-switch
- Space toggles; Enter may too | S apg-switch
- Label linked to the switch | S wcag-labels
- Focus ring visible | S wcag-focus
- Thumb moves to the other side in right-to-left | S w3c-dir
- Animation respects reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion

title: Button
about: The control people press to do something: submit, save, open, delete.
## Kinds
- A small set of kinds: primary, secondary, tertiary (text), danger | S m3-buttons hig-buttons
- One primary button per view or section | R nng-buttons
- Danger style only for actions that destroy or cannot be undone | R nng-confirm
- Links go somewhere; buttons do something. Use the right element | S govuk-button
## Content
- A verb that says what happens ("Save changes", "Send invoice") | S govuk-button
- Short, start with a capital, no "Click here" | R nng-buttons
- Icon plus text when it helps; icon-only buttons have a text name | S wcag-name-role
## Sizes and look
- Sizes from tokens (small, medium, large); touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 to 48 | S wcag-target hig-layout m3-buttons
- Text 4.5:1 contrast; button edge or fill 3:1 against the background | S wcag-contrast wcag-ui-contrast
- Looks pressable: filled or outlined, not plain text for main actions | R nng-buttons
## States
- Default, hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading | S m3-buttons
- Focus ring visible in every theme | S wcag-focus-appearance
- Loading: spinner inside, same width, cannot be pressed again | P
- Disabled buttons used sparingly; better to allow press and explain what is missing | R nng-buttons
## Placement
- Primary action placed consistently (end of the form or dialog) | S wcag-consistent
- Order follows the platform: on Windows and web varies; on iOS and Android follow their guides | S hig-buttons m3-dialogs
- Full width on phones for main actions | P
## Access
- Native button element, or role button with Enter and Space | S wcag-keyboard
- Toggle buttons say pressed (aria-pressed) | S wcag-name-role
- Button opening a menu says so (aria-haspopup, aria-expanded) | S apg-menubutton
- Name matches the visible label | S wcag-label-in-name

title: Badge
about: A small label or dot that shows a count or a status (3 unread, New, Paid).
## Kinds
- Count badge on an icon (unread messages) | S m3-badges
- Dot badge for "something new" without a number | S m3-badges
- Status badge or tag with text (Paid, Overdue, Draft) | S govuk-tag
## Content
- Counts capped (99+) | S m3-badges
- Status text short, one or two words | S govuk-tag
- Same word for the same status everywhere | R nng-consistency
## Look
- Status shown by text, not colour alone | S wcag-color
- A small set of status colours from role tokens | S dtcg-tokens
- Text contrast 4.5:1 against the badge fill | S wcag-contrast
- Badges not mistaken for buttons (no hover unless clickable) | S govuk-tag
- Badge position flips in right-to-left | S w3c-dir
## Behaviour
- Count badges disappear at zero | S m3-badges
- Dot badges clear when the person sees the new thing | P
- Not overused: too many badges stop meaning anything | R nng-indicators
## Access
- The count is part of the control's name ("Messages, 3 unread") | S wcag-name-role
- Visible badge text hidden from screen readers if the name already includes it | P
- Count changes announced politely only where important | S wcag-status

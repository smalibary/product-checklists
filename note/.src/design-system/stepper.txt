title: Stepper
about: Two meanings: a progress stepper showing the steps of a process, and a number stepper with minus and plus buttons.
## Progress stepper: when to use
- Processes with 3 to about 7 steps in a fixed order | R nng-wizards
- Not for free-order tasks (use a task list) | S govuk-task-list
## Progress stepper: look
- Each step has a number and a short name | R nng-progress
- Done, current and upcoming steps different by icon and text, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Horizontal on wide screens; vertical or "Step 2 of 5" on phones | S wcag-reflow >design-system/accessibility
- Steps that are done can be clicked to go back; future steps cannot | R nng-wizards
- Errors in a step marked on that step | P
## Progress stepper: access
- An ordered list; the current step marked aria-current="step" | S wcag-info >design-system/accessibility
- Each step's state in words for screen readers ("completed") | S wcag-name-role
- The page title also says the step | S wcag-title
## Number stepper: look and behaviour
- Minus button, a number field and plus button | S hig-pickers
- The number field can also be typed in | S wcag-dragging
- Minimum and maximum; buttons disabled at the limits, with a reason | P
- Holding a button repeats, slowly then faster | P
- Step size suits the value (1 for quantity, 0.5 for hours) | P
## Number stepper: access
- Buttons have names ("Increase quantity") and target size at least 24 px | S wcag-name-role wcag-target >design-system/accessibility
- The field is a spinbutton or number input with min, max and value | S apg-spinbutton
- Arrow up and down change the value | S apg-spinbutton
- Value changes announced | S wcag-status

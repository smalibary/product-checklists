title: Tooltip
about: A small label that appears near an element on hover or keyboard focus, to name it or add a short hint.
## When to use
- Naming icon-only buttons, and short hints that are nice to have | R nng-tooltip
- Never for information people need to finish a task (put it on the page) | R nng-tooltip
- Never for interactive content like links or buttons (use a popover) | S apg-tooltip
- Not on touch screens alone: they have no hover | R nng-tooltip
## Content
- A few words; one sentence at most | R nng-tooltip
- No repetition of the visible label | R nng-tooltip
## Look and place
- Close to the element, not covering it or what it relates to | R nng-tooltip
- Moves to stay on screen | P
- Contrast meets WCAG | S wcag-contrast
- An arrow pointing at the element is optional | P
## Timing
- Appears after a short delay (about 300 to 500 ms) on hover, at once on focus | R nng-tooltip
- Stays while the pointer is over the tooltip itself | S wcag-hover
- Closes with Escape without moving focus | S wcag-hover apg-tooltip
- Closes when focus or pointer leaves | S apg-tooltip
## Access
- role tooltip, linked from the element with aria-describedby (hint) or used as its name (aria-labelledby) | S apg-tooltip
- Shows on keyboard focus, not only on hover | S wcag-hover
- The element still has a name without the tooltip for screen readers | S wcag-name-role
- Disabled buttons: put the tooltip on a wrapper that can take focus, or explain on the page | P

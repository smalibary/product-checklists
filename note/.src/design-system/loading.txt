title: Loading
about: How the product shows that something is happening: spinners, progress bars, busy buttons and skeletons.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Choosing by wait time
- Under 1 second: show nothing, to avoid a flash | R nng-response
- 1 to 10 seconds: a spinner or a skeleton | R nng-response nng-skeleton >design-system/skeleton
- Over 10 seconds: a progress bar with percent or steps, time left, and Cancel | R nng-progress >design-system/progress
- Very long jobs: run in the background and notify when done | R nng-progress
## Kinds
- Determinate progress bar when the amount is known | S m3-progress >design-system/progress
- Indeterminate (spinning or moving) when it is not | S m3-progress
- Busy button: the button shows a spinner, keeps its width, and cannot be pressed twice | P
- Skeleton for page content (see Skeleton) | R nng-skeleton >design-system/skeleton
- Pull-to-refresh on phone lists | S hig-loading
## Messages
- Say what is happening for longer waits ("Uploading 3 of 10 files") | R nng-progress >design-system/file-upload
- Never a spinner forever: after a timeout, an error with Retry | R nng-errors
- Optimistic updates for fast, safe actions (like, tick) with rollback on failure | P
## Look
- Size and colour of the spinner consistent across the product | R nng-consistency
- Spinner and bar meet 3:1 contrast | S wcag-ui-contrast
- No page-blocking overlays unless the whole page truly cannot be used | P
## Access
- Progress bar has role progressbar, a name and its value | S wcag-name-role >design-system/progress
- Loading region marked busy (aria-busy); start and end announced once | S wcag-status
- Nothing flashes more than 3 times a second | S wcag-flash

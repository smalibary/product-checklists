title: Checkbox
about: A box that people tick to choose any number of options, or to agree to one thing.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- Choosing zero, one or many from a list | S govuk-checkboxes
- One checkbox for a yes/no agreement that takes effect on submit | S govuk-checkboxes
- Use a switch instead for settings that apply at once | R nng-toggle
- Use radios when only one choice is allowed | S govuk-radios
## Look
- Box on the start side of its label; the label is clickable too | S govuk-checkboxes
- Ticked, unticked and mixed (some children ticked) states clearly different | S apg-checkbox
- Box border 3:1 contrast | S wcag-ui-contrast
- Disabled shown clearly, with why if possible | P
- Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 | S wcag-target
## Groups
- Grouped in a fieldset with a question as the legend | S govuk-checkboxes
- Hint text under the legend ("Select all that apply") | S govuk-checkboxes
- Vertical list, one per line, in a logical order | R nng-checkboxes
- "None of these" option where it helps, which clears the others | S govuk-checkboxes
- Select all for long lists, using the mixed state | S apg-checkbox
## Behaviour
- Ticking does not submit or change the page by itself | S wcag-on-input
- Errors on the group ("Select at least one") | S govuk-error-message
- Never pre-ticked for marketing consent | L gdpr-7
## Access
- Native checkbox input, or role checkbox with aria-checked | S apg-checkbox
- Space toggles | S apg-checkbox
- Label linked to the box | S wcag-labels

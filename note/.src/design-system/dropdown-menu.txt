title: Dropdown Menu
about: A list of actions or choices that opens from a button (a menu), and the related select for picking a value.
## Which one
- Menu of actions (Edit, Duplicate, Delete) opened by a button: the menu button pattern | S apg-menubutton
- Picking one value in a form: a native select or a listbox, not an action menu | S govuk-select
- Long lists of values with typing: a combobox | S apg-combobox
- Short choices (2 to 5) are often better as radios | R nng-dropdown
## Content
- Items short, start with a verb for actions | R nng-dropdown
- Grouped with dividers; dangerous items last and marked | R nng-dropdown
- Icons optional, consistent | P
- Keyboard shortcuts shown on the right | P
- Submenus avoided, at most one level | R nng-dropdown
- Disabled items explained or hidden | P
## Look
- Opens next to its button, flips to stay on screen | P
- Menu edge and items meet contrast; selected and focused items clear | S wcag-ui-contrast wcag-focus
- Item height large enough to tap | S wcag-target
- Width fits the longest item | P
## Behaviour
- Opens on click or Enter, not on hover alone | R nng-dropdown
- Closes on Escape, on choosing, and on clicking outside | S apg-menubutton
- Focus returns to the button after closing | S apg-menubutton
- On phones, long menus open as a bottom sheet | S m3-bottom-sheets
## Keyboard and access
- Button has aria-haspopup and aria-expanded | S apg-menubutton
- Menu and menuitem roles; arrow keys move, Home/End jump, typing a letter jumps | S apg-menubutton
- Checkable items use menuitemcheckbox or menuitemradio | S apg-menubutton
- Button name says what the menu is for ("More actions for Invoice 104") | S wcag-name-role

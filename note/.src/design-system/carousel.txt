title: Carousel
about: A row of slides (images, cards) shown a few at a time, moved by arrows, swipes or dots.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- Browsing a set of similar items (photos of a product, related cards) | S m3-carousel
- Not for important messages on a homepage: later slides are rarely seen | R nng-carousel
- Show part of the next slide so people know there is more | S m3-carousel
## Controls
- Previous and next buttons with names, large enough to tap | S apg-carousel wcag-target >design-system/accessibility
- Dots or "3 of 8" to show position; dots are buttons with names | S apg-carousel
- Swipe on touch, with buttons too | S wcag-dragging >mobile/gesture-navigation
- Keyboard can reach the controls and each slide | S apg-carousel
## Auto-play
- Avoid auto-play; if used, a visible pause button | S apg-carousel wcag-pause
- Auto-play stops on hover, on focus, and for reduced motion | S apg-carousel mdn-reduced-motion >motion/motion-accessibility
- Slides stay at least 5 seconds | R nng-carousel
## Content
- Each slide makes sense alone | R nng-carousel
- Images have alt text | S wcag-nontext >design-system/accessibility
- Text on images keeps contrast (overlay) | S wcag-contrast >design-system/accessibility
- Slides sized before images load, so nothing jumps | S webdev-cls
## Access
- The carousel is a region named by its role ("carousel") and label | S apg-carousel
- Each slide is a group named "3 of 8" | S apg-carousel
- Slide changes announced politely only when the person moves it, not during auto-play | S apg-carousel
- Off-screen slides hidden from keyboard and screen readers | S apg-carousel
- Direction flips for Arabic | S w3c-dir
## Speed
- Only the visible and next images load first | S webdev-lazy

title: Navigation bar
about: The top bar of a website or app with the logo, main links and account menu.
## Content
- Logo links to home | R nng-indicators
- 5 to 7 main links with clear words | R nng-scanning
- Current section marked visually and with aria-current | R nng-indicators
- Search and account in their usual places (top right in left-to-right layouts) | P
## Phones
- Collapses into a menu or bottom bar on phones | R nng-hamburger
## Behaviour
- Menus open on click; hover menus have a delay and keyboard support | S apg-menubutton
- A skip link to the main content before it | S wcag-bypass
## Semantics
- Inside a nav landmark with a name | S apg-landmarks
## Look
- Stays consistent on every page | S wcag-consistent
- Sticky bars do not cover focused items | S wcag-obscured

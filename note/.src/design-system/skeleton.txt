title: Skeleton
about: Grey placeholder shapes shown where content will appear while it loads.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- For loads of about 1 to 10 seconds where the page layout is known | R nng-skeleton
- For quick loads under a second, show nothing (avoid a flash) | R nng-response
- For long or unknown waits, add a progress indicator or message | R nng-progress
- Not for buttons or actions, only content | R nng-skeleton
## Look
- Shapes match the real content's size and layout (text lines, images, avatars) | R nng-skeleton
- Neutral colour with low contrast, in both light and dark themes | P
- Optional slow shimmer; static when reduced motion is on | S mdn-reduced-motion
- The same skeleton style across the product | R nng-consistency
## Behaviour
- Real content replaces it in the same place, with no layout jump | S webdev-cls
- Parts load on their own (each card replaces its own skeleton) | R nng-skeleton
- Turns into an error with Retry if loading fails | R nng-errors
- Turns into an empty state if there is no data | R nng-empty
## Access
- Skeleton shapes hidden from screen readers | S wai-images
- The loading region marked busy (aria-busy), and a short "Loading" message announced | S wcag-status
- Focus not placed on skeleton parts | S wcag-focus-order
- Shimmer does not flash | S wcag-flash

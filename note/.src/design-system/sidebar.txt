title: Sidebar navigation
about: The side menu in apps for moving between sections.
## Structure
- Sections grouped with headings | R nng-vertical-nav
- Icons with text labels; icons alone only when collapsed, with tooltips | R nng-icons >design-system/tooltip
- Current page marked | R nng-indicators
## Behaviour
- Collapsible, remembering the choice | R nng-vertical-nav
- Nested items expand in place | S apg-disclosure
- Scrolls on its own when long | P
## Phones
- Becomes a drawer on small screens | S m3-nav-drawer >design-system/drawer
## Semantics
- nav landmark; current link with aria-current | S apg-landmarks
## Direction
- Moves to the right side in Arabic layouts | S w3c-dir hig-right-to-left

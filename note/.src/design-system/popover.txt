title: Popover
about: A small floating panel tied to a button, for extra options or details.
## Use
- For short, non-blocking content; use a dialog when a decision is needed | S hig-popovers >design-system/modal
- Not used for content people must read to continue | S hig-popovers
## Behaviour
- Opens on click or tap, not on hover alone | S wcag-hover
- Closes on Escape, on outside click and when the trigger is pressed again | S mdn-popover
- Focus moves into it when it has controls, and back to the trigger when closed | S apg-dialog
- Stays on screen at small sizes; repositions near edges | S wcag-reflow >design-system/accessibility
## Semantics
- The trigger says it opens something (aria-expanded) | S apg-disclosure
- Built on the browser's popover feature where possible | S mdn-popover
## Look
- Clear edge and shadow, arrow pointing at its trigger | S m3-elevation >design-system/elevation

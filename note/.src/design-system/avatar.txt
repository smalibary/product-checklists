title: Avatar
about: A small picture (or initials) that represents a person or an organisation.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Content
- Photo when there is one; initials as the fallback; then a generic icon | P
- Initials from the first letters of the name, handling one-word and Arabic names | S w3c-names
- Initials background colour from a fixed set, chosen from the name, with text contrast 4.5:1 | S wcag-contrast
- Organisations use square avatars, people round, if both appear | P
## Sizes
- A few sizes from tokens (24, 32, 40, 56, 96) | S dtcg-tokens
- Images served at the right size and cropped to fill | S webdev-images
## Extras
- Status dot (online, away) with a text name, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Groups: overlapping avatars with "+5" for more, and the full list on hover or tap | P
- Upload: crop to a circle with preview; file type and size limits stated | S owasp-upload
## Behaviour
- Broken image falls back to initials | P
- Clickable avatars open the person's profile or a menu, with a clear focus state | S wcag-focus
## Access
- Avatar beside the name: decorative (empty alt) | S wai-images
- Avatar alone: alt text with the name ("Sara Ahmed") | S wcag-nontext
- Avatar buttons have names ("Account menu for Sara") | S wcag-name-role
- Status announced in words ("Online") | S wcag-name-role

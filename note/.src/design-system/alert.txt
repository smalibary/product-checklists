title: Alert
about: A message inside the page that tells people something important about the page or what they just did (also called a callout or inline message).
also: design-system/accessibility
## Kinds
- Info, success, warning and error, each with its own colour and icon | S govuk-notification
- Error alerts for page-level problems; field errors go next to fields | S govuk-error-summary govuk-error-message >flows/showing-input-error
- Not for urgent questions that need an answer now (use an alert dialog) | S apg-alertdialog >design-system/modal
- Not for passing messages (use a toast) | R nng-indicators >design-system/toast
## Content
- A short title saying what happened | S govuk-notification
- A sentence on what to do next, with a link or button if there is an action | R nng-errors
- Plain words, no blame, no codes alone | R nng-errors >design-system/content-voice
## Look
- Kind shown by icon and words as well as colour | S wcag-color
- Icon has a text name ("Error:") or the title says it | S wcag-nontext >design-system/accessibility
- Text and icon contrast meet WCAG | S wcag-contrast wcag-ui-contrast >design-system/accessibility
- Placed near what it is about, usually at the top of the content | R nng-errors
## Behaviour
- Stays until the problem is fixed or the person closes it | R nng-indicators
- Close button only for alerts that can be ignored; it has a name | S wcag-name-role
- Does not move the page under the person's hand when it appears | S webdev-cls
## Access
- Alerts that appear after an action are announced (role alert or status) | S apg-alert wcag-status
- Do not use role alert for messages that are there on page load | S apg-alert
- After a form fails, focus moves to the error summary | S govuk-error-summary

title: Date Picker
about: A control for choosing a date or a range of dates, by typing or from a calendar.
## Choosing the right control
- Known dates far away (birthday, passport expiry): three text fields or one typed field, not a calendar | S govuk-date
- Dates near today or tied to weekdays (bookings): a calendar helps | R nng-date
- A range (from, to): one calendar showing two months | R nng-date
## Typing
- A typed field is always possible, with the format shown ("DD/MM/YYYY") | S govuk-date
- Accept common formats and fix them gently | R nng-date
- Numeric keyboard on phones | S mdn-inputmode
- autocomplete bday for birth dates | S mdn-autocomplete
## The calendar
- Opens from a button beside the field with a name ("Choose date") | S apg-datepicker
- Today marked; the chosen date clearly marked by more than colour | S wcag-color
- Unavailable dates marked and explained | R nng-date
- Month and year can be changed quickly | R nng-date
- Week starts on the locale's first day (Sunday in Saudi Arabia, Monday in much of Europe) | P
- Hijri calendar shown where the audience needs it | P
## Keyboard and access
- Arrow keys move by day, Page Up/Down by month, Home/End to week start and end | S apg-datepicker
- Calendar is a dialog with a grid; focus moves in and back | S apg-datepicker
- Each day read with full date ("Tuesday 3 March 2026") | S apg-datepicker
- Escape closes without changing the value | S apg-datepicker
## Rules and errors
- Minimum and maximum dates stated | P
- Errors in words ("Date must be in the future") | S govuk-date wcag-error
- Time zone stated if the date has a time | P
- Values stored in ISO 8601 | S iso-8601
## Platform
- On phones, the native picker is often the better choice | S hig-pickers m3-date-pickers
- Direction and numerals follow the language (Arabic) | S w3c-dir

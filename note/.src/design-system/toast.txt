title: Toast
about: A short message that appears for a few seconds to confirm an action (also called a snackbar), then goes away.
## When to use
- Low-importance confirmations ("Saved", "Message sent") | S m3-snackbar
- An undo right after an action ("Deleted. Undo") | S m3-snackbar
- Not for errors that need action or anything the person must read | R nng-indicators
- Not for the result of a form submit that fails (show errors in the form) | S govuk-error-summary
## Content
- One short line; at most one action | S m3-snackbar
- The action is never the only way to do it (undo is also in a menu) | S wcag-timing
## Look
- Fixed place (bottom centre or bottom start) across the product | S m3-snackbar
- Above the tab bar on phones | S m3-snackbar
- Contrast meets WCAG | S wcag-contrast
- One at a time; later ones wait | S m3-snackbar
## Timing
- Stays long enough to read: about 4 to 10 seconds, longer with an action | S m3-snackbar
- Pauses on hover and focus | S wcag-timing
- Can be closed | S m3-snackbar
- With reduced motion, appears without sliding | S mdn-reduced-motion
## Access
- Announced by screen readers with a status region (polite) | S wcag-status
- Does not take focus from what the person was doing | S wcag-status
- Action reachable by keyboard (a shortcut to jump to it helps) | S wcag-keyboard
- Does not cover focused content or buttons | S wcag-obscured

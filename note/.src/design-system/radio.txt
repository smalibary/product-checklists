title: Radio
about: A group of round buttons where people choose exactly one option.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- One choice from a short list (2 to about 7) where seeing all options helps | S govuk-radios
- Longer lists: a select or a combobox | R nng-dropdown >design-system/select-combobox
- Two options that mean on and off: a switch or one checkbox may be clearer | R nng-toggle >design-system/toggle >design-system/checkbox
## Look
- Circles on the start side of their labels; labels clickable | S govuk-radios
- Selected state clear by more than colour (filled dot) | S wcag-color
- Circle border 3:1 contrast | S wcag-ui-contrast
- Vertical list for most cases; side by side only for two short options | S govuk-radios
- Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 | S wcag-target >design-system/accessibility
## Content
- Group has a question as its legend | S govuk-radios
- Options short, in a logical order, not overlapping | R nng-checkboxes
- Hint text under an option when needed | S govuk-radios
- Conditional questions revealed under the chosen option, kept short | S govuk-radios
## Default
- Pre-select a default only when it is safe and most people want it | R nng-checkboxes
- No default when the choice has consequences | S govuk-radios
## Access
- Native radio inputs with the same name, in a fieldset with legend | S govuk-radios
- Or role radiogroup and radio, with aria-checked | S apg-radio
- Arrow keys move and select within the group; Tab leaves the group | S apg-radio
- Error on the group, linked from the error summary | S govuk-error-message

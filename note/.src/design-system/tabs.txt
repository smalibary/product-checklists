title: Tabs
about: A row of labels that switch between views of related content in the same place.
## When to use
- Switching between related views of the same thing (Overview, Activity, Settings) | S apg-tabs m3-tabs
- Not for steps in order (use a stepper) or for main navigation of the app (use a nav bar) | R nng-tabs >design-system/stepper
- Not when people need to compare content across tabs | R nng-tabs
## Look
- Short labels, one or two words, in a single row | R nng-tabs
- Selected tab clearly marked by more than colour (underline, weight) | S wcag-color
- Tabs look connected to their content | R nng-tabs
- Icons optional; never icons alone without names | S wcag-name-role
- Too many to fit: scroll sideways with visible arrows, or a "More" menu | S m3-tabs
## Behaviour
- Switching is instant; content keeps its state when coming back | R nng-tabs
- Selected tab kept in the address when tabs are big views | P
- Swipe between tabs on phones, with taps too | S m3-tabs >mobile/gesture-navigation
- Counts or badges on tabs when useful ("Comments 3") | P >design-system/badge
## Keyboard and access
- Tab list, tab and tab panel roles; each tab controls its panel | S apg-tabs
- Arrow keys move between tabs; Tab goes into the panel | S apg-tabs
- Home and End jump to first and last | S apg-tabs
- Selected tab marked aria-selected | S apg-tabs
- Panel labelled by its tab | S apg-tabs
- Arrows flip for right-to-left | S w3c-dir >design-system/rtl-localisation
- Targets large enough to tap | S wcag-target >design-system/accessibility

title: Charts
about: Showing numbers as pictures that people read correctly.
## Choosing
- Chart type fits the question: change over time, comparison, part of a whole | R nng-charts
- Bars start at zero | R nng-charts
- Pie charts only for a few parts | R nng-charts
## Reading
- Clear title, axis labels and units | S hig-charts
- Direct labels instead of distant legends where possible | S hig-charts
- Colour not the only way to tell lines apart | S wcag-color
- Colour pairs checked for colour-blind readers | S wcag-ui-contrast
## Interaction
- Hover or tap shows exact values, also by keyboard | S wcag-hover
## Access
- A text summary or data table alternative | S wcag-nontext hig-charts

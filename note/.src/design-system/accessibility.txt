title: Accessibility (design system)
about: What a design system builds in so every screen made from it is usable by people with disabilities.
## Target and law
- Aim at WCAG 2.2 level AA for every component | S wcag
- Laws that require it: the European Accessibility Act from June 2025, the ADA in the US | L eu-accessibility-act ada-web
- Each component's page states its accessibility support and known gaps | P
## Colour and contrast
- Text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, borders and icons of controls 3:1 | S wcag-contrast wcag-ui-contrast
- Colour never the only way to show state or meaning | S wcag-color
- Focus ring visible with 3:1 contrast in every theme | S wcag-focus-appearance
- Light, dark and high-contrast themes all checked | S mdn-color-scheme
## Keyboard and focus
- Every interactive part reachable and usable by keyboard | S wcag-keyboard
- Keyboard behaviour follows the ARIA patterns (arrows in menus, tabs, radios) | S apg
- Focus order follows reading order | S wcag-focus-order
- Focused things are never hidden behind sticky headers | S wcag-obscured
- Skip link to the main content | S wcag-bypass
## Names and roles
- Native HTML elements first; ARIA only when needed | S apg
- Every control has a name, role and state | S wcag-name-role
- Icon-only buttons have a text name | S wcag-name-role
- Status messages announced without moving focus | S wcag-status
## Touch and motion
- Targets at least 24 by 24 px (44 recommended on touch) | S wcag-target hig-layout
- Dragging always has a single-tap alternative | S wcag-dragging
- Reduced motion respected; nothing flashes more than 3 times a second | S mdn-reduced-motion wcag-flash
## Content
- Images: alt text guidance in each component | S wai-images
- Forms: labels, help text and errors linked to the field | S wcag-labels wcag-error
- Language and direction set (Arabic right-to-left) | S w3c-dir
## Process
- Automatic checks in the build (axe or similar) | P
- Manual tests with keyboard and a screen reader (NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack) | P
- Tests with disabled users when possible | R nng-accessibility

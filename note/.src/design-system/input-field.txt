## Parts
- A visible label above the field, always present | S wcag-labels govuk-text-input >design-system/form-layout >design-system/accessibility
- Hint text under the label for format or examples | S govuk-text-input
- Placeholder never used as the label | S wcag-labels >design-system/accessibility
- Error message next to the field, in words | S govuk-error-message wcag-error >flows/showing-input-error
- Optional prefix or suffix (SAR, kg, @) outside the typed text | S govuk-text-input
- Character count for limited fields | S govuk-character
## Sizes
- Width hints at expected length (postcode short, address long) | S govuk-text-input
- Height and text size large enough on phones (16 px stops iOS zooming) | P
- Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 | S wcag-target >design-system/accessibility
## States
- Default, hover, focus, filled, error, disabled, read-only, each clearly different | S m3-text-fields
- Focus ring visible with 3:1 contrast | S wcag-focus-appearance
- Error state by border, icon and message, not only red | S wcag-color
- Field border 3:1 contrast | S wcag-ui-contrast
- Read-only text still selectable and copyable | P
## Helping people type
- Right type and keyboard: email, tel, url, number pads with inputmode | S mdn-inputmode
- autocomplete set (name, email, address) | S mdn-autocomplete wcag-input-purpose
- Paste always allowed | S wcag-auth
- Spellcheck off for codes and names | P
- Accept spaces and dashes in phone and card numbers | R baymard-fields >flows/making-a-card-payment
## Checking
- Check when leaving the field or on submit, not on every key | R baymard-inline
- Clear the error once fixed | R baymard-inline
## Access
- Label linked with for and id; hint and error linked with aria-describedby | S wcag-labels >design-system/accessibility
- Invalid fields marked aria-invalid | S wcag-error
- Required fields marked in words, not only with * | S wcag-labels >design-system/form-layout >design-system/accessibility
- Direction follows the typed language (dir auto) | S w3c-dir

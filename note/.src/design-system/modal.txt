title: Modal
about: A window over the page that blocks it until the person finishes or closes it (a dialog).
## When to use
- Short tasks or decisions that must happen before going on (confirm delete, quick edit) | R nng-modal
- Not for long forms, complex tasks or content people want to compare with the page | R nng-modal
- Not on page load or as a surprise interruption | R nng-modal
- Urgent confirmations use the alert dialog pattern | S apg-alertdialog
## Content
- A title that says what it is about | S apg-dialog
- Short body; scroll inside if longer | P
- Buttons named for the action ("Delete project", "Keep editing"), not just OK and Cancel | R nng-confirm
- The safe choice is clear; dangerous action styled as dangerous | R nng-confirm
## Look
- Dimmed backdrop over the page | S m3-dialogs
- Centred on desktop; full screen or bottom sheet on phones | S m3-dialogs hig-sheets >design-system/drawer
- Close button with a name in the corner | S wcag-name-role
- Width suits the content, with margins on small screens | S wcag-reflow >design-system/accessibility
## Behaviour
- Escape closes it (except where data would be lost without warning) | S apg-dialog
- Click on the backdrop closes non-critical modals | P
- Unsaved changes warned before closing | R nng-control >flows/saving-changes
- No modals on top of modals | R nng-modal
- Page behind does not scroll | P
## Focus and access
- Focus moves into the modal (first field or the title) | S apg-dialog
- Focus stays inside while open | S apg-dialog
- Focus returns to the button that opened it | S apg-dialog
- Role dialog with aria-modal and a name from the title | S apg-dialog
- The native dialog element with showModal handles much of this | S mdn-dialog
- Page behind is hidden from screen readers (inert) | S mdn-inert
- Opening and closing motion respects reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion >motion/motion-accessibility

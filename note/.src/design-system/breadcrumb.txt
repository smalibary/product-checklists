title: Breadcrumb
about: A short trail of links showing where a page sits in the site's structure, from the top down to here.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- For sites with three or more levels of pages | R nng-breadcrumbs
- Shows the place in the structure, not the person's history | R nng-breadcrumbs
- Not a replacement for the main menu | R nng-breadcrumbs
## Look
- Placed above the page title, below the header | R nng-breadcrumbs govuk-breadcrumbs
- Separators (› or /) drawn by CSS so screen readers do not read them | S apg-breadcrumb
- Every level a link except the last, which is the current page | S apg-breadcrumb
- The current page marked (aria-current="page") | S apg-breadcrumb
- Small but readable text with enough contrast | S wcag-contrast
## Long trails
- Middle levels collapse into "..." with a menu on small screens | P
- On phones, show only the parent as a "Back to X" link | S govuk-breadcrumbs
- Long names cut with an ellipsis and full name on hover and focus | S wcag-hover
## Access
- Inside a nav landmark named "Breadcrumb" | S apg-breadcrumb
- An ordered list of links | S apg-breadcrumb
- Separator direction flips for right-to-left languages | S w3c-dir
- Link targets large enough to tap | S wcag-target
## Search
- Breadcrumb structured data so search results show the trail | S google-breadcrumb

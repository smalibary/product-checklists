title: Progress indicator
about: Bars and spinners that show something is happening and how far along it is.
## Choosing
- Bar with percent when the length is known; spinner when it is not | S m3-progress hig-progress-indicators
- Nothing for waits under about 1 second | R nng-response
- Skeletons for loading page content | R nng-skeleton
## Behaviour
- Progress only moves forward | S hig-progress-indicators
- Long waits explain what is happening and allow cancel | R nng-progress
## Semantics
- progressbar role with the value, or a status message for screen readers | S apg-meter wcag-status
## Motion
- Animation respects reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion
## Look
- Enough contrast for the bar and its track | S wcag-ui-contrast

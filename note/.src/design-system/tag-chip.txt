title: Tag and chip
about: Small labels for status or category, and small buttons for filters and choices.
## Kinds
- Status tags (read only) kept apart from chips you can press | S govuk-tag m3-chips
- Filter chips toggle and show they are on | S m3-chips
- Input chips (like email addresses) have a remove button | S m3-chips
## Text
- Short, one or two words | S govuk-tag
- Meaning not given by colour alone | S wcag-color
## Interaction
- Pressable chips are buttons with a pressed state | S apg-button
- Remove buttons named ("Remove Riyadh") | S wcag-label-name
## Look
- Text contrast meets 4.5:1 | S wcag-contrast >design-system/accessibility
- Tap targets at least 24 by 24 pixels | S wcag-target >design-system/accessibility

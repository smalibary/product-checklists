title: Launch day
about: The checklist for the day a product or big feature goes public.
## Before
- Rollback plan written and tested | S sre-release
- Monitoring, alerts and on-call in place | S sre-monitoring sre-alerting >operations/monitoring-alerts
- Load expected and tested | S sre-overload
- Status page ready | P >website/status
## Content
- Launch post, help articles and release notes ready | S keepachangelog >website/changelog
- Link previews set on the launch page | S ogp >growth/link-previews
- Launch page listed (Product Hunt, community sites) following each site's rules | P ph-launch
## During
- Rollout in stages, watching errors | S sre-canary
- Someone answering questions in every channel | P ph-launch
## After
- Results against goals reviewed | R heart
- Launch again with each meaningful update | P yc-launch

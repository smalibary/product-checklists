title: Legal pages pack
about: The pages a product must publish before it goes live.
## Must-haves
- Privacy policy: what is collected, why, how long, who it is shared with | L gdpr-13 pdpl
- Terms of use | P
- Cookie notice and choices where cookies need consent | L eprivacy
## When they apply
- Refund and cancellation policy for paid products | L eu-consumer-rights ftc-negative-option
- Accessibility statement | S w3c-a11y-statement
- Imprint or company details in the EU | L eu-consumer-rights
- Security contact (security.txt) | S rfc-securitytxt
- Community rules for user content | L eu-dsa
- Notice of AI use where people talk to an AI | L eu-ai-act
## Upkeep
- Dated, with changes announced before they apply | P
- Linked from the footer and the sign-up screen | P

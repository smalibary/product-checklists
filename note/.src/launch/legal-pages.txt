- Privacy policy: what is collected, why, how long, who it is shared with | L gdpr-13 pdpl >website/privacy
- Terms of use | P >website/terms
- Cookie notice and choices where cookies need consent | L eprivacy >website/cookie-consent
## When they apply
- Refund and cancellation policy for paid products | L eu-consumer-rights ftc-negative-option >flows/canceling-subscription >flows/requesting-a-refund
- Accessibility statement | S w3c-a11y-statement >launch/accessibility-statement
- Imprint or company details in the EU | L eu-consumer-rights
- Security contact (security.txt) | S rfc-securitytxt
- Community rules for user content | L eu-dsa >support/community-forum
- Notice of AI use where people talk to an AI | L eu-ai-act >backend/ai-features

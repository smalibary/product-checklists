title: Activity Feed (mobile)
about: A scrolling list of updates in a phone app: posts, events, or what people and things did.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Items
- Each: who, what, when, with photo or icon and a short preview | P
- Times as "5 min ago" with the full date on tap or for screen readers | P
- Similar events grouped ("Sara and 4 others...") | R nng-notifications
- Tap opens the full item | P
## Loading
- Newest first; "New posts" pill at the top instead of jumping the list | R nng-infinite
- Pull to refresh | S hig-loading
- Endless scroll with a loading row; position kept when coming back | R nng-infinite
- Skeleton rows on first load | R nng-skeleton
- Images loaded small first, videos play muted only when visible, if at all | S hig-accessibility
## Control
- Filters (all, mentions, following) as tabs or chips | P
- Hide, mute, or report an item from its menu | S apple-review
- Chronological option where the order is ranked | P
## Healthy use
- No tricks to keep scrolling (fake new items, autoplay chains) | L ftc-dark-patterns
- "You're all caught up" at the end of new items | P
## Offline
- Cached items shown with a notice | P
## Access
- Each item a single readable unit with actions as custom actions | S hig-accessibility
- Autoplay can be turned off; reduced motion respected | S wcag-pause

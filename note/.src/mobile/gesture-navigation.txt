title: Gesture navigation
about: Moving around an app with swipes and other gestures, alongside the system's own gestures.
## System gestures come first
- Do not take over the system's edge swipes (home, back, notifications) | S hig-gestures android-back
- Android back gesture: use the predictive back so people see where they go | S android-back
- iOS swipe from the left edge goes back in navigation stacks; keep it working | S hig-gestures
- Content drawn edge to edge, with controls kept away from gesture areas | S android-edge hig-layout
## App gestures
- Use standard gestures with their usual meaning (tap, swipe, pinch, long press, pull to refresh) | S hig-gestures m3-gestures
- Swipe actions on list rows (archive, delete) shown with colour, icon and text | R nng-swipe
- Every gesture has a visible alternative (button or menu) | S wcag-dragging wcag-gestures
- Gestures that need two fingers or a path have a single-tap way too | S wcag-gestures
- Destructive swipes can be undone | R nng-control
## Teaching
- Hint at hidden gestures (a peek of the swipe action, a handle on sheets) | R nng-swipe
- No reliance on gestures nobody can discover | R nng-swipe
## Feel
- Movement follows the finger and can be cancelled mid-way | S hig-gestures
- Light haptics confirm a completed gesture, where supported | S hig-feedback
- Reduced motion respected for the animations | S hig-accessibility
## Access
- Works with VoiceOver and TalkBack (custom actions for swipe actions) | S hig-accessibility
- Accidental activation avoided (actions on release, not on touch down) | S wcag-cancel
- Shake or tilt never the only way to do something | S wcag-motion-act

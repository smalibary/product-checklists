title: Onboarding (mobile)
about: The first screens of a phone app after install, before the person starts using it.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Keep it short
- Get people into the app fast; onboarding as short as possible | S hig-onboarding
- Skip button on every intro slide | R nng-onboarding
- Intro slides only if the app is new in kind; 3 at most | R nng-onboarding
- Teach in context (hints when a feature is first used) rather than up front | R nng-onboarding
## Account
- Let people look around before signing up where the app allows | S hig-onboarding
- Sign up later, when needed (saving, buying) | S apple-review >mobile/sign-up
- Sign in with Apple offered if other social sign-ins are offered on iOS | S hig-sign-in-apple
## Setup questions
- A few questions only if the answers shape the experience (interests, goals) | P
- Each skippable with sensible defaults | R nng-onboarding
- Progress shown | R nng-progress
## Permissions
- Ask one at a time, at the moment it is needed, with a pre-permission screen | S hig-privacy webdev-permissions
- App works if the person says no | S apple-review
- App Tracking Transparency prompt only if the app tracks across apps | S apple-att
## Legal
- Marketing and tracking consent separate, never pre-ticked | L gdpr-7
- Age checks for apps with age limits | L coppa
## Access
- Slides work with VoiceOver and TalkBack (swipe and buttons) | S hig-accessibility >mobile/gesture-navigation

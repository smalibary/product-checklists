title: Map View
about: A map inside a phone app showing places, routes or the person's location, often with a list beside it.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Location permission
- Ask only when the map needs the person's location, with a clear reason | S hig-privacy android-permissions apple-usage
- Ask for "while using the app" first; background location only when truly needed | S android-location hig-privacy
- Approximate location accepted where exact is not needed | S android-location
- If refused: the map still works with search or a typed address | S android-permissions
## Map
- Standard gestures: drag, pinch, double tap to zoom, two-finger rotate | S hig-maps
- Zoom buttons or another way for people who cannot pinch | S wcag-gestures
- "My location" button that centres the map | S hig-maps
- Pins clear and grouped (clustered) when crowded | S hig-maps
- Selected pin highlighted, with a card showing details | S hig-maps
- Map style follows dark mode | S hig-dark-mode
## List and map together
- Toggle or bottom sheet to switch between map and list | S m3-bottom-sheets
- Moving the map offers "Search this area" instead of reloading on every move | P
- List order matches what is visible on the map | P
## Routes and places
- Distance and time shown in local units | P
- Open in the phone's maps app for directions | S hig-maps
- Arabic place names and right-to-left labels where relevant | S w3c-dir
## Speed and cost
- Map tiles cached; usage of map services counted against limits | P
- Few markers drawn at once | P
## Access
- A list view with the same places for screen readers | S wcag-info
- Pins have names; selected place announced | S hig-accessibility

title: Login (mobile)
about: Signing in to a phone app with password, passkey, code, biometrics or another account.
## Ways in
- Passkeys offered where supported | S passkeys-dev
- Sign in with Apple on iOS if other social sign-ins are offered | S hig-sign-in-apple
- Google sign-in through the system Credential Manager on Android | S android-credential-manager
- Phone number with SMS code, with the code autofilled from the message | S android-sms-retriever apple-autofill
## Fields
- Email or phone field with the right keyboard and autofill type | S apple-autofill mdn-autocomplete
- Password field works with password managers (no blocked paste) | S nist-63b
- Show password button | R nng-password
- Login button stays usable; errors after submit | S wcag-error
## Remember
- Stay signed in by default on a personal phone | P
- Face ID or fingerprint to unlock the app later, as an option | S hig-privacy android-biometric
- Tokens kept in the Keychain or Keystore, not plain storage | S owasp-masvs
## Errors and help
- One general message for wrong email or password | S owasp-auth
- Forgot password link near the field | R nng-password
- Too many tries: wait time shown, not a dead end | S owasp-auth
- Offline: say so, keep what was typed | P
## Security
- Two-step check where risk is high | S nist-63b
- New device sign-in alert by email | S owasp-auth
## Access
- Fields have visible names, not placeholders only | S wcag-labels
- Large text supported; keyboard never covers the button | S hig-layout
- Buttons at least 44 pt / 48 dp | S hig-layout m3-layout

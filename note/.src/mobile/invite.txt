title: Invite (mobile)
about: Inviting people into a shared space in a phone app (a team, household, group or document), not for a reward.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Ways to invite
- Share a link through the system share sheet | S hig-share android-sharesheet
- Type an email or phone number | P
- Pick from contacts only after the person chooses it, with a reason | S hig-privacy android-permissions
- QR code to scan in person | P
## Roles
- Choose what the invited person can do (view, edit, admin), with one-line explanations | P
- Default is the safest useful role | S owasp-authz
## Invite link
- Expiry and "reset link" to stop an old link working | S owasp-authz
- Approval by an admin optional for link joins | P
## Waiting invites
- List of sent invites with state (waiting, accepted), resend and cancel | P
- Accepted invites move to the members list | P
## The invited person
- Link opens the app (or the store, then the right screen after install) | S apple-universal-links android-app-links
- Says who invited them and to what, before they join | P
- Accept or decline | P
## Respect
- No messages sent to contacts without the person's action | S apple-review
- Contacts not uploaded or kept without consent | L gdpr-6
## Access
- Role choices as a list with radio buttons and names | S hig-accessibility

title: Splash Screen
about: What shows while the app starts, until its first screen is ready.
## Platform rules
- iOS: the launch screen looks like the first screen without content, not a branded ad | S hig-launching
- Android 12 and later: the system splash screen (icon on a colour) is shown for every app | S android-splash
- Use the platform's splash tools; do not add a second splash screen after it | S android-splash
## Content
- Only the app icon or logo on a plain background | S android-splash
- No text that needs translating, no ads, no "Loading" messages | S hig-launching
- Background colour matches the first screen, in light and dark mode | S hig-launching hig-dark-mode >design-system/dark-mode
## Speed
- Shown as briefly as possible; start-up work moved to after the first screen | S android-splash
- Kept up only while truly needed data loads, then hand off smoothly | S android-splash
- The first screen comes back to where the person left off | S hig-launching
- Slow start-ups measured and fixed (cold start under about 2 seconds is a good aim) | S android-vitals
## Motion
- Optional short icon animation (under a second) | S android-splash >motion/motion-system
- Reduced motion respected | S hig-accessibility >motion/motion-accessibility
## Edge cases
- Offline at start: show the app with cached content and a notice, not a stuck splash | P >mobile/offline >backend/caching
- Update required: a clear screen with a button to the store | P

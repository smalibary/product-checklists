title: Push Notification Opt-in
about: Asking a phone user for permission to send push notifications, and handling yes, no and later.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Timing
- Ask when the person has a reason to want alerts (after ordering: "Get delivery updates?"), not at first launch | S hig-notifications webdev-permissions
- A pre-permission screen in the app's own words first, then the system prompt only if they say yes | R nng-permissions
- The system prompt on iOS can be shown only once; a "Not now" on the pre-screen keeps that chance | S hig-notifications
- Android 13 and later need the runtime permission too | S android-notif-perm
## The pre-permission screen
- Says what kinds of alerts and how often ("Only when your order moves") | R nng-permissions
- One clear yes button and an equal "Not now" | L ftc-dark-patterns
- An example of a notification | P >mobile/in-app-notifications
## After the answer
- Yes: confirm and offer choices of topics in settings | P >mobile/settings
- No: the app works fully without it | S apple-review
- Later: ask again only at a new, relevant moment, not on every launch | R nng-permissions
- If turned off in system settings, show how to turn it back on, with a button to the settings page | S hig-notifications >mobile/settings
## iOS options
- Provisional authorization: quiet notifications delivered without asking, so the person can decide later | S hig-notifications >mobile/in-app-notifications
- Time-sensitive and critical alerts only for real needs | S hig-notifications
## Rules
- Push not used for ads unless the person opted in to marketing | S apple-review
- Marketing consent separate from service alerts | L gdpr-7
- Notification channels on Android so people can mute kinds separately | S android-notif-perm >mobile/in-app-notifications

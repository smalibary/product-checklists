title: Search (mobile)
about: Searching inside a phone app: the search field, suggestions, results and filters.
## Getting there
- Search in a tab, or a search field at the top of the main list | S hig-search m3-search
- Tapping it opens a focused search screen with the keyboard up | S m3-search
- Pull-down reveals search on iOS lists where suited | S hig-search
## Before typing
- Recent searches, with remove and clear all | R nng-search
- Popular or suggested searches | P
- Categories to browse | P
## Typing
- Suggestions after a character or two | R baymard-autocomplete
- Typo-tolerant, handles Arabic letter forms and diacritics | P
- Search key on the keyboard | S mdn-inputmode
- Clear button and Cancel (iOS) or back arrow (Android) | S hig-search m3-search
- Voice search where it helps | P
## Results
- Count of results, grouped by type if mixed | R nng-search
- Matched words highlighted with weight, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Filters in a sheet or chips above results, with counts | R baymard-filters
- Sort choice | R nng-search
- Infinite scroll keeps position when coming back | R nng-infinite
## No results
- Say so, show the words, suggest spelling fixes and other searches | R nng-empty
## Access
- Field has a name ("Search products") | S hig-accessibility
- Number of results announced | S wcag-status
- Large text supported; results rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp | S hig-layout m3-layout

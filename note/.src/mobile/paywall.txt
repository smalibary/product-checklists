title: Paywall
about: The screen that asks people to subscribe or buy to unlock content or features in a phone app.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Store rules
- Digital content and features sold through Apple and Google in-app purchase (with regional exceptions) | S apple-review play-payments
- The price, the billing period and what is included shown clearly near the buy button | S apple-review play-subscriptions >mobile/billing
- Price per period is the most prominent price; any per-week or per-month breakdown is secondary | S apple-review
- Free trial: length, what it turns into, and when the first charge happens | L apple-subscriptions ftc-negative-option
- Links to terms of use and privacy policy | S apple-review >website/privacy >website/terms
- Restore purchases button | S apple-review
## Content
- What the person gets, in 3 to 5 short benefits | P
- Plans side by side (monthly, yearly) with the saving | P
- Social proof only if true and current | L ftc-reviews
- The screen is honest about what stays free | P
## Respect
- A visible way to close the paywall; no hidden or delayed close button | L ftc-dark-patterns apple-review
- Not shown on first launch before the app has shown value, unless the app is paid-only | P
- No fake timers or "only today" prices that are not true | L ftc-dark-patterns eu-omnibus
- Not shown repeatedly after being closed | P
## After buying
- Access unlocked at once, with a thank-you | P
- Receipt from the store | S apple-subscriptions >mobile/billing
- How to manage or cancel shown in settings | S apple-subscriptions play-subscriptions >mobile/settings
## Access
- Prices read clearly by screen readers with the currency | S hig-accessibility
- Close button has a name and is at least 44 pt / 48 dp | S hig-layout m3-layout
- Large text supported without hiding the price or buttons | S hig-accessibility

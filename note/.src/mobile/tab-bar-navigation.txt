title: Tab Bar Navigation
about: The bar at the bottom of a phone app with the main sections (also called a bottom navigation bar or dock).
also: design-system/accessibility
## Content
- 3 to 5 tabs for the main top-level sections | S hig-tab-bars m3-nav-bar
- Each tab an icon and a short label, always shown | S hig-tab-bars m3-nav-bar
- Labels one word where possible | R nng-mobile-nav
- Tabs are places, not actions (no "New post" tab unless it is a clear exception) | S hig-tab-bars
- "More" tab only if really needed | R nng-mobile-nav
## Look
- Selected tab clear by filled icon, colour and label, not colour alone | S wcag-color hig-tab-bars
- Badges for counts on tabs | S m3-badges
- Bar sits above the home indicator safe area | S hig-layout android-edge
- Visible on all top-level screens; hidden only in full-screen tasks | S hig-tab-bars
## Behaviour
- Tapping a tab opens that section where the person left it | S hig-tab-bars
- Tapping the current tab again scrolls to top or goes back to its root | S hig-tab-bars
- No moving tabs around between screens | R nng-consistency
- Each tab keeps its own navigation history | S hig-tab-bars
## Platform
- iOS: tab bar; Android: navigation bar (bottom) or rail on tablets | S hig-tab-bars m3-nav-bar
- On tablets and large screens, a side rail or sidebar instead | S m3-nav-bar
## Access
- Each tab has a name and says it is selected ("Home, tab, 1 of 5, selected") | S hig-accessibility
- Labels stay readable with large text (bigger labels shown on long press on iOS) | S hig-accessibility
- Order flips for right-to-left languages | S w3c-dir

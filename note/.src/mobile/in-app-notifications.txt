title: In-App Notifications
about: Messages shown inside the phone app while it is open: a notifications list, banners at the top, and badges.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The list
- A notifications screen reached from a bell or a tab, newest first | P
- Each item: who, what, when, with a photo or icon | P
- Unread marked by a dot and bold, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Tap opens the right screen and marks it read | P
- Swipe to delete or mark read, with buttons too | R nng-swipe wcag-dragging
- Mark all as read | P
- Empty state: "You're all caught up" | R nng-empty
## Banners while the app is open
- A short banner at the top for important new things, which goes away by itself | S hig-notifications
- Does not cover the main action or the keyboard | P
- Tap opens it; swipe up dismisses | S hig-gestures
- Not shown for the conversation the person is already in | P
## Volume and control
- Similar items combined ("Sara and 3 others liked...") | R nng-notifications
- Settings to choose topics, reachable from the list | R nng-notifications
- App icon badge count matches the unread count | S hig-notifications
## Access
- New banners announced by VoiceOver and TalkBack without moving focus | S hig-accessibility
- Banners stay long enough to read, or can be found again in the list | S wcag-timing

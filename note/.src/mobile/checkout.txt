title: Checkout (mobile)
about: Paying for physical goods or services in a phone app, from cart to confirmation.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Steps
- As few screens as possible; guest checkout allowed | R baymard-guest
- Progress shown for multi-step checkout | R nng-progress
- Order summary can be expanded on each step, with the total always visible | R baymard-abandon
- Sticky bottom bar with the total and the main button | P
## Delivery
- Address from saved addresses or autofill; map pin for places without clear addresses | S mdn-autocomplete
- Delivery options with price and date | L eu-consumer-rights
- Phone number with country code picker, numeric keyboard | S mdn-inputmode
## Payment
- Apple Pay and Google Pay first where available | S hig-apple-pay webdev-payment
- Local methods (mada, STC Pay, cash on delivery) where they suit | P
- Card fields from a certified provider, card scan with the camera | S pci-dss
- Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) opened inside the app | L psd2-sca stripe-3ds
## Store rules
- Physical goods and services may use any payment provider | S apple-review play-payments
- Digital goods and subscriptions must use in-app purchase (with regional exceptions) | S apple-review play-payments
## Costs
- All fees, delivery and tax shown before the final button | L ftc-junk-fees eu-consumer-rights
- Promo code field quiet, not a big empty box | R baymard-coupon
## Submitting
- Button names the amount ("Pay 245 SAR") | S wcag-prevent
- Busy state; no double charge | S owasp-transaction
- Declined payment explained, details kept | S wcag-error
## After
- Confirmation screen with order number and tracking | S govuk-confirm
- Receipt and VAT invoice by email | L zatca-einvoice
- Offer push alerts for delivery here (see Push Opt-in) | S hig-notifications
## Access
- Keyboard never hides the active field or the button | P

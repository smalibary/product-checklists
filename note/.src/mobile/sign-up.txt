title: Sign up (mobile)
about: Creating an account in a phone app or mobile site. Started from checklist.design's 8 items, checked 2026-11-19.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Before the form
- One line saying what you get, above the form | P
- Try before signing up (guest mode) where the product allows it | R baymard-guest
- Ask for an account at the moment it is needed (save, buy, post), not on first open | R baymard-guest
- Intro slides skippable, few of them | P
- Sign up and Log in easy to tell apart | P
## Ways to sign up
- Sign up with Apple, Google or a social account | P
- If any third-party sign-in is offered on iPhone, also offer one that limits data to name and email and can hide the email (Sign in with Apple qualifies) | L apple-4.8
- Phone number sign-up with a country-code picker (strong in the Gulf) | P
- Passkey sign-up: email first, say a passkey is part of sign-up before the phone's prompt, keep a fallback | R fido-ux
- Same email from a social account links to the existing account, no dead end | P
- Sign-up stays in the app, never sent out to the web browser | L apple-deletion
## Fields
- Only the fields needed (each extra one lowers completion) | P
- Email field brings the email keyboard; phone brings the number pad | S webdev-signup
- Password manager can fill and save (marked as new password and username) | S webdev-signup
- Auto-capitalise and auto-correct off on email and username | P
- Labels stay visible; hint text alone disappears when typing | R nng-placeholder
- Return key moves to the next field and sends on the last | P
- Paste allowed everywhere, passwords and codes too | S webdev-signup nist-63b
- Spaces at the start and end trimmed | P
- "Did you mean gmail.com?" on a typo | P
- Name field accepts any name (Arabic, one word, hyphens) | P
## Password
- No confirm-password field; a show/hide button instead | R baymard-show govuk-show
- Length over symbols: no "must have a capital and a symbol" rules | S nist-63b
- Long passwords allowed (at least 64 characters) | S nist-63b
- Known leaked or common passwords refused, with a kind message | S nist-63b
- Rules shown up front and ticked as met; strength shown while typing | P
## Errors and checking
- Each field checked when the person leaves it, not while typing | R baymard-inline
- Error removed as soon as the field is fixed; a tick when it is right | R baymard-inline
- Error next to the field, saying how to fix it | R baymard-inline
- Errors never erase what was typed | P
- No-connection state keeps what was typed and offers retry | P
- Errors read aloud by screen readers | S
## Account already exists (a real trade-off)
- Helpful: "This email has an account" with Log in and Reset right there | P
- Private: same answer whether the email exists or not, details sent by email | S owasp-auth
## Legal and consent
- Terms and privacy accepted before the account is made, readable in the app | P
- Marketing opt-in separate, off by default, as easy to withdraw | L pdpl
- Age asked neutrally (type birth month and year, no "I am over 12" box) where children's rules apply | L coppa
- Tracking asked through the phone's own prompt, before any tracking (iPhone) | L hig-privacy
## The button and sending
- Button says what it does: "Create account" | P
- Loading state; cannot be pressed twice | P
- Button stays above the keyboard | P
- A disabled button says why, or it stays enabled and shows the errors | P
- Robot check only when needed, invisible first | P
- Limits on repeated tries and code sends | S owasp-auth nist-63b
## Verification
- Product usable before the email is confirmed | P
- Code fills itself from the SMS (iPhone one-time-code; Android SMS Retriever or WebOTP) | S webdev-otp
- Code boxes take a pasted code, move on by themselves and send on the last digit | P
- Resend with a countdown, plus "change email or number" | P
- "Open mail app" button on the check-your-email screen | P
- Expired code or link: clear message and one tap for a new one | P
## After sign-up
- Welcome screen pointing to the first action | P
- Signed in straight away, never "now log in" | P
- Permissions asked when each is needed, a reason screen first, no reward for saying yes | L hig-privacy
- Profile setup optional and skippable | P
- Already-have-an-account link | P
- Delete account inside the app (iPhone and Android) plus a web link (Android) | L apple-5.1.1 play-deletion
## Access and language
- Every field has a name screen readers say | S
- Contrast passes in light and dark | S
- Large text setting does not break the layout | S
- Right-to-left for Arabic, with phone numbers and codes kept left-to-right | P
- Main actions within thumb reach | P
## Edge cases
- Leaving halfway keeps what was typed | P
- Invite link kept through the whole flow; returns to where they were | P
- Works on slow mobile data | P

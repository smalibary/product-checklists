title: Permission prompts
about: Asking for camera, location, contacts or notifications at the right time.
## Timing
- Ask when the person uses the feature that needs it, not at launch | S android-permissions hig-privacy
- Explain why first, in the app, before the system prompt | S webdev-permissions android-permissions
## Asking
- The system prompt text (purpose string) is specific | L apple-5.1.1
- Ask for the least access (approximate location, chosen photos) | S android-location android-photo-picker
## After a no
- The app still works without it | L apple-5.1.1
- A way to turn it on later, with a link to settings | S android-permissions
- No repeated nagging | R nng-permissions
## Notifications
- Android 13 and later need the notification permission | S android-notif-perm

title: Account (mobile)
about: The account area of a phone app: personal details, sign-in, security, data and deleting the account.
## Details
- Name, email, phone, each editable with its own screen | P
- Changing email or phone confirmed by a code to the new one | S owasp-auth
- Current password or Face ID asked before sensitive changes | S owasp-auth
## Sign-in and security
- Change password; add a passkey | S passkeys-dev
- Two-step check on and off, with backup codes | S nist-63b
- Signed-in devices with "Sign out" each and "Sign out everywhere" | S owasp-session
- Linked accounts (Apple, Google) with unlink, if another way in remains | P
## Data
- Download my data | L gdpr-15 gdpr-20

## Delete account

- Store subscriptions: remind to cancel in the store, with a link | S apple-deletion
- Confirm with password or typed word; never a one-tap delete | R nng-confirm

- Clear Sign out at the bottom, with a confirmation | P

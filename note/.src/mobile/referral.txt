title: Referral (mobile)
about: Inviting others with a personal link or code, where both sides may get a reward.
## The offer
- What both sides get, in one line ("You both get 25 SAR") | P
- Conditions short and clear (when the reward comes, limits, expiry) with full terms linked | L ftc-endorsements
- Reward shown in the person's currency | P
## Sharing
- Personal link and code, with Copy (and a confirmation) | P
- System share sheet so people choose the app themselves | S hig-share android-sharesheet
- Ready message they can edit; no messages sent without their action | S apple-review
- Contacts access only if they choose "pick from contacts", with a reason | S hig-privacy android-permissions
## Tracking
- List of invited people and the state of each (joined, rewarded) | P
- Total earned and where to use it | P
## Store rules
- Rewards in digital credits follow store payment rules | S apple-review play-payments
- No rewards for app reviews or ratings | S apple-review
## Fairness
- Fraud checks without blocking real users | P
- Referred person told they came through a referral | L ftc-endorsements
## Access
- Code readable by screen readers letter by letter | S hig-accessibility
- Copy and share buttons named; at least 44 pt / 48 dp | S hig-layout m3-layout

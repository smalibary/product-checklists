title: Action Sheet
about: A list of actions that slides up from the bottom of the phone screen, about the thing just tapped.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to use
- Choices about one item (Share, Edit, Delete) or confirming a destructive action | S hig-action-sheets
- Android uses a bottom sheet or a menu for the same job | S m3-bottom-sheets m3-menus >design-system/drawer
- Not for long content or forms (use a full sheet or new screen) | R nng-bottom-sheet
- Not for a simple yes/no that needs explanation (use an alert) | S hig-alerts
## Content
- Optional title and message saying what the actions are about | S hig-action-sheets
- Short action names, verbs first | S hig-writing
- Destructive action styled red and placed at the top on iOS | S hig-action-sheets
- A Cancel button at the bottom, separate from the rest | S hig-action-sheets
- Few actions; long lists scroll | S hig-action-sheets
## Behaviour
- Tap outside or swipe down closes it | S m3-bottom-sheets >mobile/gesture-navigation
- Android back closes it | S android-back
- On iPad and large screens it shows as a popover next to what was tapped | S hig-action-sheets >design-system/popover
- Destructive actions can be undone, or confirmed | R nng-confirm
## Look
- Grabber handle on draggable sheets | S m3-bottom-sheets
- Dimmed background behind | S m3-bottom-sheets
- Rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp tall | S hig-layout m3-layout
## Access
- Focus moves into the sheet and returns after | S hig-accessibility
- Screen readers announce it as a menu or dialog with a title | S hig-accessibility >design-system/modal
- Large text grows the rows instead of cutting text | S hig-accessibility

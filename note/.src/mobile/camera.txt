title: Camera
about: Taking photos, videos or scanning (documents, QR codes) inside a phone app.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Permission
- Ask for camera access only when the person taps to use it | S hig-privacy android-permissions
- A clear purpose string ("To scan your receipt") | S apple-usage
- If refused: explain and offer a button to settings, plus another way (pick from photos, type the code) | S android-permissions
- Use the system photo picker to choose photos without asking for full library access | S hig-privacy android-photo-picker
## Capture screen
- Large shutter button reachable by thumb | S hig-camera
- Flash, switch camera and close buttons with names | S hig-accessibility
- Guides for scanning (a frame for documents, a square for QR) | P
- Auto-capture when a document or code is steady, with manual capture too | P
- Feedback on success (sound or haptic and a visual mark) | S hig-feedback
## After capture
- Preview with Retake and Use photo | S hig-camera
- Crop, rotate and simple fixes | P
- Upload with progress; works again if the connection drops | R nng-progress
## Privacy
- Location in photos removed unless needed and stated | L gdpr-25
- Photos stored only where the person expects; say where | L gdpr-13
- Camera stops when leaving the screen; system indicator shows when it is on | S hig-privacy
## Access
- VoiceOver and TalkBack users get spoken hints ("Document found, hold still") | S hig-accessibility
- Alternatives for people who cannot use the camera (upload, type) | S wcag-gestures
- Works in both orientations where it makes sense | S wcag-orientation

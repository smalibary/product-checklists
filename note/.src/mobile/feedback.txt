about: Asking for ratings, reviews, bug reports and ideas inside a phone app.

## Store rating
- Use the system review prompt (SKStoreReviewController / In-App Review API) | S apple-review-prompt play-in-app-review
- Ask after a success moment, never on first launch or during a task | S apple-review-prompt
- The system limits how often it shows (3 times a year on iOS); do not build your own star prompt that sends only happy users to the store | S apple-review-prompt play-in-app-review
- No rewards for ratings | S apple-review
## Bug report and ideas
- "Send feedback" in Settings and Help | P
- Short form: what happened, optional screenshot, optional email | R nng-surveys
- Screenshot shake or attach, with the chance to blur private parts | P
- Device, app version and system version attached automatically, with notice | L gdpr-13
- Thank-you and what happens next | P
## Quick in-app surveys
- One question at a time, at most 2 or 3 | R nng-surveys
- Easy to close; not shown again soon after | R nng-modal
## Replies
- Answers come back in the app or by email; the person can follow their report | P
## Access
- Ratings with buttons, not only swipes | S wcag-gestures

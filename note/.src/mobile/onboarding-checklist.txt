title: Onboarding Checklist
about: A short list of first tasks in a new account (add photo, invite a friend, turn on alerts) that ticks off as they are done.
## Content
- 3 to 6 tasks that lead to real value, most important first | R nng-onboarding
- Each task: short name, why it helps, and a button that goes straight to it | R nng-onboarding
- One task can already be done to start ("Account created") | R nng-progress
- Time to do each task, if more than a minute | P
## Progress
- "2 of 5 done" with a bar | R nng-progress
- Tasks tick themselves when done anywhere in the app | P
- A small reward or message at the end, then the checklist goes away | P
## Placement
- On the home screen as a card, can be collapsed | P
- Can be hidden or skipped; reachable again from settings or help | R nng-onboarding
- Never blocks using the app | R nng-onboarding
## Permissions in the list
- Notifications, contacts or location tasks open a pre-permission screen first | S hig-privacy webdev-permissions
- Saying no does not leave the checklist stuck: the task can be skipped | P
## Access
- Progress announced in words | S wcag-status
- Done shown by tick and word, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Rows at least 44 pt / 48 dp; large text supported | S hig-layout m3-layout

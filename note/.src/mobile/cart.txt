## Items
- Each item: photo, name, options (size, colour), price and quantity | R baymard-cart
- Quantity with minus and plus buttons; removing at zero asks or offers undo | R baymard-cart
- Remove and Save for later | R baymard-cart
- Out of stock or price changed items marked clearly at the top | R baymard-cart
- Tap item opens its product screen | P

- Subtotal, delivery, discounts, VAT and total, with delivery estimate | L eu-consumer-rights ftc-junk-fees
- Free delivery threshold progress ("Add 30 SAR for free delivery") | P
- Promo code field folded, not a big empty box | R baymard-coupon
## Actions
- Checkout button sticky at the bottom with the total | P
- Apple Pay or Google Pay express button where available | S hig-apple-pay
- Continue shopping link | R baymard-cart
## Keeping the cart
- Cart kept across sessions and devices when signed in | R baymard-cart
- Cart badge on the tab or icon with the count | S m3-badges
- Empty cart: friendly message and suggestions | R nng-empty
## Feedback
- Adding to cart confirms with a small message, haptic and badge change, without leaving the product | S hig-feedback
- Changes to quantity update the total at once | P

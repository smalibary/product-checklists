title: Dashboard (mobile)
about: The overview screen in a phone app with key numbers, status and things to act on.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Content
- Top 2 to 4 numbers that matter most, large | R nng-dashboards
- Change from the last period with arrow and word, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Things needing action at the top (due, overdue, waiting) | R nng-dashboards
- Every card taps through to its detail | R nng-dashboards
## Layout
- One column of cards; a horizontal swipe row only for secondary items | P
- Simple charts sized for a phone (sparklines, small bars) | R nng-dashboards
- Period choice (today, week, month) at the top | P
- Last updated time; pull to refresh | S hig-loading
## Speed
- Cached data shown at once, then refreshed | P
- Cards load on their own with skeletons | R nng-skeleton
- Works offline with cached numbers marked as old | P
## Personal
- Choose and reorder cards | P
- Home screen widgets for the key number (iOS and Android) | S hig-widgets
## Access
- Charts have text summaries for screen readers | S wcag-nontext
- Cards are headings or buttons with clear names | S hig-accessibility
- Large text reflows cards instead of cutting numbers | S hig-accessibility

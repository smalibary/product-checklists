title: Upgrade (mobile)
about: Moving from free to paid, or from one plan to a higher one, inside a phone app.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Moments
- Offer the upgrade when the person reaches a limit or a paid feature, saying what it unlocks | P
- A lock icon on paid features, with a short reason on tap | P
- Not interrupting a task in the middle | R nng-modal
## Plan screen
- Current plan marked; higher plans with what is added | P
- Prices with period and currency from the store | S apple-subscriptions play-subscriptions
- Yearly saving shown honestly | L eu-omnibus
- Free trial terms clear (length, price after, first charge date) | L ftc-negative-option apple-subscriptions
## Buying
- Through Apple or Google in-app purchase for digital features (with regional exceptions) | S apple-review play-payments
- Upgrade within a subscription group takes effect at once on iOS; downgrade at renewal | S apple-subscriptions
- Google Play replacement modes (charge now, prorate) chosen on purpose | S play-subscriptions
## After
- Unlocked features available at once with a thank-you | P
- Show what is new and where to find it | R nng-onboarding
- Restore purchases for other devices | S apple-review
## Respect
- Easy to close; no repeated offers right after "No thanks" | L ftc-dark-patterns
- Downgrade and cancel explained in settings | L ftc-negative-option
## Access
- Price and plan names read clearly | S hig-accessibility

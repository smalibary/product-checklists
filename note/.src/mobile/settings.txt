title: Settings (mobile)
about: The settings screen of a phone app: grouped lists of options the person can change.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Structure
- Grouped list with short section titles (Account, Notifications, Privacy, About) | S hig-settings m3-lists
- Most used first; rarely used in deeper screens | P
- Search in settings when there are many | P
- App settings in the app, not in the system Settings app (iOS) | S hig-settings
## Rows
- Each row: name, current value, and a chevron or toggle | S hig-settings
- Toggles take effect at once, no Save needed | S hig-toggles nng-toggle
- Choices with few options open a list with a tick | S hig-settings
- Short help text under rows that need it | P
- Destructive rows (Log out, Delete account) at the bottom, styled red | S hig-action-sheets
## Content
- Notifications by kind | S android-notif-perm
- Language, with Arabic and right-to-left layout | S w3c-dir
- Appearance: system, light, dark | S hig-dark-mode
- Privacy, data download and account deletion | L gdpr-15 gdpr-17 apple-deletion
- App version and build at the bottom | P
- Help, terms, privacy policy links | S apple-review
## Behaviour
- Changes saved on the device and synced to the account | P
- Settings that need the system (notifications off, location off) link to the system settings page | S android-permissions
## Access
- Toggles read their state ("Dark mode, switch, on") | S hig-accessibility
- Large text: rows grow and wrap, values move under names | S hig-accessibility

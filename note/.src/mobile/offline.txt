title: Offline
about: What an app does with no connection or a weak one.
## Data
- Recent data shown from the device when offline | S android-offline
- Changes saved on the device and sent when back online | S android-offline
- Conflicts resolved by a clear rule | S android-offline
## Telling people
- A quiet banner when offline, not an error on every screen | P
- Actions that need a connection say so | R nng-errors
- Items waiting to send are marked | P
## Behaviour
- Retries with growing waits when the connection returns | S aws-backoff
- No data lost when the app closes while offline | S android-offline
## Web
- Service worker caches the shell and key data | S mdn-service-worker

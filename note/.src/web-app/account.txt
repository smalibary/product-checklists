title: Account (web app)
about: The page where a person manages who they are in the app: name, email, password, sign-in methods, sessions and their data.
## Profile
- Name, photo and the details others see, with a preview | P
- One full-name field, any characters | S w3c-names
- Photo upload with crop, size and type limits stated | S owasp-upload
## Email and sign-in
- Changing email sends a link to the new address and a notice to the old one | S owasp-auth
- Changing password asks for the current one | S owasp-auth
- New password follows the same rules as sign-up (length, leaked-password check) | S nist-63b
- Connected sign-in methods (Google, Apple, passkeys) listed, with add and remove | P
- Cannot remove the last way to sign in | P
- Two-step sign-in status and a link to set it up | S owasp-mfa
## Sessions and devices
- Active sessions with device, place and last used | S owasp-session
- Sign out of one session or all others | S owasp-session
- Email notice on new sign-ins | S owasp-auth
## Your data
- Download a copy of your data in a common format | L gdpr-15 gdpr-20
- Delete account, easy to find, with what is deleted and when | L gdpr-17 apple-5.1.1 play-deletion
- Language, time zone and date format settings | P
## Saving and errors
- Clear Save for forms, or save on change with a "Saved" message | S wcag-status
- Unsaved changes warned before leaving | R nng-control
- Sensitive changes ask for the password or a code again | S owasp-auth
- Errors next to the field in words | S wcag-error
## Access
- Labels, autofill (name, email, new-password) and keyboard work | S wcag-labels mdn-autocomplete wcag-keyboard
- Sections with headings and a side menu for long pages | S wcag-headings

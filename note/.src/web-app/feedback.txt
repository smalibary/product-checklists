title: Feedback (web app)
about: Ways for users to tell the team what is wrong or what they want: forms, ratings, bug reports and surveys.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Where and when
- A Feedback link always in the same place (help menu or footer) | S wcag-help >website/footer
- Short in-context asks after a task is done, not in the middle of one | R nng-surveys
- Not shown again soon after it was answered or dismissed | R nng-surveys
- Scores like NPS or CSAT asked rarely and only to people with enough use | R nng-surveys
## The form
- Type of feedback first (bug, idea, question, praise) | P
- One open text box with a helpful prompt | P
- Optional screenshot, with a way to cover private parts | P
- Page, browser and app version attached automatically, shown to the person | L gdpr-13
- Contact allowed? checkbox, off by default | L gdpr-7 >design-system/checkbox
- Few required fields | R baymard-fields >design-system/form-layout
## After sending
- Thank-you message saying what happens next | S govuk-confirm
- Bug reports get a reference and updates when fixed | P >support/bug-reports
- Public idea board with votes and status (planned, done), if used | P
- Close the loop: tell people when their request ships | P
## Respect
- Never block the app until feedback is given | R nng-surveys
- Personal data in feedback handled like any personal data | L gdpr-5 >backend/data-privacy
## Access
- The form is a dialog or page with labels and errors in words | S apg-dialog wcag-labels wcag-error >design-system/modal >design-system/accessibility
- Rating scales are radio groups with words, not only stars or faces | S apg-radio wcag-nontext >design-system/radio >design-system/accessibility
- Thank-you message announced | S wcag-status

title: Import and export
about: Bringing data in from files or other tools, and taking it out.
## Import
- Supported formats listed, with a template to download | S govuk-file-upload
- A preview with column matching before anything is saved | P
- Errors listed by row and field, with a way to fix and retry | S govuk-error-summary
- Large imports run in the background with progress | R nng-progress
- A summary at the end: added, updated, skipped | S govuk-confirm
- Undo or delete the whole import | R nng-control
## Export
- Common formats (CSV, JSON, PDF) | P
- Exports of all personal data available to the person | L gdpr-20
- Large exports emailed or ready to download later | P
- CSV safe against formula injection | P
## Safety
- Only data the person may see is exported | S owasp-authz
- Exports logged in the audit trail | S owasp-logging

title: Command palette
about: A search box that opens with a shortcut (Ctrl+K) and runs any command or jumps anywhere.
## Opening
- Ctrl+K or Cmd+K, plus a visible button for people who do not know the shortcut | R nng-shortcuts
- Opens with focus in the search field | S apg-combobox
## Results
- Results update as you type, forgiving typos | P
- Grouped (pages, actions, recent) with group names | S apg-listbox
- Each result shows its shortcut where one exists | R nng-shortcuts
- Recent and suggested items shown before typing | P
## Keys
- Arrow keys move, Enter runs, Escape closes | S apg-combobox
- The active result announced to screen readers | S apg-combobox wcag-name-role
## Safety
- Only commands the person may use are listed | S owasp-authz
- Dangerous commands still ask to confirm | R nng-confirm
## After
- Focus returns to where it was when closed | S apg-dialog

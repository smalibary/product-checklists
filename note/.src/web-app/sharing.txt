title: Sharing
about: Giving other people access to a document, project or file.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Sharing
- Share by email or name, with a role (view, comment, edit) | P
- A link option with clear access (anyone with the link, or only people invited) | S owasp-authz
- Who already has access listed, with roles changeable | P
## Links
- Links can expire and be switched off | S owasp-authz
- Link addresses are random and long | S rfc-uuid
- Copy link with confirmation | S wcag-status
## Telling people
- Invitees notified with what was shared and by whom | P
- Owners see when access is requested | P
## Safety
- Sharing outside the organisation can be limited by admins | P
- Shares and removals logged | S owasp-logging
## Access
- The sharing dialog follows dialog rules (focus, Escape, labels) | S apg-dialog

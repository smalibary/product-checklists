title: List View
about: A simple list of items (tasks, files, contacts) for scanning and opening, lighter than a data table.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Items
- Each row: the name first, then two or three key facts | R nng-scanning
- Icon or photo to tell types apart | P
- Status in words or icons with names, not only colour | S wcag-color
- The whole row opens the item; actions in a menu at the end | P
- Hover shows quick actions, which are also reachable by keyboard | S wcag-hover wcag-keyboard
## Order and grouping
- Sort choice (name, date, priority), remembered | P
- Grouping by date, status or owner, with headings and counts | P
- Groups can collapse | S apg-accordion
## Finding
- Filter and search above the list | R nng-filters
- Active filters shown with remove | R nng-filters
## Acting
- Select several with checkboxes, with a bulk action bar | P
- Drag to reorder where order matters, with a keyboard way too | S wcag-dragging
- Swipe actions on touch screens, with a visible alternative | R nng-swipe
## Lots of items
- Pagination or load more, with the total | R nng-infinite
- Back keeps the scroll position | P
## States
- Skeleton rows while loading | R nng-skeleton
- Empty and no-results states | R nng-empty
## Access
- A real list (or table if there are columns) | S wcag-info
- Row links read well alone | S wcag-link

title: Public Profile
about: The page other people see about a user: who they are and what they have made or shared.
## Content
- Name, photo, short bio and role | P
- What the person has made or shared (posts, projects, reviews) | P
- Join date and simple activity numbers | P
- Links to their other profiles, marked as external | S wcag-link
- Verified badges explained on hover or tap | S wcag-hover
## Actions for visitors
- Follow, message or contact, if the product has them | P
- Share profile link | P
- Report or block the person, easy to find | L eu-dsa
## Privacy for the owner
- The owner sees what is public, with a "View as visitor" preview | L gdpr-25
- Choose what is public field by field; private by default for sensitive data | L gdpr-25
- No email, phone or exact location shown unless the owner chose it | L gdpr-25
- Hide profile from search engines option | P
- Children's profiles private by default | L coppa
## Address and search
- A readable address (/@name) that does not change silently | P
- Person structured data where useful | S schema-person
- Page title with the person's name | S wcag-title
## Access
- Photo has alt text with the name, or is decorative beside the name | S wai-images
- Headings for sections; contrast and phone width meet WCAG | S wcag-headings wcag-contrast wcag-reflow

title: Maintenance
about: What people see when the app is down for planned work or an outage, and the warnings before it.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Before planned work
- Warned ahead in the app and by email: date, start time with time zone, how long, what stops working | P
- Planned at a quiet time for most users | P
- A banner a day before and an hour before, which can be closed | P
- Listed on the status page | S atlassian-incident
## The page itself
- Says plainly that the app is down for maintenance, and that data is safe | P
- When it should be back, with time zone | P
- Link to the status page and support contact | S atlassian-incident
- Branded but light: loads without the main app's servers | P
- Returns HTTP status 503 with a Retry-After header, so search engines do not drop pages | S google-maintenance rfc-retry-after
- Checks by itself and reloads when the app is back | P
## During
- Work in progress saved before the cutover, or people warned to save | P
- Read-only mode where possible instead of fully down | P
- Sign-ins and payments never half-finished | S owasp-transaction
## After
- Notice that it is done, with what changed | P
- If it ran late, say so honestly | S atlassian-incident
## Access
- Auto reload does not move focus or surprise screen readers | S wcag-timing
- In the app's languages | P

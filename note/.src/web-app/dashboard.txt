title: Dashboard (web app)
about: The overview screen that shows the state of things at a glance and points to what needs attention.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Purpose
- Built for one audience and its questions (an operator watches, a manager reviews) | R nng-dashboards
- The few numbers that matter most at the top, largest | R nng-dashboards
- Things needing action (overdue, failing, waiting on me) shown clearly | R nng-dashboards
- Every card links to the detail behind it | R nng-dashboards
## Numbers and charts
- Each number with its period, a comparison and a direction | R nng-dashboards
- Up and down shown by arrow and word, not only green and red | S wcag-color
- Simple charts: lines for trends, bars for comparison | R nng-dashboards >design-system/charts
- Units and time zone stated; when the data was last updated | R nng-status
- Charts have exact values on hover and on keyboard focus | S wcag-hover >design-system/charts
## Layout
- A grid that reflows on smaller screens, in a sensible reading order | S wcag-reflow wcag-meaningful-sequence >design-system/accessibility
- Cards the same style and spacing | R nng-cards
- Date range and filters apply to all cards, shown at the top | R nng-filters >flows/filtering-items
- Optional: choose, hide and reorder cards; the layout is remembered | P
## States
- Skeleton cards while loading, each card loading on its own | R nng-skeleton >design-system/skeleton
- New account with no data: an empty state that helps set up | R nng-empty >web-app/empty-state
- A card that fails shows its own error and Retry; the rest still work | R nng-errors
- Live numbers update without jumping the layout | S webdev-cls
## Access
- Cards have headings; screen readers can jump between them | S wcag-headings >design-system/accessibility
- Charts have a text summary or a table | S wcag-nontext >web-app/data-table >design-system/charts >design-system/accessibility
- Auto-refresh can be paused; motion respects reduced motion | S wcag-pause mdn-reduced-motion >motion/motion-accessibility

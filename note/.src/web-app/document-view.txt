title: Document View
about: Reading and editing one document (a page, a note, a file) inside the app, often with others.
## Reading
- Title as the main heading, with owner, last edited time and by whom | P
- Contents list from headings for long documents | S wcag-headings
- Comfortable line length (about 60 to 80 characters) and line height | S butterick-line wcag-text-spacing
- Readable at 200% zoom and in dark mode | S wcag-reflow mdn-color-scheme
- Images, tables and code blocks fit the width or scroll inside their box | S wcag-reflow
## Editing
- Clear difference between view and edit mode, or edit in place with a visible cursor | P
- Saving automatic with "Saved" or "Saving..." shown | R nng-status
- Offline edits kept and synced later | P
- Undo and redo with keyboard shortcuts | R nng-control
- Formatting toolbar with named buttons, also by keyboard shortcut | S wcag-name-role
- Paste from other apps keeps simple formatting | P

- Version history | P

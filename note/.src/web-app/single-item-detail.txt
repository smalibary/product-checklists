title: Single Item Detail
about: The page for one record (a task, a contact, an order, a file) with all its details and actions.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Top
- Item name as the main heading, with its type and status | S wcag-headings >design-system/accessibility
- Main actions near the title (Edit, Share, more in a menu) | P
- Breadcrumb or back link to the list it came from, keeping the list's filters and scroll | R nng-breadcrumbs >flows/filtering-items >design-system/breadcrumb
- Previous and next item buttons when opened from a list | P
## Details
- Key facts first in a summary; the rest grouped in sections | R nng-scanning
- Labels and values in a clear two-column list | S govuk-summary
- Empty fields show a dash or "Not set", with a way to add | R nng-empty
- Dates in the person's time zone and format, exact time on hover or focus | P
- People shown with name and avatar, linking to them | P >design-system/avatar
## Editing
- Edit in place for single fields, or an Edit mode for many | P
- Changes saved with a clear message; conflicts if someone else edited | R nng-status
- Unsaved changes warned before leaving | R nng-control >flows/saving-changes
- Who changed what and when (history) | P
## Related
- Related items, comments and files in tabs or sections | R nng-tabs >web-app/comments >design-system/tabs
- Activity log of changes | P
## States
- Loading skeleton shaped like the page | R nng-skeleton >design-system/skeleton
- Not found or no permission shown in words, with a way back | R nng-errors
- Deleted items say so, with restore if possible | R nng-control
## Access
- Each item has its own address, shareable and bookmarkable | P
- Page title includes the item name | S wcag-title

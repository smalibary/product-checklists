title: Help Center
about: The self-help library of articles that answers how-to questions and fixes problems.
## Finding
- A big search box at the top, with suggestions while typing | R nng-help baymard-autocomplete >web-app/search-results
- Topics grouped by task (getting started, billing, account), with article counts | R nng-help
- Popular articles listed | R nng-help
- Search forgives typos and synonyms | R baymard-autocomplete
- No-results page offers contact and related topics | R nng-empty
## Articles
- Titles that match the task ("Change your email") | R nng-help
- Steps numbered, one action each, with screenshots kept current | R nng-help
- Short, focused articles; one task per article | R nng-help
- Last updated date | P
- Which plan or platform the article applies to | P
- Videos with captions and a text version | S wcag-captions
- Related articles at the end | R nng-help
- "Was this helpful?" with an optional comment | P
## Getting more help
- Contact support easy to find, never hidden behind forced searches | R nng-help >flows/contacting-support
- Status page link for outages | P >website/status >operations/incident-response
- Community forum link, if any | P >support/community-forum
- In-app help opens the right article for the screen the person is on | R nng-help
## Structure and access
- Breadcrumbs from topic to article | R nng-breadcrumbs >design-system/breadcrumb
- Headings in order, contents list on long articles | S wcag-headings >design-system/accessibility
- Images and GIFs have alt text; animations can be paused | S wcag-nontext wcag-pause >motion/motion-system >design-system/accessibility
- Help link in the same place on every page of the app | S wcag-help
- Available in the app's languages | P
- Articles indexable by search engines | S google-helpful

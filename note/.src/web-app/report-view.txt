title: Report View
about: One finished report: a set of numbers, charts and tables about a period or a subject, for reading and sharing.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Header
- Report title, the period covered and who made it | P
- When the data was taken, and the time zone | P
- Filters applied shown at the top | R nng-filters >flows/filtering-items
## Content
- Summary of the key findings first, in words | R nng-scanning
- Numbers with comparison to a previous period or a target | R nng-dashboards
- Charts with titles that state the point ("Sales rose 12% in March") | P >design-system/charts
- Tables with totals and subtotals | R nng-tables >web-app/data-table
- Notes and definitions for each number | P
## Sharing
- Export to PDF and spreadsheet | P >web-app/import-export
- Share link with permissions (who can see) | S owasp-authz >flows/sharing-a-link >web-app/roles-permissions
- Schedule by email (weekly, monthly) | P
- Print layout that fits pages and repeats table headers | P >web-app/data-table
## Trust
- Data sources named | P
- Partial or missing data marked | P
- Personal data hidden or grouped unless needed | L gdpr-25 >backend/data-privacy
## Access
- Headings in order so the report reads as an outline | S wcag-headings >design-system/accessibility
- Charts have a text summary or data table | S wcag-nontext >web-app/data-table >design-system/charts >design-system/accessibility
- Exported PDF tagged and readable by screen readers | S wcag
- Colour not the only way to show good or bad | S wcag-color

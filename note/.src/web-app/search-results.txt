title: Search Results (web app)
about: The page that lists what matches a search inside the app, across one or many kinds of items.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The search box
- The search box stays at the top with the words typed still in it | R nng-search
- Suggestions and recent searches while typing | R baymard-autocomplete
- A keyboard shortcut opens search (often / or Ctrl+K) | P
- The search box has a label and a search role | S apg-landmarks wcag-labels
## Results
- Number of results shown | R nng-search
- Grouped by type (people, files, tasks), with a count per group | P
- Each result shows its type, title, where it lives and a snippet with the matched words marked | R nng-search
- Matched words marked in more ways than colour (bold too) | S wcag-color
- Best match first; the order can be changed (relevance, newest) | R nng-search
- Results the person cannot open are not shown | S owasp-authz
## Narrowing
- Filters for type, owner, date and status, with a count per choice | R nng-filters baymard-filters
- Active filters shown as chips with remove | R nng-filters
- Search terms and filters kept in the address, so results can be shared | P
## No results
- Say so, show the words searched for, suggest spelling fixes | R nng-empty
- Offer to clear filters or search everywhere | R nng-empty
- Forgives typos and common synonyms | R baymard-autocomplete
## Behaviour
- Results update as filters change, and the count is announced | S wcag-status
- Back returns to the same results and scroll position | P
- Pagination or "Load more" with the total | R nng-infinite
- Fast; a loading state if it takes over a second | R nng-response
## Access
- Results are a list with headings or links that read well alone | S wcag-link

title: Kanban board
about: Cards in columns by stage (To do, Doing, Done), moved across as work goes on.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Columns
- Column name with a count of cards | P
- Work-in-progress limit per column, warned when passed | P
- Add, rename, reorder and hide columns | P
- Collapse a column | P
- Swimlanes (rows) by person, priority or project | P
## Cards
- Title, assignee photo, due date, labels and a small set of icons (comments, files) | R nng-cards
- Overdue shown by icon and word, not only red | S wcag-color
- Labels have text, not only colour | S wcag-color
- Click opens the card detail in a side panel or page with its own address | P
- Quick add at the bottom of each column | P
## Moving
- Drag a card between columns and up and down | R nng-drag
- A clear drop place shown while dragging | R nng-drag
- Every drag has another way: a "Move to" menu or keyboard keys | S wcag-dragging apg-menubutton
- Moving to Done may ask for a reason or set fields, only when needed | P
- Undo after a move | R nng-control
- Changes from others appear live without moving the card under the person's hand | P
## Finding
- Filter by person, label and due date; search | R nng-filters
- Quick "Only my cards" filter | P
- Switch to list or table view | P
## Access
- Moving by keyboard announced ("Card moved to Doing, position 2") | S wcag-status
- Columns are lists with headings; cards are list items | S wcag-info
- On phones: one column at a time, swipe or tabs between columns | S wcag-reflow
## Speed
- Smooth with hundreds of cards | S webdev-inp

## Design
- Shortcuts for the most frequent actions only | R nng-shortcuts
- Platform conventions followed (Ctrl on Windows, Cmd on Mac) | S hig-keyboards
- No clash with browser or screen-reader keys | S wcag-keyboard >design-system/accessibility
- Single-letter shortcuts can be turned off or changed | S wcag-keyboard >design-system/accessibility
## Finding
- A help screen listing all shortcuts, opened with ? | R nng-shortcuts
- Shortcuts shown next to menu items and in tooltips | R nng-shortcuts >design-system/tooltip
## Behaviour
- Shortcuts do not fire while typing in a field | P
- Destructive shortcuts can be undone | R nng-control
## Access
- Everything a shortcut does can also be done another way | S wcag-keyboard >design-system/accessibility

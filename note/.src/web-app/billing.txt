title: Billing (web app)
about: The in-app page where an account owner sees their plan, payment method, invoices and usage, and changes or cancels.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Plan
- Current plan, price, billing period and next charge date and amount | L ftc-negative-option
- Usage against limits (seats, storage, calls) with a bar and numbers | P
- Change plan, with the price difference shown before confirming | L eu-consumer-rights
- Cancel subscription, as easy to find and do as signing up was | L ftc-negative-option apple-subscriptions
- What happens on cancel: access until the period ends, data kept for how long | L ftc-negative-option
- Turn auto-renew on again after cancelling | P
## Payment
- Saved payment method (brand and last 4 digits, expiry), with change and remove | S pci-dss
- Card expiring soon warned ahead | P
- Card details collected by a PCI-certified provider, never stored by the app | S pci-dss
- Strong customer authentication when the bank asks for it | L psd2-sca stripe-3ds
- Failed payment: banner with what to do and the date access stops | P
## Invoices
- List of invoices with date, amount, status (paid, failed, refunded) and PDF download | P
- Billing details (company, address, tax ID) editable and shown on invoices | P
- Invoices meet local rules (VAT, Saudi e-invoicing) | L zatca-einvoice
- Billing email can differ from the account email | P
## Permissions
- Only owners and billing admins see and change billing | S owasp-authz
- Others see "Ask your admin" | P
## Access
- Invoice list is a real table with headers | S wai-tables
- Amounts read clearly by screen readers (currency named) | S wcag-name-role
- Confirmations for charges name the amount | S wcag-prevent

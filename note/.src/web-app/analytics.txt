title: Analytics
about: Charts and numbers that show how something is performing over time, for people to understand and decide.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Overview
- The few numbers that matter most at the top, each with change from the last period | R nng-dashboards
- Change shown as number and arrow or word, not only green and red | S wcag-color
- Date range picker with common presets (7 days, 30 days, this month) and compare to previous | P
- Time zone of the data stated | P
- When the data was last updated | R nng-status
## Charts
- The right chart for the question: lines for trends, bars for comparison, no 3D or pies with many slices | R nng-dashboards
- Axes labelled with units; y-axis starts at zero for bars | P
- Tooltip on hover and focus showing exact values | S wcag-hover
- Legend near the lines, or labels on the lines | P
- Colours that work for colour-blind people and in dark mode | S wcag-ui-contrast wcag-color
- A table view of the same data for screen readers and export | S wcag-nontext
## Exploring
- Filter by segment (country, plan, channel) | R nng-filters
- Click a chart point to see the records behind it | P
- Filters and range kept in the address, so views can be shared | P
- Save a view or report | P
## Trust
- Definitions of each number on demand ("Active user: signed in within 7 days") | P
- Incomplete periods (today) marked | P
- Data gaps or delays shown, not drawn as zero | P
## States and speed
- Loading skeletons in chart shapes | R nng-skeleton
- No data state explains why (no events yet, filter too narrow) | R nng-empty
- Slow queries show progress and can be cancelled | R nng-response
## Access and export
- Export to CSV and image | P
- Animated charts respect reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion wcag-animation
- Works at phone width (charts scale, tables scroll) | S wcag-reflow
- Personal data aggregated or anonymised where possible | L gdpr-25

title: Login (web app)
about: Signing in to the app itself, including single sign-on for teams, passkeys and staying signed in. The website login note covers the basics.
## Ways in
- Email or username, then password or passkey | S nist-63b webauthn
- Passkeys offered and filled in by the browser (autofill on the username field) | S webauthn fido-ux
- Sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, matching how the account was made | P
- Single sign-on for companies: type the work email and the company's sign-in opens | P
- Magic link by email as an option, with the code too for other devices | P
## Fields
- Labels, autocomplete username and current-password | S mdn-autocomplete wcag-labels
- Paste and password managers allowed | S nist-63b wcag-auth
- Show password button | S nist-63b
- Caps Lock warning | P
## Errors and safety
- Same message for wrong email or wrong password ("Email or password is wrong") | S owasp-auth
- Limited tries per account and per address, then slow down; no permanent lockout by others | S owasp-auth nist-63b
- Two-step sign-in after the password where turned on | S owasp-mfa
- Robot checks only after suspicious attempts, never the first time | S wcag-auth
- Sign-in from a new device emailed | S owasp-auth
- All over HTTPS, cookies Secure and HttpOnly | S owasp-session
## Staying signed in
- "Keep me signed in" for trusted devices | P
- Sessions end after a stated idle time, with a warning before | S owasp-session wcag-timing
- After signing in, return to the page the person wanted | P
- Signed out in another tab: the others notice | P
## Help
- Forgot password link next to the password field | S owasp-forgot
- Link to sign up and to help | P
## Access
- Errors next to fields and in a summary, focus moved there | S wcag-error govuk-error-summary
- Works at phone width and 200% zoom | S wcag-reflow

title: Onboarding (web app)
about: The first minutes in the app after sign-up, getting the person to their first real success.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Shape
- Lead to the first useful result fast ("aha moment"), not a tour of every feature | R nng-onboarding
- Ask a few setup questions only if the answers change what they see | P
- Each question skippable, with a sensible default | R nng-onboarding
- A progress indicator for multi-step setup | R nng-progress >web-app/multi-step-form >design-system/progress
- A checklist of first tasks that ticks itself as they are done | P
## Help along the way
- Hints at the moment a feature is first used, not all at once | R nng-onboarding
- Tours short (3 to 5 steps), skippable, and restartable from help | R nng-onboarding
- Templates or sample data to start from, easy to delete | P
- Empty states that teach (see the Empty State note) | R nng-empty >web-app/empty-state
## Team products
- Invite teammates offered after the person has done something useful | P >flows/inviting-a-teammate
- Import from other tools (CSV, competitors) offered early | P
## Respect
- No forced long tutorials or video before use | R nng-onboarding
- Permissions (notifications, location) asked only when needed, with why | S webdev-permissions >web-app/notifications
- Marketing consent separate from setup | L gdpr-7
- Onboarding state saved, so leaving and coming back continues where they were | P
## Access
- Tour popovers reachable by keyboard, with focus moved in and back out | S apg-dialog wcag-focus-order >design-system/popover
- Tour highlights do not hide the focused element | S wcag-obscured
- Animations respect reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion >motion/motion-accessibility >motion/motion-system

title: Reporting
about: The tool for building, saving, scheduling and sharing reports (the Report View note covers reading one).
## Building a report
- Start from templates for common questions | P
- Choose data, measures (sum, count, average) and grouping in plain words | P
- Preview updates as choices change | R nng-response
- Filters and date range with presets | R nng-filters
- Choose the chart type, with a sensible default | R nng-dashboards
- Calculated fields with a clear formula editor and errors in words | S wcag-error
## Saving and organising
- Name, describe and save reports into folders | P
- Mark favourites; recent reports listed | P
- Who owns each report and when it was last run | P
## Sharing and schedules
- Share with people or teams, with view or edit rights | S owasp-authz
- Scheduled email or chat delivery, with time zone and format (PDF, CSV) | P
- Recipients can unsubscribe from scheduled reports | L can-spam
- Report data follows the viewer's permissions (row-level access) | S owasp-authz
## Speed and limits
- Long reports run in the background with progress and a notice when done | R nng-response
- Large exports emailed as a link | P
- Limits stated (rows, date range) | P
## Trust
- Definitions of each measure visible | P
- Data freshness shown | R nng-status
- Personal data limited to who needs it | L gdpr-25
## Access
- Builder works by keyboard; drag-and-drop has another way | S wcag-dragging wcag-keyboard
- Charts with a data table view | S wcag-nontext
- Works at tablet width at least; reading works at phone width | S wcag-reflow

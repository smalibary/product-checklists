title: Upgrade (web app)
about: The moment and screen where a signed-in user moves to a bigger plan.
also: design-system/accessibility
## When to offer it
- At the moment of need: when a limit is hit or a paid feature is tried | P
- Say which limit was hit and what the upgrade unlocks | R nng-heuristics
- Not on every screen; never block work already done | R nng-control
- Admins see the offer; members get "Ask your admin" with a one-click request | P
## The screen
- Current plan and the suggested plan side by side | R nng-comparison
- The exact price, billing period and tax, in the account's currency | L eu-consumer-rights ftc-junk-fees
- What is charged today (prorated amount) and on the next bill | L ftc-negative-option
- When the new features start (now) | P
- Seats or usage that change the price, with a running total | P
- Saved payment method shown, with a way to change it | P
## Paying
- One confirm button that names the action and the amount ("Pay 120 SAR and upgrade") | S wcag-prevent
- Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) handled inside the flow where cards need it | L psd2-sca stripe-3ds
- Busy state on the button; no double charge on double click | P
- Card details through a PCI-certified provider | S pci-dss
## After
- Success screen and email receipt | R govuk-confirm
- New features visible at once, with a short pointer to them | P
- Downgrade and cancel paths as easy to find as the upgrade | L ftc-negative-option
## Honesty
- No pre-ticked add-ons or hidden recurring charges | L ftc-dark-patterns
- Trial-to-paid terms stated before the card is taken | L ftc-negative-option
## Access
- Focus moves to the dialog and back; Escape closes it | S apg-dialog

## Posts

- Long posts cut with "Show more" | P

- Video starts muted, with captions and controls | S wcag-captions wcag-audio

## Order and control
- Order explained (latest or recommended), with a choice of latest where possible | L eu-dsa
- Why am I seeing this, on recommended posts | L eu-dsa
- Hide, mute, unfollow and report from each post | L eu-dsa

- Infinite scroll keeps position on Back and has an end or a pause point | R nng-infinite
- Footer still reachable (or links moved elsewhere) with infinite scroll | R nng-infinite
- New posts shown as "5 new posts" button, not pushed in while reading | P
- Skeleton posts while loading | R nng-skeleton

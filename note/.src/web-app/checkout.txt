title: Checkout (web app)
about: Paying inside the app for a plan, credits or an order, from review to confirmation.
## Order review
- What is bought, quantity, price, tax, discount and total, before paying | L eu-consumer-rights ftc-junk-fees
- Recurring charges stated: amount, how often, first charge date, how to cancel | L ftc-negative-option
- Currency shown and fixed; SAR for Saudi customers | P
- Promo code field present but quiet (a link that opens it) | R baymard-coupon
- Edit the order without starting again | R baymard-abandon
## Details
- Only the fields needed; company and tax ID optional, for invoices | R baymard-fields
- Billing address only if the payment provider or tax needs it | R baymard-fields
- Autofill for name, card and address | S mdn-autocomplete webdev-payment
- Already-known details filled in | S wcag-redundant
## Payment
- Card fields from a PCI-certified provider | S pci-dss
- Card number with spaces, the brand detected, numeric keyboard | R baymard-card
- Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay) and local methods (mada, STC Pay) where they suit | P
- Strong customer authentication (3-D Secure) inside the flow | L psd2-sca stripe-3ds
- Trust signs near the card fields (lock, provider name) | R baymard-trust
## Submitting
- Button names the action and amount ("Pay 120 SAR") | S wcag-prevent
- Busy state; double click never charges twice | S owasp-transaction
- Declined card: plain reason and what to try, details kept | S wcag-error
## After
- Confirmation page with what was bought, a receipt number and next steps | S govuk-confirm
- Receipt and tax invoice by email | L zatca-einvoice
- New access available at once | P
## Honesty and access
- No pre-ticked extras; terms and refund policy linked | L ftc-dark-patterns eu-consumer-rights
- Labels, errors in words, keyboard and phone width meet WCAG | S wcag-labels wcag-error wcag-keyboard wcag-reflow

title: User Management
about: Where admins invite people, give them roles, and remove them from a workspace.
also: design-system/accessibility
## List of people
- Table: name, email, role, status (active, invited, suspended), last active | R nng-tables
- Search and filter by role and status | R nng-filters
- Sort by name or last active | R nng-tables
- Count of seats used and left | P
- Pending invites listed with resend and cancel | P
## Inviting
- Invite by email, several at once (paste a list) | P
- Choose a role while inviting, with what each role can do | S owasp-authz
- Invite links expire and can be turned off | S owasp-auth
- Domain sign-up: anyone with the company email can join, if the admin turns it on | P
- Price change shown before adding a paid seat | L eu-consumer-rights
## Roles
- Few clear roles (Owner, Admin, Member, Guest), described in plain words | S owasp-authz
- A table showing what each role can do | P
- Least privilege by default: new people get the smallest role | S owasp-authz
- Changes take effect at once, including for people already signed in | S owasp-authz
- There is always at least one owner | P
## Removing
- Remove or suspend a person, with what happens to their work (kept, moved to someone) | P
- Their sessions end at once | S owasp-session
- Confirm with the person's name in the message | R nng-confirm
- Bulk actions for many people, with a count in the confirm | P
## Safety and records
- Every change logged: who did what to whom and when | S owasp-logging
- Single sign-on and automatic user sync (SCIM) for larger plans | P
- Require two-step sign-in for all members | S owasp-mfa
## Access
- Table with headers, row actions in a menu with names | S wai-tables apg-menubutton
- Status shown in words, not only colour | S wcag-color

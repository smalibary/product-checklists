title: Settings (web app)
about: The place where people change how the app works for them, or for their whole workspace.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Structure
- Personal settings separate from workspace (team) settings | P
- A side menu of sections (General, Account, Notifications, Billing, Members, Security, Integrations) | P
- Each section has its own address so it can be linked and bookmarked | P
- Search across settings for apps with many | P
- Most used settings first; dangerous ones last in a "Danger zone" | P
## Controls
- One control type per job: switch for on/off taking effect at once, checkbox when a Save button follows | R nng-toggle
- Each setting has a label and one line explaining what it changes | R nng-heuristics
- Defaults that suit most people | R nng-heuristics
- Disabled settings say why (plan, permission) | P
## Saving
- Either save on change (with a "Saved" message) or one Save button per section, not a mix | R nng-heuristics
- Saved message announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
- Unsaved changes warned before leaving | R nng-control
- Undo, or reset to default | R nng-control
- Failed save says why and keeps what was typed | S wcag-redundant
## Dangerous actions
- Deleting a workspace or transferring ownership needs typing the name or re-entering the password | S wcag-prevent
- Say exactly what will be lost | R nng-confirm
## Access
- Side menu is navigation with the current section marked | S apg-landmarks
- Works at phone width (menu becomes a list or a drop-down) | S wcag-reflow
- Dark mode and text size settings respected | P mdn-color-scheme

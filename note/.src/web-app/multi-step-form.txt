title: Multi-step form
about: A long form split into steps (a wizard), so people answer a few questions at a time.
## Shape
- Split only when the form is long or later questions depend on earlier answers | R nng-wizards
- One topic per step; one question per page for hard or unfamiliar questions | S govuk-question
- Steps named, with "Step 2 of 5" and a progress indicator | R nng-progress
- Before starting, say how long it takes and what to have ready | S govuk-question
- A task list for long applications, so steps can be done in any order | S govuk-task-list
## Moving
- Continue and Back buttons in the same places on every step | S wcag-consistent
- Browser Back works and keeps answers | P
- Answers saved as people go; leaving and coming back continues | P
- Save and come back later, for long forms | S govuk-question
- Steps that do not apply are skipped, not shown greyed out | S govuk-question
## Checking
- Errors checked when leaving a field or on Continue, never while typing the first time | R baymard-inline
- Error summary at the top of the step, linking to each field | S govuk-error-summary
- Error message next to each field in words | S govuk-error-message wcag-error
- Answers already given are never asked again | S wcag-redundant
## The end
- A check-your-answers page with Change links for each answer | S govuk-check
- A clear final button naming the action ("Submit application") | S wcag-prevent
- Confirmation page with a reference number and what happens next | S govuk-confirm
- Confirmation by email | P
## Access
- Focus moves to the step heading after Continue | S wcag-focus-order
- Page title changes per step ("Step 2 of 5: Address") | S wcag-title
- Labels, autofill and the right keyboard on every field | S wcag-labels mdn-autocomplete mdn-inputmode
- No time limit, or one that can be extended | S wcag-timing
- Works at phone width | S wcag-reflow

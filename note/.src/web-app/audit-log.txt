title: Audit Log
about: The record of who did what, when and from where, for admins to check security and changes.
## What is recorded
- Who (person or key), what action, on which item, when, and from where (IP, device) | S owasp-logging
- Sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password and 2FA changes | S owasp-logging
- Permission and role changes, member added or removed | S owasp-logging
- Settings, billing and data export changes | S owasp-logging
- Before and after values for changes | S owasp-logging
- Admin "view as user" sessions | S owasp-logging
## Reading it
- Newest first, with exact time and time zone (ISO 8601 on export) | S iso-8601
- Filters for person, action type, item and date range | R nng-filters
- Search | R nng-tables
- Each entry opens to the full detail | P
- Plain-language descriptions ("Sara changed Ali's role from Member to Admin") | P
## Trust
- Entries cannot be edited or deleted by anyone in the app | S owasp-logging
- Kept for a stated time per plan | P
- Export to CSV and stream to outside tools (SIEM) | P
- No passwords, tokens or full card numbers ever in the log | S owasp-logging pci-dss
- Personal data in logs handled like any personal data (retention, access) | L gdpr-5 gdpr-32
## Access
- Only owners and security admins can see it | S owasp-authz
- Viewing the audit log is itself logged | S owasp-logging
- Real table with headers, works by keyboard | S wai-tables wcag-keyboard

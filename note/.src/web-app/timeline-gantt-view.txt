title: Timeline / Gantt View
about: Shows tasks as bars along a time line, with their dates, overlaps and dependencies.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Layout
- Task names in a fixed left column, bars on the right along time | P
- Today marked with a line | P
- Zoom levels: days, weeks, months, quarters | P
- Weekends and holidays shaded | P
- Grouping by project, person or status, with collapse | P
- Milestones as diamonds, distinct from bars | P
## Bars
- Bar shows start and end; label with name inside or beside | P
- Colour by status or owner, with a legend, plus text or pattern | S wcag-color
- Progress inside the bar | P
- Dependencies as arrows; conflicts (task starts before its blocker ends) flagged | P
- Critical path highlight for project plans | P
## Editing
- Drag to move, drag edges to change dates, snapping to days | P
- Every drag has a non-drag way: edit dates in a form or with keys | S wcag-dragging
- Undo after a move | R nng-control
- Moving a task offers to move its dependents | P
## Navigation
- Scroll sideways in time, jump to today, jump to a date | P
- Header with dates stays visible | P
## Access
- A list or table view with the same data, for screen readers and phones | S wcag-info
- Keyboard reaches each bar and shows its dates | S wcag-keyboard
- Tooltips on hover also show on focus | S wcag-hover
## Speed
- Smooth with thousands of tasks (draw only what is visible) | S webdev-inp

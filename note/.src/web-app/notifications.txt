title: Notifications (web app)
about: The in-app list (often a bell) of things that happened which the person may need to act on.
also: design-system/accessibility
## The bell
- An icon button with a name and the unread count ("Notifications, 3 unread") | S wcag-name-role
- Count capped (9+) and shown as a badge | R nng-indicators
- New notifications announced politely, not interrupting | S wcag-status
## The list
- Newest first, grouped by today, this week, older | P
- Each item: who, what, on which item, and when (relative time, exact on hover) | P
- Unread items marked by more than colour (a dot plus bold) | S wcag-color
- Clicking opens the item and marks it read | P
- Mark one or all as read | P
- Quick actions inline (approve, reply) for common jobs | P
- Filters: all, mentions, assigned to me | P
- Link to notification settings | P
## Volume
- Similar notifications combined ("Sara and 4 others commented") | R nng-notifications
- Only things the person cares about; let them mute a thread | R nng-notifications
- Old notifications cleared after a stated time | P
## States
- Empty state: "You're all caught up" | R nng-empty
- Loading and error states | R nng-skeleton
## Access
- The panel is a dialog or popover with focus moved in and Escape to close | S apg-dialog
- Works at phone width (full-screen panel) | S wcag-reflow

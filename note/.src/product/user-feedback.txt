## Channels
- A feedback link on every page or screen | S govuk-feedback
- Short in-product surveys at natural moments, never during a task | R nng-surveys nng-feedback
- In-app review prompts only after a success, within store limits | L apple-review-prompt play-in-app-review
## Asking well
- One or two questions, the rating scale kept the same | R nng-surveys
- An open box for "what would make this better" | S govuk-feedback
## Using it
- Feedback read on a schedule and sorted into themes | R nng-feedback
- Paired with analytics and research before acting | R nng-analytics
- Results told back to the people who gave them | P
## Measures
- A standard satisfaction or ease score tracked over time | R sus heart

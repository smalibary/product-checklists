title: Retiring a feature
about: Removing a feature or product without leaving people stranded.
## Deciding
- Usage and cost checked before deciding | S govuk-retire
- Who relies on it, and on what, listed | S govuk-retire
## Telling people
- Announced well ahead with a date and the reason | S govuk-retire
- In-product notices where the feature lives | P
- API users told with Deprecation and Sunset headers | S rfc-deprecation rfc-sunset
## Leaving
- A way to export their data | L gdpr-20 >flows/exporting-data
- An alternative suggested, or help to move | S govuk-retire
- Paid plans adjusted or refunded fairly | L eu-consumer-rights
## After
- Old addresses redirected | S gsc-redirects
- Code and data removed; data deleted on schedule | L gdpr-5

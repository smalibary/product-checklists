title: Feature requests
about: Collecting, sorting and answering what people ask the product to do.
## Collecting
- One place to send ideas, linked from help and settings | R nng-feedback
- A form that asks what they are trying to do, not just what to build | R nng-interviews
- Duplicates merged; votes or counts kept | P
## Sorting
- Each request tagged by problem, customer type and size | P
- Requests weighed against goals, not just votes | R nng-prioritization
## Answering
- Every request acknowledged | P
- Status shown (under review, planned, shipped, not planned) | P
- People told when what they asked for ships | P
## Records
- Personal data in requests handled under the privacy policy | L gdpr-5 >website/privacy >backend/data-privacy

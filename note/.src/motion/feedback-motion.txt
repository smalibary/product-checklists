title: Feedback motion
about: Animation that says success, error or "look here".
## Success and error
- Success shown briefly with a tick or colour change, then gets out of the way | R nng-microinteractions
- A small shake on a wrong code or password, with a text message too | P
- Errors never shown by motion or colour alone | S wcag-color wcag-error
## Attention
- New items or changes highlighted once, briefly | R nng-animation-usability
- Badges and dots animate in once, not forever | P >design-system/badge
- Nothing blinks; anything moving over 5 seconds can be stopped | S wcag-pause
## Reading aloud
- The message behind the motion is announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
## Evidence
- Animation helps only when it shows change people need to follow | R tversky-animation gonzalez-animation

title: Micro-interactions
about: The small animated responses to a single action: press, toggle, like, copy.
## Parts
- A trigger, a response, rules and an end state, all designed | R nng-microinteractions
- Response starts within 100 ms of the action | R nng-response
## Common ones
- Button press shows a pressed state | S m3-buttons hig-buttons
- Toggles slide and change colour together | S m3-switch hig-toggles
- Like or favourite gives a short, clear reaction | R nng-microinteractions
- Copy shows "Copied" briefly next to the button | P
- Hover and focus states animate, not jump | S mdn-transitions
## Restraint
- Delight only where it helps; never slows a repeated task | R nng-animation-usability
- Focus state always visible, even mid-animation | S wcag-focus

## Use
- Scroll effects support reading, never hide content until it animates | R nng-animation-usability
- Content readable and usable if the animation never runs | S wcag-c39
- Scroll-linked effects built with scroll-driven animation, not scroll events | S mdn-scroll-driven
## Limits
- No scroll hijacking: the page scrolls at the person's speed | R nng-animation-usability
- Parallax small and turned off for reduced motion | S wcag-animation a11yproject-vestibular >motion/motion-accessibility
- Sticky headers shrink smoothly and do not cover focused items | S wcag-obscured >website/header
## Performance
- Only transform and opacity animated during scroll | S webdev-anim-perf
- No layout shift as items reveal | S webdev-cls

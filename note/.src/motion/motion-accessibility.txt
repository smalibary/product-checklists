title: Motion accessibility

## Settings

## Harm
- Large zooms, spins and parallax avoided; they trigger dizziness | S wcag-animation a11yproject-vestibular
- Nothing flashes more than three times a second | L wcag-flash
- Auto-playing motion over 5 seconds can be paused, stopped or hidden | L wcag-pause
## Use
- No information only available while animating | S wcag-c39
- Focus is never lost or moved by an animation | S wcag-focus-order

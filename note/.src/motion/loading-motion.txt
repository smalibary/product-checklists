title: Loading motion
about: Animation that shows work in progress and makes waiting clearer.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Choosing
- Under 1 second: no indicator | R nng-progress
- 1 to 10 seconds: spinner or skeleton | R nng-progress nng-skeleton
- Over 10 seconds: a progress bar with percent or time left | R nng-progress >design-system/progress
## Skeletons
- Shapes match the real layout, so nothing jumps when content arrives | R nng-skeleton webdev-cls
- A gentle shimmer, paused for reduced motion | S mdn-reduced-motion >motion/motion-accessibility
## Progress
- Progress bars move steadily, never backwards | R nng-progress >design-system/progress
- Determinate when the length is known | S m3-progress hig-progress-indicators
## Access
- Busy state announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
- Spinners stop when done; nothing spins forever on error | P

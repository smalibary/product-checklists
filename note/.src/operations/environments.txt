title: Environments
about: The separate copies of the system (dev, test, production) and how they are kept apart.
## Separation
- Dev, test and production in separate accounts or projects | S twelve-factor aws-wa
- No production data in dev or test; fake or masked data instead | L gdpr-5
- Different secrets and keys in each | S owasp-secrets
## Sameness
- The same build runs in every environment; only settings differ | S twelve-factor
- Infrastructure described as code | S aws-wa
## Access
- Production access limited to few people, logged, and time-limited | S owasp-authz
- Test copies not open to the public, or protected by sign-in | P
## Costs
- Test copies scaled down or switched off when not used | S aws-cost
## Clarity
- The environment name shown clearly in each copy | P

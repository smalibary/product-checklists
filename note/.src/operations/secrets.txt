## Access
- Each service gets only the secrets it needs | S owasp-secrets
- Access to secrets logged | S owasp-secrets >operations/logging
## Life
- Secrets rotated on a schedule and after anyone leaves | S owasp-secrets
- Rotation without downtime (two valid during the switch) | S owasp-secrets
- Short-lived credentials where the platform offers them | S owasp-secrets
## Handling
- Secrets never printed in logs, errors or build output | S owasp-secrets owasp-logging >operations/logging
- Shared with people only through the secret store, never by chat or email | S owasp-secrets

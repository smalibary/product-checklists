title: Backups
about: Copies of data you can actually restore when something goes wrong.
## Plan
- How much data you can lose (RPO) and how long you can be down (RTO) agreed | S nist-contingency
- Point-in-time recovery for the main database | S pg-backup
- 3 copies, on 2 kinds of storage, 1 in another place | P
## Safety
- Backups encrypted | L gdpr-32
- At least one copy cannot be changed or deleted by the app's accounts | S nist-contingency
- Access to backups limited and logged | S owasp-logging >operations/logging
## Testing
- Restores tested on a schedule, timed, and written up | S nist-contingency
- A full disaster-recovery practice at least yearly | S nist-contingency
## Scope
- Files, settings and secrets covered, not only the database | S nist-contingency >operations/secrets
## Privacy
- Deleted personal data removed from backups within a stated time | L gdpr-17 >backend/data-privacy
## Watching
- An alert when a backup fails or is late | S sre-alerting >operations/monitoring-alerts

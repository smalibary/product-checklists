- One incident lead; roles for talking, fixing and writing down | S sre-incident
- Stop the harm first, find the cause later | S sre-incident
- A running timeline of what was done | S sre-incident
- Customers told on the status page early and updated regularly | S atlassian-incident >website/status
## Data breaches
- Personal data breaches reported to the authority within 72 hours where required | L gdpr-33 pdpl >backend/data-privacy
- Affected people told when the risk is high | L gdpr-33
## After
- A blameless postmortem with causes and actions | S sre-postmortem

- Lessons shared with the whole team | S sre-postmortem

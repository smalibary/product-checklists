title: Submitting a form (flow)
about: The moment a form is sent: checking, sending, and telling the person what happened.
## Before submit
- Button always active; checks run on submit | R baymard-inline
- Button names the action ("Send request"), not "Submit" | R nng-buttons
- For legal, money or data changes: review step or a way to correct | S wcag-prevent
## On submit
- All fields checked; error summary at the top with links | S wcag-error govuk-error-summary
- Focus moves to the summary or first wrong field | S wcag-focus-order
- Busy state on the button; no double send | S owasp-transaction
- Works if the connection is slow: what was typed is kept | S wcag-timing
## Success
- Confirmation page or message saying what happens next and when | S govuk-confirm
- Reference number and email copy where useful | S govuk-confirm
- Form not resent on refresh (redirect after sending) | P
## Failure
- Server error: plain message, data kept, Try again | S wcag-error
- Session expired: keep the data and sign in again | S wcag-timing
## Safety
- Server checks everything again | S owasp-authz
- Spam protection without robot puzzles that block people (honeypot, rate limits) | S wcag-auth
- CSRF protection | S owasp-csrf
## Access
- Enter submits from a text field | S wcag-on-input
- Result announced | S wcag-status
- Works without a mouse | S wcag-keyboard

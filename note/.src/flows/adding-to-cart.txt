title: Adding to cart (flow)
about: From choosing a product's options to the item being in the cart, with clear feedback.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Before adding
- Required options (size, colour) chosen first; the button says what is missing | R baymard-cart
- Price, stock and delivery estimate visible near the button | L eu-consumer-rights
- Out of stock: button replaced by "Notify me", not hidden | P
- Quantity choice where it makes sense | P
## Adding
- Button shows busy state; no double add on double tap | S owasp-transaction
- Works without signing in (guest cart) | R baymard-guest >web-app/login >website/cart
## Feedback
- Confirmation without leaving the page: a small panel or message with the item, "View cart" and "Checkout" | R baymard-cart >web-app/checkout >website/cart
- Cart count updates at once | S m3-badges >website/cart
- Message announced to screen readers | S wcag-status
- Haptic on phones | S hig-feedback
## Problems
- Failure explained ("Only 2 left") with a way forward | S wcag-error
- Price changed since the page loaded: say so | P >product/pricing-changes
## After
- Person stays where they were to keep shopping | R baymard-cart
- Cart kept across visits and devices | R baymard-cart >website/cart
## Access
- Option pickers are real buttons or radios with names | S wcag-name-role >design-system/radio
- Focus stays sensible after adding | S wcag-focus-order

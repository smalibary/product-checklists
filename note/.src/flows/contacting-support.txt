title: Contacting support (flow)
about: Getting help from a person or a bot, from the first question to an answer.
## Finding help
- Help link in the same place on every page | S wcag-consistent-help
- Search help articles first, but never hide the way to a person | R nng-help
- Ways shown with response times (chat now, email in 24 hours) | R nng-contact
## Asking
- Choose a topic, then a short form | P
- Account details and the page they came from attached automatically, with notice | L gdpr-13
- Attach screenshots | P
- Suggested articles as they type, without blocking sending | R nng-help
## Chat and bots
- Bot says it is a bot | L eu-ai-act
- "Talk to a person" always available | R nng-chatbots
- Chat history kept, transcript by email | P
- Works when the person leaves and comes back | P
## After sending
- Confirmation with a ticket number and when to expect a reply | S govuk-confirm
- Replies by email and in the app, threaded | P
- Status of open requests | P
- Rating after it is solved, short and optional | R nng-surveys
## Access
- Chat works with screen readers and keyboard; new messages announced | S wcag-status wcag-keyboard
- Phone not the only way | S wcag-consistent-help
- No time-outs that lose a typed message | S wcag-timing

## Email or phone
- Say where the code or link went, with a way to change it | R nng-errors
- Code field: one box, numbers keyboard, autofill from SMS or email (one-time-code) | S webdev-otp mdn-autocomplete
- Paste works for the whole code | S webdev-otp
- Code expiry shown; Resend after a short wait with a countdown | S owasp-auth
- Link opens in any browser and signs in or continues the flow | P
- Code entry tries limited | S owasp-auth
## While waiting
- The person can use the parts of the app that do not need verifying | P
- "Check spam" hint and a support link | P >flows/contacting-support
## Identity checks (when the law or risk needs them)
- Say why it is needed, what is collected and how long it is kept | L gdpr-13
- Steps listed first (photo of ID, selfie) with time needed | R nng-wizards
- Camera guides and clear retake | S hig-camera >mobile/camera
- Status shown (checking, approved, needs more) with notifications | P >web-app/notifications
- Another way for people who cannot use the camera | S wcag-gestures >mobile/camera
## Done
- Clear success and the next step | S govuk-confirm
- Verified mark explained where shown | P
## Security
- Codes random, short-lived and single use | S nist-63b
- No telling attackers whether an account exists | S owasp-auth
## Access
- Code field has a name and no time pressure that cannot be extended | S wcag-labels wcag-timing >design-system/accessibility
- No robot check that blocks screen reader users without another way | S wcag-auth

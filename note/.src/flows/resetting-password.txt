title: Resetting password (flow)
about: Getting back into an account after forgetting the password.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Starting
- "Forgot password?" next to the password field | R nng-password
- Email field filled with what was typed on login | P >web-app/login
- Same message whether or not the account exists ("If an account exists, we sent a link") | S owasp-forgot
## The link or code
- Random, single use, expires soon (about an hour or less) | S owasp-forgot
- Sent by email, or code by SMS where that is how people sign in | S owasp-forgot >web-app/login
- Resend after a short wait | S owasp-auth
- Link opens on any device | P
## New password
- One field with Show, not two to match | R nng-password
- Rules shown up front, checked as typed: at least 8 characters (15 if password-only), no forced mix of symbols | S nist-63b
- Common or leaked passwords refused with a reason | S nist-63b
- Password managers can fill and save (autocomplete new-password) | S mdn-autocomplete
## After
- Signed in, or sent to login with a success message | P
- Other sessions signed out, with the choice shown | S owasp-session >backend/sessions
- Email telling the person the password changed, with "This wasn't me" | S owasp-forgot
## Security
- Requests rate-limited | S owasp-auth >api/rate-limits
- Two-step check still asked if it was on | S owasp-mfa >flows/setting-up-two-step
- No security questions | S nist-63b
## Access
- Fields named; no time pressure | S wcag-labels wcag-timing >design-system/accessibility
- Copy-paste allowed | S wcag-auth

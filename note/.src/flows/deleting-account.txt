title: Deleting account (flow)
about: Permanently removing an account and its data, by the person's own choice.
also: design-system/accessibility
## Finding it
- In account settings, clearly named | S apple-deletion play-deletion
- Inside the app if accounts are made in the app (App Store); a web page too (Google Play) | S apple-deletion play-deletion
## Before deleting
- Say what will be deleted, what is kept by law and for how long | L gdpr-17
- Offer to download data first | L gdpr-20
- Active subscriptions: say they must be cancelled, with a link (store plans in the store) | S apple-deletion
- Owned shared spaces: transfer or delete them | P
- Alternatives offered once (deactivate, sign out), not pushed | L ftc-dark-patterns
## Confirming
- Password or a fresh sign-in | S owasp-auth
- Typed confirmation for big accounts ("type DELETE") | R nng-confirm
- Final button names the action ("Delete my account") in danger style | R nng-confirm
## After
- Signed out, confirmation screen and email | P
- Grace period to undo (for example 30 days) said clearly, with how to undo | P
- Data deleted from all systems and backups within a stated time | L gdpr-17
- Third parties told to delete | L gdpr-17
## Rules
- GDPR right to erasure; Saudi PDPL also gives the right to destruction of personal data | L gdpr-17 pdpl
## Access
- No time limit on the confirmation | S wcag-timing

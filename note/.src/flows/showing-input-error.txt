title: Showing input error (flow)
about: Telling people a field is wrong, and helping them fix it.
## When to check
- After leaving the field, not on every key press | R baymard-inline
- On submit, all fields checked together | S wcag-error
- Once a field shows an error, re-check as they type so it clears when fixed | R baymard-inline
- No error for an empty field the person has not reached yet | R baymard-inline
## What the message says
- What is wrong and how to fix it ("Enter a date like 21/03/2026") | S wcag-suggest
- Plain words; no codes, no blame | R nng-errors
- Near the field, under it, tied to it for screen readers (aria-describedby) | S wcag-error
## How it looks
- Red border plus an icon and text, not colour alone | S wcag-color
- Contrast at least 3:1 for the border, 4.5:1 for the text | S wcag-ui-contrast wcag-contrast
- Layout does not jump much when the message appears | P
## On submit
- Summary at the top listing errors as links to each field | S govuk-error-summary
- Focus moves to the summary or the first wrong field | S wcag-focus-order
- Page title starts with "Error:" on full page forms | S govuk-error-summary
- Everything typed is kept | S wcag-error
## Prevent errors
- Accept many formats (spaces in card numbers, +966 or 05) | R baymard-fields
- Hints before typing for format rules | S wcag-labels
- Right keyboard and autofill | S mdn-inputmode mdn-autocomplete
## Access
- Field marked invalid (aria-invalid) | S wcag-name-role
- Errors announced | S wcag-status
- Arabic and English messages in the right direction | S w3c-dir

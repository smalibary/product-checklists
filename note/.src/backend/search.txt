title: Search (server)
about: Finding records by words quickly and fairly.
## Index
- A full-text index rather than LIKE scans | S pg-fts
- Arabic and English handled: language rules, letter forms and diacritics | S pg-fts unicode-normalization
- The index updated when records change | S pg-fts
## Queries
- Typos forgiven where possible | P
- Results ranked by match and then by freshness or popularity | S pg-fts
- Filters use indexes too | S pg-indexes
- Results paged with a maximum | S google-aip-pagination
## Safety
- Each person sees only results they may open | S owasp-authz
- Query length and complexity limited | S owasp-dos
- Search input passed as a parameter, never joined into a query | S owasp-sql
## Quality
- Searches with no results logged to improve the index | P
- Slow searches measured | S pg-explain

title: File storage
about: Keeping uploaded and generated files safe, private and affordable.
## Storage
- Files in object storage, not in the database or on one server's disk | S twelve-factor
- Private by default; shared only through expiring signed links | S owasp-authz
- Encrypted at rest | L gdpr-32
## Uploads
- Type and size checked, files renamed, scanned before sharing | S owasp-file-upload
- Image metadata (like location) removed | S owasp-file-upload
## Serving
- Served from a separate domain with safe headers | S owasp-file-upload owasp-headers
- Thumbnails and sizes made once and cached | S mdn-cache-control
## Life
- Old and unfinished uploads cleaned up by rule | P
- Files deleted when their owner deletes them, including copies | L gdpr-17
- Backups of important files, tested | S pg-backup nist-contingency
## Costs
- Storage and transfer measured against the plan's limits | S aws-cost

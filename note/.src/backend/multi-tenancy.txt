title: Multi-tenancy
about: Keeping many customers in one system without any of them seeing another.
## Separation
- Every row carries its tenant id | S owasp-multitenant
- Every query filtered by tenant, ideally forced by row-level security | S pg-rls owasp-multitenant
- The tenant comes from the signed-in session, never from a field the caller sends | S owasp-multitenant
- Files and cache keys include the tenant | S owasp-multitenant
## Fairness
- Limits per tenant so one cannot slow the others | S owasp-dos
- Heavy jobs queued per tenant | P
## Admin
- Staff access to a tenant is granted, time-limited and logged | S owasp-authz owasp-logging
## Data
- Export and delete of a whole tenant | L gdpr-20 gdpr-17
- A tenant can be restored without restoring everyone | S pg-backup
## Testing
- Tests that try to read and change another tenant's data and must fail | S owasp-asvs

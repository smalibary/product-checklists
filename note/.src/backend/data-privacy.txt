title: Data privacy (server)
about: Handling personal data the way GDPR and Saudi PDPL require.
## Basics
- A list of what personal data is kept, why, where and for how long | L gdpr-30 pdpl
- A legal reason for each use | L gdpr-6 pdpl
- Collect only what is needed | L gdpr-5
- Privacy built into the design from the start | L gdpr-25
## People's rights
- Access and a copy of their data | L gdpr-15 gdpr-20
- Correction and deletion, including from backups within a stated time | L gdpr-17 pdpl
- Objection to marketing and profiling | L gdpr-21
## Security
- Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited by role | L gdpr-32
- Breaches reported within 72 hours where required | L gdpr-33
## Suppliers
- A data processing agreement with each supplier | L gdpr-28
- Transfers outside the country only under the rules | L gdpr-44 pdpl
## Risk
- An impact assessment for high-risk processing | L gdpr-35
- Saudi cybersecurity controls checked where they apply | L nca-ecc

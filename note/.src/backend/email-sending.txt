title: Email sending
about: Sending account and product emails that arrive in the inbox and follow the law.
## Domain
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for the sending domain | S rfc-spf rfc-dkim rfc-dmarc
- Gmail and Yahoo rules met: alignment, low spam rate, one-click unsubscribe for bulk mail | L google-sender yahoo-sender
- Account emails and marketing emails sent from separate addresses or streams | S google-sender
## Content
- A plain-text version with every HTML email | P
- The sender's name and postal address in marketing emails | L can-spam
## Consent
- Marketing only with consent where the law needs it | L eprivacy gdpr-6
- One-click unsubscribe header and a working link, honoured quickly | L rfc-unsubscribe can-spam
## Delivery
- Sending through a queue with retries | S aws-backoff
- Bounces and complaints handled: stop sending to bad addresses | S google-sender
- Daily caps per user and in total | S aws-cost
## Safety
- No secrets or full card numbers in emails | S pci-dss
- Links in emails expire when they sign someone in | S owasp-forgot
## Watching
- Delivery, bounce and complaint rates measured | S google-sender

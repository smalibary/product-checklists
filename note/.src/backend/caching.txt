title: Caching
about: Keeping copies of answers so repeated requests are fast and cheap, without serving wrong data.
## HTTP caching
- Cache-Control on every response: public, private or no-store | S rfc-caching mdn-cache-control
- Personal pages marked private or no-store | S rfc-caching
- ETag or Last-Modified so clients can ask "changed since?" | S mdn-etag
- Static files with content hashes in their names, cached for a year | S mdn-cache-control
## App caching
- Cache keys include everything that changes the answer (user, language, tenant) | P >backend/multi-tenancy
- A time limit on every cached item | P
- Clear invalidation when the source changes | P
- Protection against many requests rebuilding the same item at once | P
## Safety
- Never cache answers with another user's data under a shared key | S owasp-multitenant
- The app still works when the cache is empty or down | S sre-cascading
## Costs
- Expensive results (AI answers, outside lookups) cached | S aws-cost >backend/ai-features
## Watching
- Hit rate and size measured | S sre-monitoring

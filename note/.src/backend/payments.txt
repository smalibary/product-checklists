title: Payments (server)
about: Taking money safely: payment intents, webhooks, refunds and records.
## Card data
- Card details collected by the payment provider; your servers never see them | S pci-dss
- Card fields hosted by the provider keep you at the lightest PCI level | S pci-dss
## Flow
- One payment intent per order, created on the server with the amount set by the server | S stripe-payment-intents
- An idempotency key on every create call | S stripe-idempotency >api/idempotency
- Bank checks (3-D Secure) handled where required | L psd2-sca stripe-3ds
- The order marked paid only from a signed webhook, not from the browser | S stripe-webhooks >api/webhooks-receiving
## Money
- Amounts as whole numbers in the smallest unit with a currency code | S iso-4217 >backend/dates-money
- VAT added and shown correctly; e-invoices where required | L zatca-einvoice
## After paying
- Refunds through the provider, linked to the original payment | S stripe-refunds >flows/requesting-a-refund
- Disputes tracked with evidence and deadlines | S stripe-disputes
- Store subscriptions checked on the server with the store's notifications | S apple-server-notif play-rtdn
## Records
- A ledger of every charge, refund and fee, matched to the provider each day | P
- Logs never contain card numbers or full bank details | S pci-dss owasp-logging >operations/logging

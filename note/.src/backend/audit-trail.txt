title: Audit trail
about: A permanent record of who did what, for security, support and the law.
## What
- Sign-ins, permission changes, data exports, deletes and admin actions recorded | S owasp-logging >flows/exporting-data
- Each entry: who, what, which record, when, from where, and the result | S owasp-logging
- Before and after values for important changes | P
## Integrity
- Entries cannot be changed or deleted by the app | S owasp-logging
- Stored apart from normal data, with its own access rules | S owasp-logging
- Times in UTC from a synced clock | S rfc-datetime >backend/dates-money
## Privacy
- No passwords, tokens or full card numbers in entries | S owasp-logging pci-dss
- Kept for a stated period, then removed | L gdpr-5
## Use
- Customers can see their own account's trail where it helps | P
- Searchable by user, record and time | P
- Alerts on suspicious patterns | S owasp-logging >operations/monitoring-alerts

title: Concurrency
about: Keeping data right when many people or servers change the same thing at once.
## Changes
- Optimistic locking: a version or ETag must match, or the change is refused | S mdn-etag rfc-http
- Database locks for counters and stock, taken in a fixed order to avoid deadlocks | S pg-locking
- Isolation levels chosen for money and stock moves | S pg-isolation
- Unique rules prevent double creates | S pg-constraints
## Hot spots
- Busy counters split across several rows and added up when read | P
- Conflicts and retries measured as a warning sign | P
## Retries
- Transactions that fail on conflict retried a few times with a wait | S pg-isolation aws-backoff
## Distributed work
- Side effects (emails, payments) happen after the transaction commits, via an outbox | S outbox >backend/payments
- Every message handler is idempotent | S aws-idempotent >api/idempotency
## Testing
- Tests that run the same action in parallel | P

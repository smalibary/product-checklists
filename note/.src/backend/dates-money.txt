title: Dates, times and money
about: Storing and showing times, currencies and numbers correctly for every country.
## Time
- Stored in UTC; shown in the person's time zone | S rfc-datetime iana-tz
- Time zones by name (Asia/Riyadh), not by fixed offset | S iana-tz
- Text dates in RFC 3339 or ISO 8601 | S rfc-datetime iso-8601
- Hijri and Gregorian calendars shown where people expect them | S cldr mdn-intl
- Daylight saving handled for countries that use it | S iana-tz
## Money
- Whole numbers in the smallest unit or exact decimals, never floating point | S iso-4217
- The currency code stored with every amount | S iso-4217
- Rounding rules stated and applied once | P
- Exchange rates stored with the time they were used | P
## Showing
- Numbers, dates and currency formatted by locale (Intl) | S mdn-intl cldr
- Arabic-Indic or Western digits by the person's choice | S cldr
## Testing
- Tests at month ends, leap years and time-zone changes | P

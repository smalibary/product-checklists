## Article page
- A clear title and summary at the top | R nng-microcontent
- Author, publish date and update date shown | S gsc-date
- Article structured data | S google-article
- Headings, short paragraphs and a table of contents for long posts | R nng-web-writing
- Images with alt text and sizes set | S wcag-nontext webdev-images
## Listing
- Posts by topic or tag, with page-by-page lists | S gsc-links
- Related posts at the end | P
## Sharing
- Link preview tags on every post | S ogp
- An RSS or Atom feed | S rfc-atom
## Upkeep
- Old posts reviewed; out-of-date ones updated or redirected | S google-helpful gsc-redirects
- Comments, if any, moderated and marked ugc | L gsc-qualify

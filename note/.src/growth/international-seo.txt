title: International SEO
about: Serving the right language version of each page to each searcher.
## Addresses
- One address per language (folder or subdomain), never switched by cookie | S gsc-hreflang
- hreflang links between all versions, each pointing back | S gsc-hreflang
- An x-default for the language chooser | S gsc-hreflang
## Content
- Whole pages translated, not just menus | S gsc-hreflang >design-system/rtl-localisation
- Language set in the page's lang attribute | S wcag-lang
- Right-to-left pages mirrored for Arabic | S w3c-dir >design-system/rtl-localisation
- Dates, numbers and money in local formats | S mdn-intl
## Choice
- No forced redirects by location; offer a switch | S gsc-hreflang >flows/switching-language
- Each version listed in the sitemap | S gsc-sitemaps >growth/technical-seo

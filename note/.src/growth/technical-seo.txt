title: Technical SEO
about: Making sure search engines can find, read and list every page that should be found.
## Crawling
- A sitemap listing every public page, sent to the search console | S gsc-sitemaps
- robots.txt blocks only what should stay out of search | S gsc-robots
- Links are real links with addresses, not click handlers | S gsc-links
- Pages that need scripts still show their content to the crawler | S gsc-js
## Indexing
- One main address per page, marked as canonical | S gsc-canonical
- Private or thin pages marked noindex | S gsc-noindex
- Moved pages send a permanent redirect | S gsc-redirects
- Missing pages return a real 404 | S google-404
## Pages
- Short, readable addresses with words, not ids | S gsc-url
- Same content on phone and computer | S gsc-mobile-first
- Fast loading and stable layout | S webdev-vitals
- Structured data for the page type, checked with Google's test | S gsc-structured schema-org

- One clear question and one main measure chosen up front | R kohavi-book

- People split at random and keep their version | R kohavi-book
- Split checked: groups the expected sizes | R kohavi-book
- Run full weeks; no stopping early on a good day | R kohavi-book

- Results read with their uncertainty, not just the winner | R kohavi-book

CHECKBOX CHECKLIST: 20 items
A box that people tick to choose any number of options, or to agree to one thing.
Backing: 1 Rule, 16 Standard, 2 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Choosing zero, one or many from a list   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
 2. One checkbox for a yes/no agreement that takes effect on submit   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
 3. Use a switch instead for settings that apply at once   (Research: nng-toggle)
 4. Use radios when only one choice is allowed   (Standard: govuk-radios)

LOOK
 5. Box on the start side of its label; the label is clickable too   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
 6. Ticked, unticked and mixed (some children ticked) states clearly different   (Standard: apg-checkbox)
 7. Box border 3:1 contrast   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)
 8. Disabled shown clearly, with why if possible   (Practice)
 9. Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44   (Standard: wcag-target)

GROUPS
10. Grouped in a fieldset with a question as the legend   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
11. Hint text under the legend ("Select all that apply")   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
12. Vertical list, one per line, in a logical order   (Research: nng-checkboxes)
13. "None of these" option where it helps, which clears the others   (Standard: govuk-checkboxes)
14. Select all for long lists, using the mixed state   (Standard: apg-checkbox)

BEHAVIOUR
15. Ticking does not submit or change the page by itself   (Standard: wcag-on-input)
16. Errors on the group ("Select at least one")   (Standard: govuk-error-message)
17. Never pre-ticked for marketing consent   (Rule: gdpr-7)

ACCESS
18. Native checkbox input, or role checkbox with aria-checked   (Standard: apg-checkbox)
19. Space toggles   (Standard: apg-checkbox)
20. Label linked to the box   (Standard: wcag-labels)

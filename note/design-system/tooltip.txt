TOOLTIP CHECKLIST: 18 items
A small label that appears near an element on hover or keyboard focus, to name it or add a short hint.
Backing: 0 Rule, 8 Standard, 7 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Naming icon-only buttons, and short hints that are nice to have   (Research: nng-tooltip)  -> see design-system/icon
 2. Never for information people need to finish a task (put it on the page)   (Research: nng-tooltip)
 3. Never for interactive content like links or buttons (use a popover)   (Standard: apg-tooltip)  -> see design-system/popover
 4. Not on touch screens alone: they have no hover   (Research: nng-tooltip)

CONTENT
 5. A few words; one sentence at most   (Research: nng-tooltip)
 6. No repetition of the visible label   (Research: nng-tooltip)

LOOK AND PLACE
 7. Close to the element, not covering it or what it relates to   (Research: nng-tooltip)
 8. Moves to stay on screen   (Practice)
 9. Contrast meets WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
10. An arrow pointing at the element is optional   (Practice)

TIMING
11. Appears after a short delay (about 300 to 500 ms) on hover, at once on focus   (Research: nng-tooltip)
12. Stays while the pointer is over the tooltip itself   (Standard: wcag-hover)
13. Closes with Escape without moving focus   (Standard: wcag-hover, apg-tooltip)
14. Closes when focus or pointer leaves   (Standard: apg-tooltip)

ACCESS
15. role tooltip, linked from the element with aria-describedby (hint) or used as its name (aria-labelledby)   (Standard: apg-tooltip)
16. Shows on keyboard focus, not only on hover   (Standard: wcag-hover)
17. The element still has a name without the tooltip for screen readers   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
18. Disabled buttons: put the tooltip on a wrapper that can take focus, or explain on the page   (Practice)

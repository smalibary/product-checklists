DROPDOWN MENU CHECKLIST: 22 items
A list of actions or choices that opens from a button (a menu), and the related select for picking a value.
Backing: 0 Rule, 12 Standard, 5 Research, 5 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHICH ONE
 1. Menu of actions (Edit, Duplicate, Delete) opened by a button: the menu button pattern   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
 2. Picking one value in a form: a native select or a listbox, not an action menu   (Standard: govuk-select)
 3. Long lists of values with typing: a combobox   (Standard: apg-combobox)  -> see design-system/select-combobox
 4. Short choices (2 to 5) are often better as radios   (Research: nng-dropdown)  -> see design-system/radio

CONTENT
 5. Items short, start with a verb for actions   (Research: nng-dropdown)
 6. Grouped with dividers; dangerous items last and marked   (Research: nng-dropdown)
 7. Icons optional, consistent   (Practice)
 8. Keyboard shortcuts shown on the right   (Practice)  -> see web-app/keyboard-shortcuts
 9. Submenus avoided, at most one level   (Research: nng-dropdown)
10. Disabled items explained or hidden   (Practice)

LOOK
11. Opens next to its button, flips to stay on screen   (Practice)
12. Menu edge and items meet contrast; selected and focused items clear   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast, wcag-focus)  -> see design-system/accessibility
13. Item height large enough to tap   (Standard: wcag-target)  -> see design-system/accessibility
14. Width fits the longest item   (Practice)

BEHAVIOUR
15. Opens on click or Enter, not on hover alone   (Research: nng-dropdown)
16. Closes on Escape, on choosing, and on clicking outside   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
17. Focus returns to the button after closing   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
18. On phones, long menus open as a bottom sheet   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets)  -> see design-system/drawer

KEYBOARD AND ACCESS
19. Button has aria-haspopup and aria-expanded   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
20. Menu and menuitem roles; arrow keys move, Home/End jump, typing a letter jumps   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
21. Checkable items use menuitemcheckbox or menuitemradio   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
22. Button name says what the menu is for ("More actions for Invoice 104")   (Standard: wcag-name-role)

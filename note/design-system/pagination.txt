PAGINATION CHECKLIST: 10 items
The control for moving between pages of a long list.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 2 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PARTS
 1. Previous and Next with words, not only arrows   (Standard: govuk-pagination)
 2. Page numbers with the current one marked, and gaps shown with ...   (Standard: govuk-pagination)
 3. Current page marked with aria-current   (Standard: govuk-pagination)

BEHAVIOUR
 4. Each page has its own address   (Practice)
 5. Focus or scroll moves to the top of the new results   (Practice)
 6. A "View all" option when lists are short enough   (Research: nng-pagination)
 7. Infinite scroll avoided where people need to find things again   (Research: nng-infinite)

LOOK
 8. Targets big enough to tap   (Standard: wcag-target)
 9. Inside a nav landmark with a name ("Pagination")   (Standard: apg-landmarks)

ACCESS
10. Works by keyboard; link names say the page number   (Standard: wcag-link)

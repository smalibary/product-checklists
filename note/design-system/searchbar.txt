SEARCHBAR CHECKLIST: 21 items
The text box where people type to search, with its button, suggestions and clear control.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 9 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

LOOK
 1. A wide box; the width hints at query length (about 27 characters visible)   (Research: nng-search)
 2. Placed where people expect: top of the page or app   (Research: nng-search)
 3. Magnifier icon and a Search button, or Enter to search, stated   (Research: nng-search)
 4. Placeholder can hint scope ("Search orders") but is not the label   (Standard: wcag-labels)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 5. Clear (x) button once something is typed   (Standard: m3-search)

SUGGESTIONS
 6. Suggestions appear after a character or two, at most about 10   (Research: baymard-autocomplete)
 7. Suggestion text highlights what is added, not what was typed   (Research: baymard-autocomplete)
 8. Recent searches shown when empty, with a way to remove them   (Research: nng-search)
 9. Arrow keys move through suggestions; Enter picks one   (Standard: apg-combobox)
10. Typo-tolerant   (Research: baymard-autocomplete)

BEHAVIOUR
11. Scope choice if the product has several kinds of items   (Research: nng-search)
12. A shortcut opens it (/ or Ctrl+K), shown in the box   (Practice)
13. Search runs on Enter, or live after a short pause for small data sets   (Practice)
14. The query stays in the box on the results page   (Research: nng-search)

PHONES
15. Opens full screen with the keyboard ready   (Standard: m3-search, hig-search)
16. Search key on the keyboard (inputmode search, enterkeyhint)   (Standard: mdn-inputmode)
17. Cancel button returns to where the person was   (Standard: hig-search)

ACCESS
18. Search landmark (role search or the search element)   (Standard: apg-landmarks)
19. Real label, visible or for screen readers ("Search")   (Standard: wcag-labels)  -> see design-system/accessibility
20. Suggestion list follows the combobox pattern; number of suggestions announced   (Standard: apg-combobox, wcag-status)  -> see design-system/select-combobox
21. type="search" so browsers add their own features   (Standard: mdn-input-search)

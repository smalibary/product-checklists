MOTION CHECKLIST: 8 items
Animation rules: when things move, how fast, and for whom motion is turned off.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

PURPOSE
 1. Motion explains a change (where something came from or went) and is never only decoration   (Research: nng-animation)
 2. Short: about 100 to 300 ms for small changes, up to about 500 ms for big ones   (Standard: m3-motion)
 3. Easing curves from a shared set (tokens)   (Standard: m3-motion, dtcg-tokens)

SAFETY
 4. Reduced motion setting respected: swap movement for fades or nothing   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion, wcag-animation)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility
 5. Nothing flashes more than 3 times a second   (Standard: wcag-flash)
 6. Moving content over 5 seconds can be paused   (Standard: wcag-pause)

BEHAVIOUR
 7. Animations never block input   (Research: nng-response)  -> see motion/motion-system
 8. The same motion for the same job everywhere   (Standard: m3-motion)

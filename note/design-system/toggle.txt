TOGGLE CHECKLIST: 17 items
A switch that turns one setting on or off and takes effect at once.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 7 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. A setting that applies immediately (Notifications on)   (Research: nng-toggle)
 2. Not inside a form with a Save button (use a checkbox)   (Research: nng-toggle)
 3. Not for choosing between two different things (use radios or a segmented control)   (Research: nng-toggle)

LOOK
 4. Track and thumb; on and off clearly different by position and fill, not only colour   (Standard: wcag-color, m3-switch)
 5. An optional check icon inside the thumb when on   (Standard: m3-switch)
 6. Label on the start side says what is switched, not the state ("Email alerts")   (Research: nng-toggle)
 7. Track border and thumb 3:1 contrast in both states   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)
 8. Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44 to 48   (Standard: wcag-target, hig-toggles)

BEHAVIOUR
 9. Changes apply at once, with a short "Saved" message if it is saved to a server   (Standard: wcag-status)
10. If saving fails, the toggle goes back and an error says why   (Research: nng-errors)
11. If the change is slow, a busy state   (Research: nng-response)
12. Labels never change meaning with the state ("On" text can confuse)   (Research: nng-toggle)
13. Disabled toggles explain why   (Practice)

ACCESS
14. role switch with aria-checked, or a native checkbox styled as a switch   (Standard: apg-switch)
15. Space toggles; Enter may too   (Standard: apg-switch)
16. Label linked to the switch   (Standard: wcag-labels)
17. Thumb moves to the other side in right-to-left   (Standard: w3c-dir)

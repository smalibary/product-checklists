RADIO CHECKLIST: 18 items
A group of round buttons where people choose exactly one option.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 4 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. One choice from a short list (2 to about 7) where seeing all options helps   (Standard: govuk-radios)
 2. Longer lists: a select or a combobox   (Research: nng-dropdown)  -> see design-system/select-combobox
 3. Two options that mean on and off: a switch or one checkbox may be clearer   (Research: nng-toggle)  -> see design-system/toggle, design-system/checkbox

LOOK
 4. Circles on the start side of their labels; labels clickable   (Standard: govuk-radios)
 5. Selected state clear by more than colour (filled dot)   (Standard: wcag-color)
 6. Circle border 3:1 contrast   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)
 7. Vertical list for most cases; side by side only for two short options   (Standard: govuk-radios)
 8. Touch area at least 24 px, ideally 44   (Standard: wcag-target)  -> see design-system/accessibility

CONTENT
 9. Group has a question as its legend   (Standard: govuk-radios)
10. Options short, in a logical order, not overlapping   (Research: nng-checkboxes)
11. Hint text under an option when needed   (Standard: govuk-radios)
12. Conditional questions revealed under the chosen option, kept short   (Standard: govuk-radios)

DEFAULT
13. Pre-select a default only when it is safe and most people want it   (Research: nng-checkboxes)
14. No default when the choice has consequences   (Standard: govuk-radios)

ACCESS
15. Native radio inputs with the same name, in a fieldset with legend   (Standard: govuk-radios)
16. Or role radiogroup and radio, with aria-checked   (Standard: apg-radio)
17. Arrow keys move and select within the group; Tab leaves the group   (Standard: apg-radio)
18. Error on the group, linked from the error summary   (Standard: govuk-error-message)

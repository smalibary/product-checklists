POPOVER CHECKLIST: 9 items
A small floating panel tied to a button, for extra options or details.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

USE
 1. For short, non-blocking content; use a dialog when a decision is needed   (Standard: hig-popovers)
 2. Not used for content people must read to continue   (Standard: hig-popovers)

BEHAVIOUR
 3. Opens on click or tap, not on hover alone   (Standard: wcag-hover)
 4. Closes on Escape, on outside click and when the trigger is pressed again   (Standard: mdn-popover)
 5. Focus moves into it when it has controls, and back to the trigger when closed   (Standard: apg-dialog)
 6. Stays on screen at small sizes; repositions near edges   (Standard: wcag-reflow)

SEMANTICS
 7. The trigger says it opens something (aria-expanded)   (Standard: apg-disclosure)
 8. Built on the browser's popover feature where possible   (Standard: mdn-popover)

LOOK
 9. Clear edge and shadow, arrow pointing at its trigger   (Standard: m3-elevation)

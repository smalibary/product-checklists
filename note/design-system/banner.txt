BANNER CHECKLIST: 18 items
A full-width message across the top of the app or page, about something that affects the whole product (outage, trial ending, cookies).
Backing: 2 Rule, 12 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. For product-wide news or problems that persist (trial ends, payment failed, read-only mode)   (Standard: m3-banner)
 2. One banner at a time; more wait in line   (Standard: m3-banner)
 3. Not for messages about one action (use a toast) or one section (use an alert)   (Research: nng-indicators)  -> see design-system/toast

CONTENT
 4. One short sentence on what is happening   (Standard: govuk-notification)
 5. One or two actions (Update payment, Learn more)   (Standard: m3-banner)
 6. Dates and times with time zone where relevant   (Practice)

LOOK
 7. Kind shown by colour, icon and words (info, warning, error)   (Standard: wcag-color)
 8. Contrast for text and buttons meets WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 9. Stays at the top of the content, under the header   (Standard: m3-banner)

BEHAVIOUR
10. Can be closed if not critical; closed stays closed for that message   (Practice)
11. Critical banners (account suspended) cannot be closed until fixed   (Practice)
12. Does not cover content; the page moves down once, not while reading   (Standard: webdev-cls)
13. Does not cover focused items when sticky   (Standard: wcag-obscured)

ACCESS
14. Region with a name, or role status if it appears after load   (Standard: wcag-status)
15. Close button has a name ("Dismiss trial notice")   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
16. Reachable early in the tab order   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)

COOKIE BANNER
17. Accept and Reject equally easy, no pre-ticked boxes   (Rule: eprivacy, gdpr-7)
18. Settings link for choices by purpose   (Rule: eprivacy)

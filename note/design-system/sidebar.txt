SIDEBAR NAVIGATION CHECKLIST: 9 items
The side menu in apps for moving between sections.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

STRUCTURE
 1. Sections grouped with headings   (Research: nng-vertical-nav)
 2. Icons with text labels; icons alone only when collapsed, with tooltips   (Research: nng-icons)  -> see design-system/tooltip
 3. Current page marked   (Research: nng-indicators)

BEHAVIOUR
 4. Collapsible, remembering the choice   (Research: nng-vertical-nav)
 5. Nested items expand in place   (Standard: apg-disclosure)
 6. Scrolls on its own when long   (Practice)

PHONES
 7. Becomes a drawer on small screens   (Standard: m3-nav-drawer)  -> see design-system/drawer

SEMANTICS
 8. nav landmark; current link with aria-current   (Standard: apg-landmarks)

DIRECTION
 9. Moves to the right side in Arabic layouts   (Standard: w3c-dir, hig-right-to-left)

DATE PICKER CHECKLIST: 23 items
A control for choosing a date or a range of dates, by typing or from a calendar.
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 5 Research, 4 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING THE RIGHT CONTROL
 1. Known dates far away (birthday, passport expiry): three text fields or one typed field, not a calendar   (Standard: govuk-date)
 2. Dates near today or tied to weekdays (bookings): a calendar helps   (Research: nng-date)
 3. A range (from, to): one calendar showing two months   (Research: nng-date)

TYPING
 4. A typed field is always possible, with the format shown ("DD/MM/YYYY")   (Standard: govuk-date)
 5. Accept common formats and fix them gently   (Research: nng-date)
 6. Numeric keyboard on phones   (Standard: mdn-inputmode)
 7. autocomplete bday for birth dates   (Standard: mdn-autocomplete)

THE CALENDAR
 8. Opens from a button beside the field with a name ("Choose date")   (Standard: apg-datepicker)
 9. Today marked; the chosen date clearly marked by more than colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
10. Unavailable dates marked and explained   (Research: nng-date)
11. Month and year can be changed quickly   (Research: nng-date)
12. Week starts on the locale's first day (Sunday in Saudi Arabia, Monday in much of Europe)   (Practice)
13. Hijri calendar shown where the audience needs it   (Practice)

KEYBOARD AND ACCESS
14. Arrow keys move by day, Page Up/Down by month, Home/End to week start and end   (Standard: apg-datepicker)
15. Calendar is a dialog with a grid; focus moves in and back   (Standard: apg-datepicker)  -> see design-system/modal
16. Each day read with full date ("Tuesday 3 March 2026")   (Standard: apg-datepicker)
17. Escape closes without changing the value   (Standard: apg-datepicker)

RULES AND ERRORS
18. Minimum and maximum dates stated   (Practice)
19. Errors in words ("Date must be in the future")   (Standard: govuk-date, wcag-error)
20. Time zone stated if the date has a time   (Practice)
21. Values stored in ISO 8601   (Standard: iso-8601)

PLATFORM
22. On phones, the native picker is often the better choice   (Standard: hig-pickers, m3-date-pickers)
23. Direction and numerals follow the language (Arabic)   (Standard: w3c-dir)

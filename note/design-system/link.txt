LINK CHECKLIST: 9 items
Text that takes people to another page or place.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 2 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

LOOK
 1. Looks like a link: underlined or otherwise clear, not by colour alone   (Research: nng-links)
 2. Visited links look different where it helps   (Research: nng-links)
 3. Focus style clear   (Standard: wcag-focus)

WORDS
 4. Link text says where it goes; no "click here"   (Standard: wcag-link)
 5. Links to files say type and size   (Standard: wcag-link)

BEHAVIOUR
 6. Opens in the same tab unless there is a strong reason; new tabs announced   (Standard: wcag-on-input)
 7. Links go to places; buttons do actions   (Standard: apg-button)

LOOK
 8. Contrast against text and background   (Standard: wcag-contrast)
 9. Big enough to tap on phones   (Standard: wcag-target)

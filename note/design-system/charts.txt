CHARTS CHECKLIST: 9 items
Showing numbers as pictures that people read correctly.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 3 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Chart type fits the question: change over time, comparison, part of a whole   (Research: nng-charts)
 2. Bars start at zero   (Research: nng-charts)
 3. Pie charts only for a few parts   (Research: nng-charts)

READING
 4. Clear title, axis labels and units   (Standard: hig-charts)
 5. Direct labels instead of distant legends where possible   (Standard: hig-charts)
 6. Colour not the only way to tell lines apart   (Standard: wcag-color)
 7. Colour pairs checked for colour-blind readers   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)

INTERACTION
 8. Hover or tap shows exact values, also by keyboard   (Standard: wcag-hover)

ACCESS
 9. A text summary or data table alternative   (Standard: wcag-nontext, hig-charts)

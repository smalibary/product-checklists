TABS CHECKLIST: 19 items
A row of labels that switch between views of related content in the same place.
Backing: 0 Rule, 12 Standard, 5 Research, 2 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Switching between related views of the same thing (Overview, Activity, Settings)   (Standard: apg-tabs, m3-tabs)
 2. Not for steps in order (use a stepper) or for main navigation of the app (use a nav bar)   (Research: nng-tabs)  -> see design-system/stepper
 3. Not when people need to compare content across tabs   (Research: nng-tabs)

LOOK
 4. Short labels, one or two words, in a single row   (Research: nng-tabs)
 5. Selected tab clearly marked by more than colour (underline, weight)   (Standard: wcag-color)
 6. Tabs look connected to their content   (Research: nng-tabs)
 7. Icons optional; never icons alone without names   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
 8. Too many to fit: scroll sideways with visible arrows, or a "More" menu   (Standard: m3-tabs)

BEHAVIOUR
 9. Switching is instant; content keeps its state when coming back   (Research: nng-tabs)
10. Selected tab kept in the address when tabs are big views   (Practice)
11. Swipe between tabs on phones, with taps too   (Standard: m3-tabs)  -> see mobile/gesture-navigation
12. Counts or badges on tabs when useful ("Comments 3")   (Practice)  -> see design-system/badge

KEYBOARD AND ACCESS
13. Tab list, tab and tab panel roles; each tab controls its panel   (Standard: apg-tabs)
14. Arrow keys move between tabs; Tab goes into the panel   (Standard: apg-tabs)
15. Home and End jump to first and last   (Standard: apg-tabs)
16. Selected tab marked aria-selected   (Standard: apg-tabs)
17. Panel labelled by its tab   (Standard: apg-tabs)
18. Arrows flip for right-to-left   (Standard: w3c-dir)  -> see design-system/rtl-localisation
19. Targets large enough to tap   (Standard: wcag-target)  -> see design-system/accessibility

RIGHT-TO-LEFT AND LANGUAGES CHECKLIST: 11 items
Making screens work in Arabic and other languages, not only English.
Backing: 0 Rule, 10 Standard, 0 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

DIRECTION
 1. The page direction set (dir=rtl) and the language marked   (Standard: w3c-dir, wcag-lang)
 2. Logical CSS properties (start and end, not left and right)   (Standard: mdn-logical)
 3. Layouts, icons with direction and progress flip; clocks, media controls and logos do not   (Standard: hig-right-to-left, android-rtl)
 4. Mixed Arabic and English text isolated so punctuation lands in the right place   (Standard: w3c-dir)

TEXT
 5. Arabic fonts with proper shaping and enough line height   (Standard: hig-typography)
 6. No text in images   (Standard: wcag-images-text)
 7. Room for longer text in other languages   (Standard: w3c-forms-i18n)

FORMATS
 8. Dates, numbers, digits and currency by locale   (Standard: cldr, mdn-intl)
 9. Hijri dates where expected   (Standard: cldr)
10. Names and addresses fields flexible for every country   (Standard: w3c-names)

TESTING
11. Every screen checked in Arabic, not only translated strings   (Practice)

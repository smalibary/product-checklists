BREADCRUMB CHECKLIST: 16 items
A short trail of links showing where a page sits in the site's structure, from the top down to here.
Backing: 0 Rule, 11 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. For sites with three or more levels of pages   (Research: nng-breadcrumbs)
 2. Shows the place in the structure, not the person's history   (Research: nng-breadcrumbs)
 3. Not a replacement for the main menu   (Research: nng-breadcrumbs)

LOOK
 4. Placed above the page title, below the header   (Research: nng-breadcrumbs, govuk-breadcrumbs)
 5. Separators (› or /) drawn by CSS so screen readers do not read them   (Standard: apg-breadcrumb)
 6. Every level a link except the last, which is the current page   (Standard: apg-breadcrumb)
 7. The current page marked (aria-current="page")   (Standard: apg-breadcrumb)
 8. Small but readable text with enough contrast   (Standard: wcag-contrast)

LONG TRAILS
 9. Middle levels collapse into "..." with a menu on small screens   (Practice)
10. On phones, show only the parent as a "Back to X" link   (Standard: govuk-breadcrumbs)
11. Long names cut with an ellipsis and full name on hover and focus   (Standard: wcag-hover)

ACCESS
12. Inside a nav landmark named "Breadcrumb"   (Standard: apg-breadcrumb)
13. An ordered list of links   (Standard: apg-breadcrumb)
14. Separator direction flips for right-to-left languages   (Standard: w3c-dir)
15. Link targets large enough to tap   (Standard: wcag-target)

SEARCH
16. Breadcrumb structured data so search results show the trail   (Standard: google-breadcrumb)

ALERT CHECKLIST: 17 items
A message inside the page that tells people something important about the page or what they just did (also called a callout or inline message).
Backing: 0 Rule, 12 Standard, 5 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

KINDS
 1. Info, success, warning and error, each with its own colour and icon   (Standard: govuk-notification)
 2. Error alerts for page-level problems; field errors go next to fields   (Standard: govuk-error-summary, govuk-error-message)  -> see flows/showing-input-error
 3. Not for urgent questions that need an answer now (use an alert dialog)   (Standard: apg-alertdialog)  -> see design-system/modal
 4. Not for passing messages (use a toast)   (Research: nng-indicators)  -> see design-system/toast

CONTENT
 5. A short title saying what happened   (Standard: govuk-notification)
 6. A sentence on what to do next, with a link or button if there is an action   (Research: nng-errors)
 7. Plain words, no blame, no codes alone   (Research: nng-errors)  -> see design-system/content-voice

LOOK
 8. Kind shown by icon and words as well as colour   (Standard: wcag-color)
 9. Icon has a text name ("Error:") or the title says it   (Standard: wcag-nontext)  -> see design-system/accessibility
10. Text and icon contrast meet WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast, wcag-ui-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
11. Placed near what it is about, usually at the top of the content   (Research: nng-errors)

BEHAVIOUR
12. Stays until the problem is fixed or the person closes it   (Research: nng-indicators)
13. Close button only for alerts that can be ignored; it has a name   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
14. Does not move the page under the person's hand when it appears   (Standard: webdev-cls)

ACCESS
15. Alerts that appear after an action are announced (role alert or status)   (Standard: apg-alert, wcag-status)
16. Do not use role alert for messages that are there on page load   (Standard: apg-alert)
17. After a form fails, focus moves to the error summary   (Standard: govuk-error-summary)

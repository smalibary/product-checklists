ACCORDION CHECKLIST: 18 items
A stack of headings that each open to show their content, saving space on long pages.
Backing: 0 Rule, 13 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Many sections where people need only some of them (FAQ, settings groups)   (Research: nng-accordion)
 2. Not when most people need all the content: show it open instead   (Research: nng-accordion)
 3. Not for very short content or for navigation menus   (Standard: govuk-accordion)

LOOK
 4. Each header is a full-width button with the section title   (Standard: apg-accordion)
 5. An icon (chevron or plus) that changes when open   (Standard: apg-accordion)
 6. Header and its content look connected   (Research: nng-accordion)
 7. "Show all sections" for long accordions   (Standard: govuk-accordion)
 8. Enough space to tap the header   (Standard: wcag-target)

BEHAVIOUR
 9. Several sections can be open at once by default   (Research: nng-accordion)
10. Opening one does not jump the page or move what was being read   (Standard: webdev-cls)
11. Open sections remembered when coming back, if helpful   (Standard: govuk-accordion)
12. Content findable with the browser's Find (hidden="until-found")   (Standard: mdn-until-found)
13. Linking to a section opens it   (Practice)

ACCESS
14. Header button inside a heading of the right level   (Standard: apg-accordion)
15. Button says open or closed (aria-expanded) and controls its panel (aria-controls)   (Standard: apg-accordion)
16. Enter and Space toggle; optional arrow keys between headers   (Standard: apg-accordion)
17. Animation short and respects reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)
18. Native details and summary elements are a good simple choice   (Standard: mdn-details)

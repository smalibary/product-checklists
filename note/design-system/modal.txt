MODAL CHECKLIST: 24 items
A window over the page that blocks it until the person finishes or closes it (a dialog).
Backing: 0 Rule, 14 Standard, 7 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Short tasks or decisions that must happen before going on (confirm delete, quick edit)   (Research: nng-modal)
 2. Not for long forms, complex tasks or content people want to compare with the page   (Research: nng-modal)
 3. Not on page load or as a surprise interruption   (Research: nng-modal)
 4. Urgent confirmations use the alert dialog pattern   (Standard: apg-alertdialog)

CONTENT
 5. A title that says what it is about   (Standard: apg-dialog)
 6. Short body; scroll inside if longer   (Practice)
 7. Buttons named for the action ("Delete project", "Keep editing"), not just OK and Cancel   (Research: nng-confirm)
 8. The safe choice is clear; dangerous action styled as dangerous   (Research: nng-confirm)

LOOK
 9. Dimmed backdrop over the page   (Standard: m3-dialogs)
10. Centred on desktop; full screen or bottom sheet on phones   (Standard: m3-dialogs, hig-sheets)  -> see design-system/drawer
11. Close button with a name in the corner   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
12. Width suits the content, with margins on small screens   (Standard: wcag-reflow)  -> see design-system/accessibility

BEHAVIOUR
13. Escape closes it (except where data would be lost without warning)   (Standard: apg-dialog)
14. Click on the backdrop closes non-critical modals   (Practice)
15. Unsaved changes warned before closing   (Research: nng-control)  -> see flows/saving-changes
16. No modals on top of modals   (Research: nng-modal)
17. Page behind does not scroll   (Practice)

FOCUS AND ACCESS
18. Focus moves into the modal (first field or the title)   (Standard: apg-dialog)
19. Focus stays inside while open   (Standard: apg-dialog)
20. Focus returns to the button that opened it   (Standard: apg-dialog)
21. Role dialog with aria-modal and a name from the title   (Standard: apg-dialog)
22. The native dialog element with showModal handles much of this   (Standard: mdn-dialog)
23. Page behind is hidden from screen readers (inert)   (Standard: mdn-inert)
24. Opening and closing motion respects reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility

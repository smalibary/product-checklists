DARK MODE CHECKLIST: 10 items
A dark colour scheme that follows the device setting or the person's choice.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 1 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

SWITCHING
 1. Follows the system setting by default, with a manual choice   (Standard: mdn-color-scheme, hig-dark-mode)
 2. The choice remembered   (Practice)
 3. No flash of the wrong theme on load   (Practice)

COLOURS
 4. Dark grey surfaces rather than pure black   (Standard: m3-color)
 5. Every colour pair checked for contrast in both themes   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 6. Colours defined as role tokens with a value per theme   (Standard: m3-tokens, dtcg-tokens)  -> see design-system/tokens
 7. Bright colours toned down to avoid glare   (Standard: hig-dark-mode)

CONTENT
 8. Images and logos checked on dark backgrounds   (Practice)
 9. Charts and status colours re-checked   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)  -> see design-system/charts

RESEARCH
10. Some people read better in light mode; keep both   (Research: nng-dark-mode)

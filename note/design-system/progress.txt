PROGRESS INDICATOR CHECKLIST: 8 items
Bars and spinners that show something is happening and how far along it is.
Backing: 0 Rule, 5 Standard, 3 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING
 1. Bar with percent when the length is known; spinner when it is not   (Standard: m3-progress, hig-progress-indicators)  -> see design-system/loading
 2. Nothing for waits under about 1 second   (Research: nng-response)
 3. Skeletons for loading page content   (Research: nng-skeleton)  -> see design-system/skeleton

BEHAVIOUR
 4. Progress only moves forward   (Standard: hig-progress-indicators)
 5. Long waits explain what is happening and allow cancel   (Research: nng-progress)

SEMANTICS
 6. progressbar role with the value, or a status message for screen readers   (Standard: apg-meter, wcag-status)

MOTION
 7. Animation respects reduced motion   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility, motion/motion-system

LOOK
 8. Enough contrast for the bar and its track   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)

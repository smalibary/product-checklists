LOADING CHECKLIST: 18 items
How the product shows that something is happening: spinners, progress bars, busy buttons and skeletons.
Backing: 0 Rule, 7 Standard, 8 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CHOOSING BY WAIT TIME
 1. Under 1 second: show nothing, to avoid a flash   (Research: nng-response)
 2. 1 to 10 seconds: a spinner or a skeleton   (Research: nng-response, nng-skeleton)
 3. Over 10 seconds: a progress bar with percent or steps, time left, and Cancel   (Research: nng-progress)
 4. Very long jobs: run in the background and notify when done   (Research: nng-progress)

KINDS
 5. Determinate progress bar when the amount is known   (Standard: m3-progress)
 6. Indeterminate (spinning or moving) when it is not   (Standard: m3-progress)
 7. Busy button: the button shows a spinner, keeps its width, and cannot be pressed twice   (Practice)
 8. Skeleton for page content (see Skeleton)   (Research: nng-skeleton)
 9. Pull-to-refresh on phone lists   (Standard: hig-loading)

MESSAGES
10. Say what is happening for longer waits ("Uploading 3 of 10 files")   (Research: nng-progress)
11. Never a spinner forever: after a timeout, an error with Retry   (Research: nng-errors)
12. Optimistic updates for fast, safe actions (like, tick) with rollback on failure   (Practice)

LOOK
13. Size and colour of the spinner consistent across the product   (Research: nng-consistency)
14. Spinner and bar meet 3:1 contrast   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)
15. No page-blocking overlays unless the whole page truly cannot be used   (Practice)

ACCESS
16. Progress bar has role progressbar, a name and its value   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
17. Loading region marked busy (aria-busy); start and end announced once   (Standard: wcag-status)
18. Nothing flashes more than 3 times a second   (Standard: wcag-flash)

NAVIGATION BAR CHECKLIST: 10 items
The top bar of a website or app with the logo, main links and account menu.
Backing: 0 Rule, 5 Standard, 4 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

CONTENT
 1. Logo links to home   (Research: nng-indicators)
 2. 5 to 7 main links with clear words   (Research: nng-scanning)
 3. Current section marked visually and with aria-current   (Research: nng-indicators)
 4. Search and account in their usual places (top right in left-to-right layouts)   (Practice)

PHONES
 5. Collapses into a menu or bottom bar on phones   (Research: nng-hamburger)

BEHAVIOUR
 6. Menus open on click; hover menus have a delay and keyboard support   (Standard: apg-menubutton)
 7. A skip link to the main content before it   (Standard: wcag-bypass)

SEMANTICS
 8. Inside a nav landmark with a name   (Standard: apg-landmarks)

LOOK
 9. Stays consistent on every page   (Standard: wcag-consistent)
10. Sticky bars do not cover focused items   (Standard: wcag-obscured)

TOAST CHECKLIST: 18 items
A short message that appears for a few seconds to confirm an action (also called a snackbar), then goes away.
Backing: 0 Rule, 17 Standard, 1 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Low-importance confirmations ("Saved", "Message sent")   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
 2. An undo right after an action ("Deleted. Undo")   (Standard: m3-snackbar)  -> see flows/undoing-an-action
 3. Not for errors that need action or anything the person must read   (Research: nng-indicators)
 4. Not for the result of a form submit that fails (show errors in the form)   (Standard: govuk-error-summary)

CONTENT
 5. One short line; at most one action   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
 6. The action is never the only way to do it (undo is also in a menu)   (Standard: wcag-timing)  -> see flows/undoing-an-action

LOOK
 7. Fixed place (bottom centre or bottom start) across the product   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
 8. Above the tab bar on phones   (Standard: m3-snackbar)  -> see mobile/tab-bar-navigation
 9. Contrast meets WCAG   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
10. One at a time; later ones wait   (Standard: m3-snackbar)

TIMING
11. Stays long enough to read: about 4 to 10 seconds, longer with an action   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
12. Pauses on hover and focus   (Standard: wcag-timing)
13. Can be closed   (Standard: m3-snackbar)
14. With reduced motion, appears without sliding   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)  -> see motion/motion-accessibility

ACCESS
15. Announced by screen readers with a status region (polite)   (Standard: wcag-status)
16. Does not take focus from what the person was doing   (Standard: wcag-status)
17. Action reachable by keyboard (a shortcut to jump to it helps)   (Standard: wcag-keyboard)  -> see design-system/accessibility
18. Does not cover focused content or buttons   (Standard: wcag-obscured)

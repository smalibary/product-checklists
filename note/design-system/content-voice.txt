CONTENT AND VOICE CHECKLIST: 9 items
How the product speaks: words, tone and labels across every screen.
Backing: 0 Rule, 4 Standard, 5 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

VOICE
 1. Voice described in a few dimensions (formal or casual, serious or playful)   (Research: nng-voice)
 2. Tone shifts with the moment: calm for errors, warm for success   (Research: nng-voice)

WORDS
 3. Plain words people use, not internal names   (Standard: hig-writing)
 4. Buttons say what they do (Save changes, not OK)   (Research: nng-buttons)  -> see flows/saving-changes
 5. Sentence case for labels   (Standard: hig-writing)
 6. Numbers, dates and money formatted by locale   (Standard: cldr)

ERRORS
 7. Say what happened and how to fix it, without blame   (Research: nng-errors)

CONSISTENCY
 8. One word for one thing across the product (a glossary)   (Research: nng-consistency)

LANGUAGES
 9. Text written to translate well, with room for longer words   (Standard: w3c-forms-i18n)  -> see design-system/rtl-localisation

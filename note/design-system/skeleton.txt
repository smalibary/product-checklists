SKELETON CHECKLIST: 16 items
Grey placeholder shapes shown where content will appear while it loads.
Backing: 0 Rule, 6 Standard, 9 Research, 1 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. For loads of about 1 to 10 seconds where the page layout is known   (Research: nng-skeleton)
 2. For quick loads under a second, show nothing (avoid a flash)   (Research: nng-response)
 3. For long or unknown waits, add a progress indicator or message   (Research: nng-progress)
 4. Not for buttons or actions, only content   (Research: nng-skeleton)

LOOK
 5. Shapes match the real content's size and layout (text lines, images, avatars)   (Research: nng-skeleton)
 6. Neutral colour with low contrast, in both light and dark themes   (Practice)
 7. Optional slow shimmer; static when reduced motion is on   (Standard: mdn-reduced-motion)
 8. The same skeleton style across the product   (Research: nng-consistency)

BEHAVIOUR
 9. Real content replaces it in the same place, with no layout jump   (Standard: webdev-cls)
10. Parts load on their own (each card replaces its own skeleton)   (Research: nng-skeleton)
11. Turns into an error with Retry if loading fails   (Research: nng-errors)
12. Turns into an empty state if there is no data   (Research: nng-empty)

ACCESS
13. Skeleton shapes hidden from screen readers   (Standard: wai-images)
14. The loading region marked busy (aria-busy), and a short "Loading" message announced   (Standard: wcag-status)
15. Focus not placed on skeleton parts   (Standard: wcag-focus-order)
16. Shimmer does not flash   (Standard: wcag-flash)

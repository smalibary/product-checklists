DRAWER CHECKLIST: 22 items
A panel that slides in from the edge of the screen, for navigation or for details and settings beside the page.
Backing: 0 Rule, 17 Standard, 2 Research, 3 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

WHEN TO USE
 1. Navigation drawer for many destinations on small screens   (Standard: m3-nav-drawer)
 2. Side panel (sheet) for details or filters while keeping the page in view   (Standard: m3-side-sheets)
 3. Not for short confirmations (use a dialog) or for messages (use a toast)   (Research: nng-modal)

TYPES
 4. Modal drawer: covers the page with a dimmed backdrop and blocks it   (Standard: m3-nav-drawer)
 5. Non-modal (standard) drawer: sits beside the page, which stays usable   (Standard: m3-side-sheets)
 6. Bottom drawer on phones for actions (see Action Sheet)   (Standard: m3-bottom-sheets)

LOOK
 7. Width fits the content; on phones up to almost the full width   (Standard: m3-nav-drawer)
 8. Title at the top and a close button with a name   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
 9. Clear edge or shadow between the drawer and the page   (Standard: wcag-ui-contrast)
10. Footer for main actions when the drawer holds a form   (Practice)

BEHAVIOUR
11. Opens from the side that matches reading direction (left in English, right in Arabic)   (Standard: w3c-dir, mdn-logical)
12. Closes with Escape, the close button and a click on the backdrop (modal)   (Standard: apg-dialog)
13. Swipe to close on touch screens, with the button as well   (Standard: wcag-dragging)
14. Unsaved changes warned before closing   (Research: nng-control)
15. Its open state can be in the address for detail drawers   (Practice)
16. Motion short and eased; reduced motion respected   (Standard: m3-motion, mdn-reduced-motion)

FOCUS AND ACCESS
17. Modal drawer: focus moves in, stays inside, and returns to the button that opened it   (Standard: apg-dialog)
18. Modal drawer marked as a dialog with a name (aria-modal)   (Standard: apg-dialog)
19. Non-modal drawer: a landmark or region with a name; focus is not trapped   (Standard: apg-landmarks)
20. The opening button says whether the drawer is open (aria-expanded)   (Standard: wcag-name-role)
21. Page behind cannot be scrolled while a modal drawer is open   (Practice)

TOKENS
22. Width, padding, backdrop colour, radius and motion are tokens   (Standard: dtcg-tokens)

TAG AND CHIP CHECKLIST: 9 items
Small labels for status or category, and small buttons for filters and choices.
Backing: 0 Rule, 9 Standard, 0 Research, 0 Practice
Rule = a store or a law requires it · Standard = a published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found

KINDS
 1. Status tags (read only) kept apart from chips you can press   (Standard: govuk-tag, m3-chips)
 2. Filter chips toggle and show they are on   (Standard: m3-chips)
 3. Input chips (like email addresses) have a remove button   (Standard: m3-chips)

TEXT
 4. Short, one or two words   (Standard: govuk-tag)
 5. Meaning not given by colour alone   (Standard: wcag-color)

INTERACTION
 6. Pressable chips are buttons with a pressed state   (Standard: apg-button)
 7. Remove buttons named ("Remove Riyadh")   (Standard: wcag-label-name)

LOOK
 8. Text contrast meets 4.5:1   (Standard: wcag-contrast)  -> see design-system/accessibility
 9. Tap targets at least 24 by 24 pixels   (Standard: wcag-target)  -> see design-system/accessibility

SIGN-UP CHECKLIST (mobile): all 65 items
Backing: Rule = store or law requires it · Standard = published standard · Research = usability studies · Practice = good practice, no study found


1. BEFORE THE FORM
 1. One line saying what you get, above the form   (Practice)
 2. Try before signing up (guest mode) where the product allows it   (Research)
 3. Ask for an account at the moment it is needed (save, buy, post), not on first open   (Research)
 4. Intro slides skippable, few of them   (Practice)
 5. Sign up and Log in easy to tell apart   (Practice)

2. WAYS TO SIGN UP
 6. Sign up with Apple, Google or a social account   (Practice (checklist.design))
 7. If any third-party sign-in is offered on iPhone, also offer one that limits data to name and email and can hide the email (Sign in with Apple qualifies)   (Rule)
 8. Phone number sign-up with a country-code picker (strong in the Gulf)   (Practice)
 9. Passkey sign-up: email first, say a passkey is part of sign-up before the phone's prompt, keep a fallback   (Research)
10. Same email from a social account links to the existing account, no dead end   (Practice)
11. Sign-up stays in the app, never sent out to the web browser   (Rule)

3. FIELDS
12. Only the fields needed (each extra one lowers completion)   (Practice (checklist.design))
13. Email field brings the email keyboard; phone brings the number pad   (Standard)
14. Password manager can fill and save (marked as new password and username)   (Standard)
15. Auto-capitalise and auto-correct off on email and username   (Practice)
16. Labels stay visible; hint text alone disappears when typing   (Research)
17. Return key moves to the next field and sends on the last   (Practice)
18. Paste allowed everywhere, passwords and codes too   (Standard)
19. Spaces at the start and end trimmed   (Practice)
20. "Did you mean gmail.com?" on a typo   (Practice)
21. Name field accepts any name (Arabic, one word, hyphens)   (Practice)

4. PASSWORD
22. No confirm-password field; a show/hide button instead   (Research)
23. Length over symbols: no "must have a capital and a symbol" rules   (Standard)
24. Long passwords allowed (at least 64 characters)   (Standard)
25. Known leaked or common passwords refused, with a kind message   (Standard)
26. Rules shown up front and ticked as met; strength shown while typing   (Practice (checklist.design))

5. ERRORS AND CHECKING
27. Each field checked when the person leaves it, not while typing   (Research)
28. Error removed as soon as the field is fixed; a tick when it is right   (Research)
29. Error next to the field, saying how to fix it   (Research)
30. Errors never erase what was typed   (Practice)
31. No-connection state keeps what was typed and offers retry   (Practice)
32. Errors read aloud by screen readers   (Standard (WCAG 4.1.3))

6. ACCOUNT ALREADY EXISTS (A REAL TRADE-OFF)
33. Helpful: "This email has an account" with Log in and Reset right there   (Practice)
34. Private: same answer whether the email exists or not, details sent by email   (Standard)

7. LEGAL AND CONSENT
35. Terms and privacy accepted before the account is made, readable in the app   (Practice (checklist.design))
36. Marketing opt-in separate, off by default, as easy to withdraw   (Rule (Saudi), GDPR (EU))
37. Age asked neutrally (type birth month and year, no "I am over 12" box) where children's rules apply   (Rule (US))
38. Tracking asked through the phone's own prompt, before any tracking (iPhone)   (Rule)

8. THE BUTTON AND SENDING
39. Button says what it does: "Create account"   (Practice)
40. Loading state; cannot be pressed twice   (Practice)
41. Button stays above the keyboard   (Practice)
42. A disabled button says why, or it stays enabled and shows the errors   (Practice)
43. Robot check only when needed, invisible first   (Practice)
44. Limits on repeated tries and code sends   (Standard)

9. VERIFICATION
45. Product usable before the email is confirmed   (Practice (checklist.design))
46. Code fills itself from the SMS (iPhone one-time-code; Android SMS Retriever or WebOTP)   (Standard)
47. Code boxes take a pasted code, move on by themselves and send on the last digit   (Practice)
48. Resend with a countdown, plus "change email or number"   (Practice)
49. "Open mail app" button on the check-your-email screen   (Practice)
50. Expired code or link: clear message and one tap for a new one   (Practice)

10. AFTER SIGN-UP
51. Welcome screen pointing to the first action   (Practice (checklist.design))
52. Signed in straight away, never "now log in"   (Practice)
53. Permissions asked when each is needed, a reason screen first, no reward for saying yes   (Rule)
54. Profile setup optional and skippable   (Practice)
55. Already-have-an-account link   (Practice (checklist.design))
56. Delete account inside the app (iPhone and Android) plus a web link (Android)   (Rule)

11. ACCESS AND LANGUAGE
57. Touch targets at least 24 px (WCAG AA); 44 recommended, and Apple's own guide uses 44 points   (Standard)
58. Every field has a name screen readers say   (Standard (WCAG 1.3.1, 4.1.2))
59. Contrast passes in light and dark   (Standard (WCAG 1.4.3))
60. Large text setting does not break the layout   (Standard (WCAG 1.4.4))
61. Right-to-left for Arabic, with phone numbers and codes kept left-to-right   (Practice)
62. Main actions within thumb reach   (Practice)

12. EDGE CASES
63. Leaving halfway keeps what was typed   (Practice)
64. Invite link kept through the whole flow; returns to where they were   (Practice)
65. Works on slow mobile data   (Practice)

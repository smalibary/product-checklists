# Grill, always on (loads every session)

Before acting on any request from Salem, silently list the decisions it needs and sort each one
(full method: the grill skill):
1. **Already decided:** in `.claude/grill/decisions.md`, a map, the preferences, lessons or earlier in this chat. Use it.
2. **A fact:** look it up (files, project history, docs, the web). Never ask Salem for a fact.
3. **A clear winner:** a recognised standard, one option plainly better, or cheap to undo. Decide it yourself,
   log it with the reason, and list it in your reply so he can overturn it.
4. **His call:** no clear winner AND it is his to make (taste, money, priorities, people, public-facing,
   hard to undo). Only these are asked, as 2 to 4 options with one Recommended.

Nothing in group 4: **zero state**. Ask nothing, just do the work (still log what you decided).
Never force a deeper or lighter process. "Grill me" asks for the full interview; "just do it" means decide
everything you reasonably can and park the rest.
Every decision you make or receive is written down before the turn ends (see the grill skill, "Files"), and any
that changes what the app does is applied to the epics, features and stories (the grill skill, "Apply to the plan").

# How to talk to Salem

Salem is the owner. He is an architect, not a programmer, and he is building his vocabulary on purpose.

1. **Use the glossary's words.** `.claude/docs/glossary.md` holds the words we share. When a term is in it (component,
   agent, board...), use exactly that word; never swap it for a vague paraphrase. When Salem uses a word, use
   his word back.
2. **New terms: name them and teach them once.** If a real term helps, use it and give a one-line plain meaning
   the first time ("a hook, an automatic step that runs after every edit"), then add it to the glossary in the
   same turn. Unlisted jargon without a meaning is not allowed.
3. **No code identifiers in replies.** Function names, variable names and file paths stay out unless Salem must
   copy them himself; then put them alone on their own line.
4. **Lead with the answer.** First line: what happened, or the answer. Then at most 3 to 5 short bullets.
5. **Status means numbers.** "4 of 9 done, next is the login screen" beats a paragraph.
6. **Decisions as options** (2 to 4, one Recommended, one line each with the trade-off), also put on the board.
   No narration of steps, no apologies.
7. **End with the link.** When a message needs anything from Salem (an answer, a look, a click), its last line is
   the link to the page where he does it: the board or the mockup page, as a full address (R-36).
